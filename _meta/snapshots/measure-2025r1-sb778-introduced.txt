83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Senate Bill 778
Sponsored by Senator NASH, Representative LEVY B; Representative HARBICK (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act tells OHA to make changes to the  rates for reimbursing air ambulance services.
(Flesch Readability Score: 67.5).
Requires the Oregon Health Authority and coordinated care organizations  to provide re-
imbursement for air ambulance services provided to medical  assistance recipients at rates in ac-
cordance with the national Ambulance Inflation Factor.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to reimbursement rates for air ambulance services.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2025 Act is added to and made a part of ORS chapter 414.
SECTION 2. (1) Every two years, the Oregon Health Authority shall recalculate  and re-
vise the reimbursement rates in the medical assistance program  for air ambulance services
in accordance with the national Ambulance Inflation Factor under  section 1834(l)(3)(B) of the
Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395m).
(2) A coordinated care organization shall provide reimbursement  for air ambulance ser-
vices at the rates established under subsection (1) of this section.
SECTION 3. Section 2  of this 2025 Act applies to contracts between a coordinated care
organization and the Oregon Health Authority entered  into, amended or renewed on or after
the effective date of this 2025 Act.
