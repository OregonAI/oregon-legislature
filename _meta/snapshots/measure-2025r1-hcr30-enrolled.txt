83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Concurrent Resolution 30
Sponsored by Representative HELFRICH, Senator BONHAM
Whereas Friends of Timberline was  founded by Oregonians in 1975 with a mission to preserve
and conserve the iconic Timberline Lodge,  an Oregon treasure and National Historic Landmark; and
Whereas Timberline Lodge, constructed during the Great Depression as part  of the Works
Progress Administration, stands as a testament to American craftsmanship, ingenuity  and resilience;
and
Whereas Friends of Timberline  has been a steadfast partner with the United States Forest Ser-
vice and area operator R.L.K. and Company, sharing an  initiative for collaborative stewardship to
maintain the viability and character of the historic structure and environs,  to meet obligations to
guests and the American people and to reaffirm protocols and responsibilities as stewards; and
Whereas Friends of Timberline has  contributed to public education, archival collection, research
to benefit future generations and outreach  efforts that raise awareness of Timberline Lodge’s role
in Oregon’s history, culture and tourism economy; and
Whereas 2025 marks  the 50th anniversary of Friends of Timberline’s ongoing commitment to a
shared vision and sustainable public-private partnership as Oregon’s first “friends of” nonprofit;
now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly, recognize and honor  Friends of
Timberline for five  decades of exceptional service in preserving and celebrating the historic
Timberline Lodge; and be it further
Resolved,  That  we express our deepest appreciation for the perseverance of Friends of
Timberline in ensuring the  lodge remains a beacon of Oregon’s heritage and a beloved destination
for visitors worldwide; and be it further
Resolved, That a copy of this resolution shall be presented to Friends of Timberline in recogni-
tion of this momentous anniversary and their enduring legacy.
