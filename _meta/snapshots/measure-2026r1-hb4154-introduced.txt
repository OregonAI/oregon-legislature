Sponsored by Representatives  WISE, DOBSON, RIEKE SMITH, Senators FREDERICK, REYNOLDS; Represen-
tatives BOWMAN, FRAGALA, GRAYBER, MCDONALD, MCLAIN, NELSON, NOSSE,  Senators NERON

SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Makes public  data that are collected about students going to school. (Flesch Readability
Score:  64.9).
Requires the compilation of data about students who are regular attenders or who are chron-
ically absent.
Takes effect July 1, 2026.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to student attendance data; creating new provisions; amending ORS 327.133;  and prescrib-
ing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
 ORS 327.133 is amended to read:
327.133. (1)(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of  this subsection, each school district, other
than an education service district, shall file with the Superintendent of Public Instruction:
(A) By July 15 of each  year, an annual report covering the school year ending on the preceding
June 30; and
(B) By January 15,  of each year, a December quarterly report covering the quarter of the cur-
rent school year commencing October 1 and ending December 31.
(b) Pursuant to rules adopted by the  State Board of Education, the Superintendent of Public
Instruction may waive a reporting date  or specify an alternative date for filing a report if a
human-created disaster or a natural disaster affects the ability  of the school district to file a report
by the specified date.
(2) Each such report shall :
(a) Show the average daily membership of resident pupils of the  school district for the period
covered [ and shall also contain such ].
(b) Allow the department to  determine whether a pupil is a regular attender or is
chronically absent, as those terms are defined in section 3 of this 2026 Act.
(c) Be  disaggregated by student group, as defined in ORS 327.164, and align, to the
greatest extent practicable, with student groups for which data is  collected under ORS
327.173 (2)(d). The  student group of students with disabilities must be disaggregated by stu-
dents who have an education plan developed in accordance with section 504 of the  Rehabili-
tation Act of 1973, 29 U.S.C. 794, and students who have an  individualized education program.
(d) Include any other information as the Superintendent of Public Instruction may require.
SECTION 2. Section 3 of this 2026 Act is added to and made a part of ORS chapter 329.
SECTION 3. (1) As used in this section:

(a) “Absent” means a missed school day that  is the result of an excused absence, a
nonexcused absence or a disciplinary exclusion.
(b) “Chronically absent” means that  a student is absent for 10 percent or more of the
school days for which the student is enrolled.
(c) “Regular attender” means that a student  is absent for less than 10 percent of the
school days for which the student is enrolled.
(2) Using student attendance data received from a school district under  ORS 327.133, the
Department of Education shall calculate the following for students who were enrolled  at the
school district or a public charter school for a total of at  least 10 days during the reporting
period:
(a) The total number of students in average daily membership for the school district or
public charter school;
(b) The total number of students in average daily membership  who are regular attenders;
(c) The percentage of students who are regular attenders;
(d) The total number  of students in average daily membership who are chronically ab-
sent; and
(e) The percentage of students who are chronically absent.
(3)(a) Four times per calendar year, the department shall:
(A) Compile the data as provided by subsection (2) of this section;
(B) Provide each school district and public  charter school with the opportunity to review
the compiled data  and request adjustments be made to the data before making the data
available to the public; and
(C) Make  available to the public the compiled data on the department’s website in an
easy-to-use format, including disaggregating the data by student group  as provided by ORS
327.133.
(b) When making the data available to the public, the department shall clearly describe:
(A) The requirements used for determining when a student is a regular attender  or
chronically absent; and
(B) Whether any changes have been made in the methodology for determining when a
student is a regular attender or chronically absent.
SECTION 4.
(1) The reporting requirements and the data compilation requirements of
section 3 of this 2026 Act first apply to the 2026-2027 school year.
(2) The Department of Education shall  include on the department’s website a clear de-
scription of the changes that have been made in the methodology for determining when a
student is a regular attender or chronically absent for the 2026-2027 school  year in compar-
ison to making that determination for the 2025-2026 school year.
SECTION 5.
This 2026 Act takes effect on July 1, 2026.
