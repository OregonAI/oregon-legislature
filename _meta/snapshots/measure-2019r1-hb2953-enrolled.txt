CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to the impacts of Good Neighbor Authority Agreement projects.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The State Forestry Department shall report outcomes from all projects
pursued in this state under  the Good Neighbor Authority Agreement described in section 1,
chapter 96,  Oregon Laws 2018. No later than December 31 of every even-numbered year, the
department shall submit the report, in the manner provided by ORS 192.245, to  an interim
committee of the Legislative Assembly relating to economic development.
SECTION 2. The State Forestry Department shall produce the first  report required by
section 1 of this 2019 Act no later than December 31, 2020.
