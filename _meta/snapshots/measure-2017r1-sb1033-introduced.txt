79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 1033
Sponsored by Senators KNOPP, ROBLAN, Representatives PARRISH,  BUEHLER; Representatives EVANS, HACK,
HAYDEN, NEARMAN, REARDON, SOLLMAN
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Department of Education  to conduct feasibility study to determine viability of estab-
lishing public boarding school.
Appropriates moneys to department from General Fund for purpose of conducting study.
Sunsets December 31, 2018.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2017.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to public boarding school; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Department of Education shall conduct a feasibility study to deter-
mine the viability of establishing a public boarding school in this state.
(2) When conducting the feasibility study, the department shall identify:
(a) The methods for funding a public boarding school;
(b) The characteristics of the students who would be served by a public  boarding school,
including consideration of students who are in foster care, who are homeless or  who come
from families with an income below a specified threshold;
(c) Where in this state a public boarding school would be located; and
(d) The general calendar for  a public boarding school, including considering whether the
public boarding school should be available only during the school year, or all year.
(3) For the purpose of conducting the feasibility study, the department:
(a) Shall collaborate  or enter into a contract with a national nonprofit organization that
develops partnerships with urban communities to provide innovative educational opportu-
nities.
(b) May accept contributions  of funds and assistance from any source, public or private,
and agree to conditions placed on the funds not  inconsistent with the purposes of this sec-
tion.
(4) The department shall submit a report on  the feasibility study conducted under this
section, including recommendations for legislation, to  the committees of the Legislative As-
sembly related to education no later than March 1, 2018.
SECTION 2.
Section 1 of this 2017 Act is repealed on December 31, 2018.
SECTION 3. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Department of Education,  for the biennium beginning July 1, 2017, out of the
General Fund, the amount  of $20,000, which may be expended for the purpose of the feasi-
bility study described in section 1 of this 2017 Act.
