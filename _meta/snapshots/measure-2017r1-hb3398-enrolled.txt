CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to state financial administration; creating new provisions; amending ORS 735.612 and
sections 26, 27 and 42, chapter  698, Oregon Laws 2013; repealing ORS 735.612; and declaring an
emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. The Oregon Medical Insurance Pool Account is abolished. Any  unexpended
balance remaining in the Oregon Medical Insurance Pool Account on the  operative date of
this section shall be refunded to insurers in  a manner determined by the Department of
Consumer and Business Services to be fair and equitable.
SECTION 2.  Section 26, chapter 698, Oregon Laws 2013, is amended to read:
Sec. 26.  The Oregon Medical Insurance Pool [ and the Oregon Medical Insurance Pool Account
are] is  abolished. On the operative  date of this section, the tenure of office of the members of the
Oregon Medical Insurance Pool Board ceases.
SECTION 3.  Section 27, chapter 698, Oregon Laws 2013, is amended to read:
Sec. 27.  Before the operative date of  section 26 , [ of this 2013 Act ] chapter 698, Oregon Laws
2013, the Oregon Medical Insurance Pool Board shall pay  all valid outstanding claims against the
Oregon Medical Insurance  Pool. [ Any balances of amounts remaining in the Oregon Medical Insur-
ance Pool  Account after the payment of claims shall be refunded to insurers in a manner determined
by the board to be fair and equitable. ]
SECTION 4.  Section 42, chapter 698, Oregon Laws 2013, as  amended by section 20, chapter 640,
Oregon Laws 2013, is amended to read:
Sec. 42. [(1) ORS 414.868, 414.872, 735.614, 735.640 and 746.222 and section 1, chapter 803, Oregon
Laws 2009, are repealed January 1, 2014. ]
[(2)] Sections 1, 2, 4 and 4a, chapter 698, Oregon Laws 2013,  and ORS 735.600, 735.605, 735.610,
[735.612,] 735.615, 735.616, 735.620,  735.625, 735.630, 735.635, 735.645 and 735.650 are repealed July
1, 2017.
SECTION 5.  ORS 735.612 is amended to read:
735.612. (1) There is established in the  State Treasury, the Oregon Medical Insurance Pool Ac-
count, which shall consist of:
(a) Moneys appropriated to the account by the Legislative Assembly.
(b) Interest earnings from the investment of moneys in the account.
(c) Assessments and other revenues collected or received by the  Oregon Medical Insurance Pool
Board.

SECTION 6.
ORS 735.612 is repealed on January 2, 2018.
SECTION 7. Section 1 of this 2017 Act becomes operative on November 1, 2017.
SECTION 8. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist, and  this 2017 Act takes effect
on its passage.
