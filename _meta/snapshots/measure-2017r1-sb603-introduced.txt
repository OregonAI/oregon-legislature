79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 603
Sponsored by Senator KNOPP (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Provides that statutes providing for Oregon Retirement Savings Plan  create contract between
State of  Oregon and participants in retirement plan providing that contributions to retirement plan
may not be combined with moneys contained in Public Employees Retirement Fund.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to a  contract preventing contributions to the Oregon Retirement Savings Plan from being
combined with moneys in the Public Employees Retirement Fund; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. Section 2 of this  2017 Act is added to and made a part of ORS 178.200 to
178.245.
SECTION 2. ORS 178.200 to 178.245 create a contract  between the State of Oregon and
participants in the retirement plan established as provided in section 15, chapter 557, Oregon
Laws 2015, providing that contributions  to the retirement plan may not be combined with
moneys contained in the Public Employees Retirement Fund for any purpose.
SECTION 3.
This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2017 Act takes effect
on its passage.
