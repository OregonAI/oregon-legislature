82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
Senate Bill 49
Sponsored by Senator FINDLEY (at the request of Representative Kevin Mannix) (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires Oregon School Activities Association to study availability of competition-size swim-
ming pools at public and private  secondary schools in this state. Directs association to submit
findings and recommendations for legislation to interim committees  of Legislative Assembly related
to education not later than September 15, 2023. Appropriates  moneys from General Fund for pur-
poses of study.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to competition-size swimming pools in secondary schools; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Oregon School Activities Association shall study the availability of
competition-size swimming  pools at public and private secondary schools throughout this
state.  The association shall submit a report in the manner provided by ORS 192.245, and must
include recommendations for legislation to support construction,  maintenance and operation
of competition-size swimming pool facilities  at all accredited secondary schools within this
state, to the interim committees of  the Legislative Assembly related to education no later
than September 15, 2023.
SECTION 2. There is appropriated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services,
for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, out of  the General Fund, the amount of $_______ for
distribution to the Oregon School Activities  Association for the purpose of carrying out the
provisions of section 1 of this 2023 Act.
SECTION 3. Section 1 of this 2023 Act is repealed on January 2, 2024.
SECTION 4. This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2023 Act takes effect
on its passage.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
