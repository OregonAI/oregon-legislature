81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
House Bill 2260
Sponsored by Representative MOORE-GREEN (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Establishes Oregon Cleanup and Beautification pilot program within Oregon Corrections Enter-
prises. Requires Oregon Corrections Enterprises to report on pilot program to interim  committee
of Legislative Assembly no later than September 15, 2022.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund for pilot program.
Sunsets pilot program January 2, 2024.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2021.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to beautification pilot program; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) Oregon Corrections  Enterprises shall establish, administer and supervise
the Oregon Cleanup and Beautification pilot  program as a prison work and on-the-job train-
ing program as described in this section.
(2)(a) The program shall establish and operate a center to process wreckage and waste
materials through recycling, repurposing, reuse,  scrapping and disposal. The center shall
focus on the processing of materials not typically handled by standard waste disposal ser-
vices, including wrecked and derelict motor vehicles, recreational vehicles, boats,  hot tubs,
engines and other machinery.
(b) The center must be initially located at either:
(A) The Oregon State Penitentiary site; or
(B) Property within the Mill Creek Corporate Center owned  or leased by the Department
of Corrections, Oregon Corrections Enterprises or Oregon Department of Administrative
Services.
(4) Waste processed at the center must come from:
(a) Waste from households dropped off at the center; or
(b) Waste removed from public property by programs  administered by local governments,
designated nonprofits or the Oregon Corrections Enterprises.
(5) In administering the program, Oregon Corrections  Enterprises shall coordinate and
consult with the Department of  Corrections, Marion County, the City of Salem and private
industries that are involved in waste hauling, recycling, salvage and  beautification within the
Willamette Valley.
SECTION 2.
No later than  September 15, 2022, Oregon Corrections Enterprises shall
provide a report to an appropriate interim committee of the Legislative Assembly, in the
manner provided in ORS 192.245, on the Oregon Cleanup and  Beautification pilot program
under section 1 of this  2021 Act, including recommendations for the expansion or discontin-
uation of the pilot program.

SECTION 3.
Sections 1 and 2 of this 2021 Act are repealed on January 2, 2024.
SECTION 4. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services,  for the biennium beginning July
1, 2021, out of the General Fund,  the amount of $2,000,000, for distribution to Oregon Cor-
rections Enterprises for deposit into a segregated fund under  ORS 421.442 for the sole pur-
poses of sections 1 and 2 of this 2021 Act.
SECTION 5. This 2021 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2021 Act takes effect
July 1, 2021.
