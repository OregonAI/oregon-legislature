81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
House Bill 2614
Sponsored by Representatives BREESE-IVERSON, SMITH  DB; Representatives BONHAM, BOSHART DAVIS,
NEARMAN, OWENS, STARK, ZIKA, Senator KNOPP (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Directs State Forestry Department to study capacity of Oregon’s nursery industry to provide
seedlings for reforestation following forest fires and report to appropriate committee or interim
committee of Legislative Assembly on or before September 15, 2022.
Sunsets January 2, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to production of nursery seedlings.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The State Forestry Department shall study the  capacity of Oregon’s nursery
industry to provide seedlings for reforestation following forest fires and shall present  the
study in a report  to an appropriate committee or interim committee of the Legislative As-
sembly, in the manner provided under ORS 192.245, on or before September 15, 2022.
SECTION 2. Section 1 of this 2021 Act is repealed on January 2, 2023.
