82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2392
Sponsored by Representative LEWIS (at the request of Oregon District Attorneys Association) (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates  moneys  to Department of Justice for distribution to district attorneys for purposes
of paying expenses relating to review of body camera footage.
Takes effect on 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the financial administration  of the Department of Justice; and prescribing an effective
date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to  the Department of Justice, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, out of the Gen-
eral Fund, the amount of $7,200,000, which  may be expended by distributing $200,000 to each
district attorney to pay  expenses related to review of body camera footage, including estab-
lishing positions for deputy district attorneys and support staff.
SECTION 2. This 2023 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on  which the 2023
regular session of the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
