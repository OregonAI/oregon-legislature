SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates money to Oregon Department of Administrative Services for  deposit in Court Ap-
pointed Special Advocate Fund and distribution to CASA Volunteer Programs.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to CASA Volunteer Programs; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) In addition to and not in lieu of any other  appropriation, there is appro-
priated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services, for the biennium ending June
30, 2021, out of the  General Fund, the amount of $2,616,255, for deposit in the Court Ap-
pointed Special Advocate Fund established in ORS 184.498.
(2) The moneys appropriated under this section  may be expended for distribution to
CASA Volunteer Programs, as defined in ORS  184.489, for the purposes set forth in ORS
184.492 and 419B.112, including but not limited to:
(a) Hiring staff to ensure greater support of and quality assurance  within CASA Volun-
teer Programs; and
(b) Increasing the number and  diversity of CASA Volunteer Programs in an effort to
reduce the average time that each child spends in foster care.
SECTION 2.
This 2020 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2020 Act takes effect
on its passage.
