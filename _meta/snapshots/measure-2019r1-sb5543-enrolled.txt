vices Fund, to the Veterans’ Department  of Veterans’ Affairs Dedicated Lottery Fund, the following
amounts for the following purposes:
(1) [Veterans’ Services Program ........... $ 8,176,492]
Veterans’ Services Program ..... $ 8,378,042
(2) County Veterans’ Service
Officers ................................................$ 6,904,151
(3) National Service
Organizations ..................................... $ 118,156

peace, health and safety, an emergency  is declared to exist, and this 2019 Act takes effect
on its passage.
