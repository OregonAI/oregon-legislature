79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 2402
Sponsored by Representative LIVELY; Representatives BARNHART, NATHANSON, NOSSE (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Center for Health Statistics to establish program allowing  individual who is homeless
to obtain certified copy of individual’s record of live birth, free of charge.
Becomes operative January 1, 2018.
Takes effect on 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to individuals who  are homeless; creating new provisions; amending ORS 432.435; and pre-
scribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Sections 2 and 3 of  this 2017 Act are added to and made a part of ORS
chapter 432.
SECTION 2. The Center for Health Statistics shall  establish a program allowing an indi-
vidual who is homeless to obtain a certified copy of the individual’s record of live birth,  free
of charge. As part of the program, the center shall:
(1) Determine on an annual basis  the maximum number of certified copies of records of
live birth that the authority will issue pursuant to this section;
(2) Prescribe the form and manner of making a request under  this section, including the
method by which the center may ascertain that  an individual making a request pursuant to
this section is homeless; and
(3) Coordinate with the  state, regional and local agencies and organizations listed in ORS
458.528 in providing the outreach services and collecting the data necessary for the  effective
administration of this section.
SECTION 3. (1) There is established in the State Treasury, separate and distinct from the
General Fund, the Free  Birth Certificates for Homeless Persons Fund. Interest earned by the
Free Birth Certificates for Homeless Persons Fund shall be credited to  the fund. All moneys
in the Free Birth  Certificates for Homeless Persons Fund are continuously appropriated to
the Center for Health Statistics for purposes of section 1 of this 2017 Act.
(2) The Free  Birth Certificates for Homeless Persons Fund shall consist of all moneys
credited to the fund, including  moneys appropriated or transferred to the fund by the Leg-
islative Assembly.
