SECTION 3.
Notwithstanding any  other law limiting expenditures, the amount of
$327,713,551 is established for a six-year period beginning July 1, 2023, as the maximum  limit
for payment of expenses  from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Re-
ceipts, but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected  or received by the Higher
Education Coordinating Commission, for expenditures of proceeds from state  bonds issued
for the benefit of a public  university, pursuant to agreements between the commission and
a public university.
SECTION 3a. Notwithstanding any other law  limiting expenditures, the amount of
$37,500,000 is  established for a six-year period beginning July 1, 2023, as the maximum limit
for payment of expenses  from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Re-
ceipts, but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected  or received by the Higher
Education Coordinating Commission, for expenditures of proceeds from state  bonds issued
for the benefit of a community college, pursuant to agreements between the commission  and
a community college.
SECTION 4. The project approvals and expenditure limitations in sections 1, 2, 3 and  3a
of this 2023 Act and the expenditure limitations established by  the Emergency Board during
the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, for capital construction or acquisition  projects, expire
on June 30, 2029, unless otherwise noted.
