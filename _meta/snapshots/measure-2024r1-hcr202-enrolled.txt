House Concurrent Resolution 202
Introduced and printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the request of House In-

Whereas for the  last two decades, the face of service to our veterans in Bend, in Deschutes
County, and all across Oregon has been Lieutenant Colonel Dick Tobiason; and
Whereas Dick Tobiason served 20 years in the United States Army, retiring as a  Lieutenant
Colonel and Army Master Aviator with two tours in Vietnam; and
Whereas Dick Tobiason is a combat-wounded veteran and a recipient of the Purple Heart who
has dedicated his life to honoring Oregon’s veteran community; and
Whereas during his first tour of duty  in Vietnam in 1966 and 1967, Dick Tobiason served as the
commanding officer of a forward air patrol unit that supported the 3rd Marine Division; and
Whereas during his second tour in 1972 and 1973, Dick Tobiason  was an advisor to the South
Vietnamese government; and
Whereas after  retiring from the Army, Dick Tobiason worked at NASA as a manager of its flight
research program in Washington, D.C.; and
Whereas Dick Tobiason  has been involved as an advocate in the federal, state and local legis-
lative process since 2003, and in that time, his  nonprofit organization, the Bend Heroes Foundation,
has helped Oregon adopt laws honoring nearly half a million  Oregon veterans who served during
World War I, World War II,  the Korean War, the Vietnam War, the Persian Gulf War and the
Afghanistan and Iraq War; and
Whereas as chairman and founder, Dick Tobiason led the  Bend Heroes Foundation to open the
Central Oregon Veterans Village, a safe rest village that helps  homeless veterans move into per-
manent housing and stable employment; and
Whereas Dick Tobiason also successfully led efforts in Deschutes  County to designate a bridge
in honor of World War II Medal of Honor recipient Bob Maxwell; and
Whereas Dick Tobiason helped pass legislation in 2015 designating the Purple  Heart Trail in
Oregon; and
Whereas in 2017, Dick Tobiason contributed significantly to the passage of House Bill 2100,
designating a portion of United States Highway 20 as the Oregon Medal of Honor Highway; and
Whereas since the passage of House Bill 2100 (2017), 12 other states, in partnership  with Dick
Tobiason, have adopted similar frameworks and created a memorial highway network that  spans the
nation and honors Medal of Honor recipients from coast to coast; and
Whereas Dick Tobiason has continued his tireless efforts to honor veterans, helping to pass
legislation in 2020 designating the POW/MIA  Memorial Highway in Oregon, in 2022 designating the
Oregon Nisei Veterans World War II Memorial Highway and,  in 2023, creating Oregon’s Gold Star
Families Memorial Highway, honoring the families of service members who fell in  the line of duty
and creating places of remembrance for these surviving family members; and
Whereas Dick Tobiason’s remarkable life has been  led not only in service to Oregon, but also
dedicated to his brothers and sisters in arms  throughout the great history of the United States; and
Whereas Dick Tobiason has held aloft the memory of  those our country has lost to war, and
every day he honors all Oregonians who carry the distinction of calling themselves veterans;  now,
therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
Enrolled House Concurrent Resolution 202 (HCR 202-A) Page 1
That we, the members of the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly, recognize and honor  Lieuten-
ant Colonel Dick Tobiason for his lifetime of service and dedication to Oregon’s veterans,  Medal of
Honor recipients and Gold Star families.
