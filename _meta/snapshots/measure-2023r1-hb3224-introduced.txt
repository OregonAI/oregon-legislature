82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 3224
Sponsored by Representative ANDERSEN, Senator PATTERSON; Representatives EVANS, MANNIX
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires Department of Transportation, State Department of Energy and Department  of Envi-
ronmental Quality to collaborate with City of  Salem and mass transit agencies to study feasibility
of developing rail streetcar system in city of Salem. Directs agencies to submit findings to  interim
committees of Legislative Assembly related to transportation not later than January 1, 2025.
Sunsets January 2, 2026.
Takes effect on 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to a streetcar system in the City of Salem; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Department of Transportation, the State Department of Energy and  the
Department of Environmental  Quality shall collaborate with the City of Salem and mass
transit agencies in the  City of Salem to conduct a study to determine the feasibility of de-
veloping a rail streetcar system in the City of Salem  that connects West Salem, including
the Edgewater District, with Salem Hospital, Willamette University, state offices  and sur-
rounding businesses. The agencies conducting the study  shall submit a report in the manner
provided by ORS 192.245, and  may include recommendations for legislation, to the interim
committees of the Legislative Assembly related to transportation  no later than January 1,
2025.
SECTION 2. Section 1 of this 2023 Act is repealed on January 2, 2026.
SECTION 3. This 2023 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on  which the 2023
regular session of the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
