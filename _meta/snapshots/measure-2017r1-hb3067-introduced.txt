79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 3067
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON EARLY CHILDHOOD AND FAMILY SUPPORTS
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Department of Human Services to conduct  statewide study regarding family supports
no later than July 1, 2018.
Requires department to  submit report regarding study to interim legislative committees no later
than November 1, 2018.
Sunsets January 2, 2019.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to family supports; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) No later than July  1, 2018, the Department of Human Services shall
conduct a statewide study regarding the status of family  supports in this state. The depart-
ment shall collaborate with the Department of Education  in conducting the study under this
subsection.
(2) No later than November  1, 2018, the department shall submit a report to the interim
legislative committees related to child welfare regarding the study conducted in accordance
with subsection (1) of this section.
SECTION 2.
Section 1 of this 2017 Act is repealed on January 2, 2019.
SECTION 3. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2017 Act takes effect
on its passage.
