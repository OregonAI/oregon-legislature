82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
Senate Bill 564
Sponsored by Senator GELSER BLOUIN, Representative HARTMAN; Senator DEMBROW,  Representatives
NELSON, PHAM K (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys to be used for regional health equity coalitions.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to regional health equity coalitions; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Health Authority, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, out of the
General Fund, the amount of $12,000,000, which shall be expended to:
(1) Provide funding  to support five additional regional health equity coalitions for a
statewide total of 15;
(2) Provide funding to regional health  equity coalitions to enable each coalition to hire
two additional full-time staff; and
(3) Allocate $2,200,000 to regional health equity coalitions to be used for:
(a) Facility costs;
(b) Costs related to programs providing  child care, transportation and interpretation and
translation services; and
(c) Stipends paid for regional health equity coalition members attendance at meetings.
SECTION 2.
This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2023 Act takes effect
July 1, 2023.
