80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
Senate Bill 949
Sponsored by Senators DEMBROW, HANSELL, PROZANSKI,  Representative WILLIAMSON; Representative
NOSSE
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys to Higher Education Coordinating Commission for purposes of prison edu-
cation programs.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to state financial administration; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the  Higher Education Coordinating Commission, for the biennium beginning July 1,
2019, out of the General Fund,  the amount of $350,000 for distribution to the University of
Oregon for the purposes of providing and expanding educational  programs in Oregon
correctional facilities.
SECTION 2. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the  Higher Education Coordinating Commission, for the biennium beginning July 1,
2019, out of  the General Fund, the amount of $150,000 for distribution to Blue Mountain
Community College for the purposes of providing and expanding  educational programs in
Oregon correctional facilities.
SECTION 3. This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2019 Act takes effect
July 1, 2019.
