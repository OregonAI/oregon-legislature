80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2020 Regular Session
House Bill 5201
Sponsored by JOINT COMMITTEE ON WAYS AND MEANS
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Modifies limit for six-year period beginning July 1, 2015, on  payment of expenses from fees,
moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts, but excluding lottery funds and federal
funds, collected or received by Oregon Military Department for capital construction.
Limits expenditures  from federal funds collected or received by Oregon Military Department for
Youth  Challenge  Program expansion for six-year period beginning July 1, 2017, in lieu of expenditure
limitation previously established by Emergency Board.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to state financial administration;  creating new provisions; amending section 1, chapter 808,
Oregon Laws 2015; and declaring an emergency.

SECTION 2.
Notwithstanding any other law limiting expenditures, and in lieu of the lim-
itation on expenditures established at the December 2018 Emergency Board meeting  (Item
No. 22), the amount  of $5,825,000 is established for a six-year period beginning July 1, 2017,
as the maximum limit for payment of expenses for  the Youth Challenge Program expansion
from federal funds collected or received by the Oregon Military Department.
SECTION 3. This 2020 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2020 Act takes effect
on its passage.
