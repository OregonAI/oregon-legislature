82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2239
Sponsored by Representative NERON (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires school districts to encourage  community engagement in budget process and to ensure
that training is received on developing and understanding school district budget.
Directs Department of Education  to develop best practices for developing school district budgets
and to make best practices available to school districts.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to district education budgets; amending ORS 328.542.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
 ORS 328.542 is amended to read:
328.542. (1) Subject  to the Local Budget Law (ORS 294.305 to 294.565) and to sections 11 and
11b, Article XI, Oregon Constitution,  each school district board shall prepare annually or biennially
the budget of the school district and shall certify ad valorem property taxes to  the assessor as
provided by law.
(2) In addition to the requirements prescribed by ORS 294.305 to 294.565,  a school district
must:
(a) Include at least one member of the educational equity advisory committee  of the school
district on the budget committee.
(b) Encourage community engagement in the budget process, including:
(A) Providing opportunity for the community of the school district to  give input into the
preparation of the budget and taking into consideration that input  when preparing the
budget. The school district must seek the input of:
(i) School employees;
(ii) Students from student groups for  which an additional amount is computed under ORS
327.013 (1)(c); and
(iii) Parents of students described in sub-subparagraph (ii) of this subparagraph.
(B) Requiring approval of the budget by the  school district board at an open meeting,
following:
(i) Provision of  the proposed budget at the main office of the school district and on the
school district’s website;
(ii) Oral presentation of the proposed budget by an administrator of the  school district
to the school district board; and
(iii) Opportunity for the public to comment on the proposed budget at an open meeting.
(C) Ensuring that  the approved budget identifies the intended uses for the additional
amounts received by the  school district as provided by ORS 327.013 (1)(c). The approved
budget also may identify the intended uses for moneys received as average daily membership
