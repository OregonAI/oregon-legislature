81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
House Bill 2920
Sponsored by Representative NERON (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Allocates $2,000,000 of lottery revenue to Higher Education Coordinating  Commission for
Oregon Promise Fund.
Establishes Oregon Promise Fund. Continuously appropriates moneys in Oregon Promise Fund
to Higher Education Coordinating Commission to issue Oregon Promise grants.
Declares emergency, effective on July 1, 2021.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to funding the Oregon Promise program; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There is allocated for the biennium beginning July 1, 2021, from the  Admin-
istrative Services Economic Development Fund, to the  Higher Education Coordinating Com-
mission, the amount of $2,000,000 for the Oregon Promise Fund.
SECTION 2. (1) The Oregon Promise Fund is established in the State Treasury, separate
and distinct from the General Fund.
(2) Moneys in the Oregon Promise Fund are continuously appropriated to the  Higher
Education Coordinating Commission to issue grants pursuant to ORS 341.522.
(3) Moneys in the Oregon Promise Fund shall consist of:
(a) Moneys appropriated or allocated to the fund by the Legislative Assembly.
(b) Amounts donated to the fund.
(c) Moneys from any other source.
SECTION 3.
This 2021 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2021 Act takes effect
July 1, 2021.
