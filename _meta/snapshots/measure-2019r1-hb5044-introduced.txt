80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 5044
Introduced and printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the  request of Oregon Department of
Administrative Services)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Oregon Climate Authority for biennial expenses.
Limits biennial expenditures from fees, moneys  or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Re-
ceipts, but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received by authority.
Limits biennial expenditures by authority from certain lottery moneys.
Limits biennial expenditures by authority from federal funds.
Authorizes specified nonlimited expenditures.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the financial administration  of the Oregon Climate Authority; and declaring an emer-
gency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There is appropriated to the Oregon Climate  Authority, for the biennium
beginning July  1, 2019, out of the General Fund, the following amounts for the following
purposes:
(1) Carbon Markets and Policy ....... $ 9,128,620
(2) Climate and Energy Policy
and Programs............................ $ 308,214
(3) Debt Service.............................. $ 4,334,048
(4) Administrative Services ............ $ 3,931,301
SECTION 2. Notwithstanding any other  law limiting expenditures, the amount of
$31,898,669 is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, as the maximum limit for
payment of expenses  from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts,
but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or  received by the Oregon Climate
Authority.
SECTION 3.
Notwithstanding any other law limiting  expenditures, the amount of
$3,023,370 is established  for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, as the maximum limit for
payment of expenses from lottery moneys allocated from  the Administrative Services Eco-
nomic Development Fund to the Oregon Climate  Authority for debt service for the energy
efficiency and sustainable technology loan program,  and for the Public Schools Grant Pro-
gram.
SECTION 4. Notwithstanding any other  law limiting expenditures, the amount of
$2,435,545 is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, as the maximum  limit for
payment of expenses from federal  funds collected or received by the Oregon Climate Au-
thority.
