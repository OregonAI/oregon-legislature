81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2022 Regular Session
Senate Bill 1540
Sponsored by Senator GIROD (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys to Department of State Police to  fund Firearms Instant Check System unit.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to Department of State Police funding.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Department of State Police, for the biennium ending June 30, 2023, out of  the
General Fund, the amount of $2,600,000, for  the purpose of funding the Firearms Instant
Check System unit of the department.
