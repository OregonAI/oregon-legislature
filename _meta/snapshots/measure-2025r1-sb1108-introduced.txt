83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Senate Bill 1108
Sponsored by Senator FREDERICK
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act makes new laws that grant  employees time off from work to donate blood.
(Flesch Readability Score: 90.0).
Requires employers to  allow employees to take up to four hours of blood donation leave per
calendar year without the loss of time, pay or benefits.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to paid time off for employees to donate blood.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used in this section:
(a) “Blood donation leave” means time off from  work that is taken to donate blood to any
off-premises blood donation.
(b) “Employer” has the meaning given that term in ORS 653.010.
(c) “Off-premises blood donation” means any voluntary and  noncompensatory blood do-
nation made in connection with a program for the drawing of blood that is:
(A) Approved by the American Association of Blood Banks  or the American Red Cross;
and
(B) Located off of the employer’s premises.
(2) An employer in this state shall permit an employee to use blood donation leave  with-
out loss of time, pay or benefits. Blood donation leave may be taken by an employee for up
to four hours per calendar year. Blood  donation leave is in addition to any other accrued paid
leave to which an employee may be entitled.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
