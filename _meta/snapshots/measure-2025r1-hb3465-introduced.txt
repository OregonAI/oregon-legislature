83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 3465
Sponsored by Representative BOICE
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act tells a state agency to pursue a fire protection contract with a federal  agency.
The contract would apply in two counties. (Flesch Readability Score: 62.7).
Directs the State Forestry Department to  endeavor to enter into a fire protection contract with
the United States Forest Service that pertains to certain counties.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to fire protection.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The State Forestry Department shall endeavor to enter into a fire response
and fire suppression contract with the United States Forest Service that:
(1) Pertains to fire response and fire suppression in Douglas County and Linn County.
(2) Is modeled after any similar, existing contract  between the department and the Bu-
reau of Land Management, to the extent practicable.
