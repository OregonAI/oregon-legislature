83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Senate Bill 637
Sponsored by Senator BONHAM (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: This Act says that fees from  programs to reduce greenhouse gases from motor vehicle
fuel must go to the State Highway Fund. (Flesch Readability Score: 69.1).
Requires state agencies that administer programs to reduce  greenhouse gas emissions from the
combustion of motor vehicle fuel to deposit fees, assessments or other charges imposed  pursuant to
the program in the State Highway Fund.
Directs the Governor to include in the Governor’s budget an estimate of the amounts to  be
transferred in the coming biennium and recommendations for alternative revenue sources.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to  the transfer to the State Highway Fund of charges imposed to reduce greenhouse gas
emissions from the use of motor vehicle fuel; creating new provisions;  and amending ORS
291.216.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used in this section:
(a) “Greenhouse gas” has the meaning given that term in ORS 468A.210.
(b) “Motor vehicle” has the meaning given that term in ORS 801.360.
(c) “Motor vehicle fuel” means gasoline, diesel or any  other liquid product used to power
a motor vehicle upon the highways of this state.
(2) Notwithstanding any other provision of law, any fee, assessment  or other charge im-
posed by state statute or administrative rule pursuant to a  program to reduce the amount
of greenhouse gas emissions produced by the combustion of  motor vehicle fuel shall be ded-
icated for highway purposes and transferred by the  agency that collected the fee, assessment
or other charge to the State Highway Fund established under ORS 366.505.
(3) An agency required to  transfer amounts under subsection (2) of this section shall
make the required transfers each calendar quarter.
(4) The Governor shall  include in the Governor’s budget an estimate of the amount re-
quired to be transferred under this section  in the coming biennium and make recommen-
dations for  whether the amount transferred by each agency should be wholly or partially
replaced with revenue from any other source.
