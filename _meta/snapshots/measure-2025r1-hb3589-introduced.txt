83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 3589
Sponsored by Representative MARSH, Senator PATTERSON; Representative GOMBERG
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: This Act requires OHCS to focus on  senior housing and sweeps moneys from tax defer-
rals for such housing. (Flesch Readability Score: 61.6).
Requires the Housing and Community Services Department to develop a senior housing  devel-
opment initiative. Transfers moneys from  the Senior Property Tax Deferral Revolving Account to
be used for the initiative.
Requires the Department  of Revenue to annually review the account, beginning in 2027, for ex-
cess funds to transfer to the Elderly and Disabled Housing Fund for the initiative. Sunsets  July 1,
2031.
Provides that projects funded by the  Elderly and Disabled Housing Fund remain eligible for af-
fordable housing tax credits.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to housing; creating new provisions; and amending ORS 317.097.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2025 Act is added to and made a  part of ORS 456.515 to
456.725.
SECTION 2. (1) The Housing and Community Services Department shall establish and
maintain a senior housing development initiative to incentivize housing targeted to  older
adults and persons with disabilities, including housing that  prioritizes visitability and acces-
sibility, but not including housing providing care. Under the initiative, the department shall:
(a) Focus on combining state and federal resources available to fund  the department for
the development, rehabilitation and preservation of housing for older adults and persons with
disabilities and leveraging existing moneys to increase development, redevelopment  and
preservation opportunities.
(b) Coordinate with the Oregon Health Authority in operating the authority’s  Healthy
Homes Program under ORS 431A.400 to provide modest  accessibility modifications to homes
housing older adults or persons with disabilities.
(c) Provide technical assistance to developers,  affordable housing and service providers,
planners, stakeholders and local governments to:
(A) Support housing location, design, development and the  delivery of home and
community-based services for older adults; and
(B) Help ensure housing developments meet  the needs of older adults to age well in their
homes and communities through housing that enables choice, safety and physical accessibil-
ity.
(d) Provide regular public updates and track goals and progress relating  to affordable
rental housing targeted toward  populations of older adults and persons with disabilities. Up-
dates that must be given under this paragraph may be  provided through or in coordination

SECTION 3.
Notwithstanding ORS 311.701, the amount of  $27,000,000 is transferred from
the Senior Property Tax Deferral Revolving Account  to the General Fund for general gov-
ernmental purposes. The transfer shall be made on the effective date of this 2025 Act.
SECTION 4. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Housing and  Community Services Department, for the biennium beginning July
1, 2025, out of the General Fund:
(1) $23,500,000 to fund the development, rehabilitation or preservation of housing for older
adults and persons with disabilities under the senior housing development initiative in sec-
tion 2 (1)(a) of  this 2025 Act. This appropriation is available continuously until expended for
the purpose specified in this subsection.
(2) $3,000,000 to fund accessibility modifications under the senior housing development
initiative in section 2 (1)(b) of this 2025 Act.
(3) $500,000 to provide technical  assistance under the senior housing development initi-
ative in section 2 (1)(c) of this 2025 Act.
SECTION 5.
(1) The Department of Revenue shall, before December 31  of each year, de-
termine the amount estimated to be required to be maintained in the Senior Property  Tax
Deferral Revolving Account in ORS 311.701 to fund the obligations  for the following calendar
year, including reserves, with the assumption  that the obligations will equal the greatest
amount in the previous 10 years as adjusted for inflation and population growth.
(2) On or before July 1 of the estimated year, the department  shall transfer the positive
difference between the amount in the account and the estimated obligations under subsection
(1) of this section, if any,  to the Housing and Community Services Department for deposit
into the Elderly and Disabled Housing Fund under ORS 456.535 for use by the  department to
implement the senior housing development initiative under section 2 of this 2025 Act.
SECTION 6.
Section 5 of this 2025 Act becomes operative on January 1, 2027.
SECTION 7. Section 5 of this 2025 Act is repealed on July 1, 2031.
