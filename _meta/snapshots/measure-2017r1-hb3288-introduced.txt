79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 3288
Sponsored by Representative WHISNANT
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires each public institution of higher education to submit annual  report containing infor-
mation about noninstructional staff  employed by institution to Higher Education Coordinating
Commission. Requires commission to compile reports and submit  compiled report to interim com-
mittees of Legislative Assembly related to education.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to noninstructional professional positions at public institutions of higher education.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) Not later  than October 1 of each year, each public institution of higher
education as defined in ORS 350.350 shall submit a  report to the Higher Education Coordi-
nating Commission containing the following information:
(a) The ratio of noninstructional professional staff to faculty employed  by the institution.
(b) The number of new  noninstructional professional positions created by the institution.
(c) The contracted  wage of each new and existing noninstructional professional position
at the institution.
(d) The reason for the creation of  each new noninstructional professional position by the
institution.
(2) The report required under subsection  (1) of this section must include the required
information for the academic year in which the report is  submitted as well as for each pre-
vious academic year beginning with the 2012-2013 academic year.
(3) The Higher Education Coordinating Commission shall compile a report from the  re-
ports received by the commission pursuant to subsection (1) of this section  and, not later
than December 1 of each year, submit the compiled report to the interim committees of  the
Legislative Assembly related to higher education in the manner required under ORS  192.245.
SECTION 2. (1) A public institution of education must submit the  first report required
under section 1 (1) of this 2017 Act no later than October 1, 2018.
(2) The Higher Education Coordinating Commission must submit the first  compiled re-
port required under section 1 (3) of this 2017 Act no later than December 1, 2018.
