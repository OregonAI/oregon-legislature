79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 2223
Sponsored by Representative WHISNANT, Senator MONNES ANDERSON, Representative KENY-GUYER;  Repre-
sentatives DOHERTY, HACK, MCLAIN, SMITH WARNER, Senator TAYLOR (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Department of Education to establish program to develop and maintain statewide  school
nursing services.  Allows State Board of Education to adopt rules for program. Directs Oregon
Health Authority to ensure care provided  to students by school nurses is reimbursed by state plan
for medical assistance.
Appropriates moneys to Department of Education for purposes of program.
Becomes operative on January 1, 2018.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to school nurses; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used  in this section and section 4 of this 2017 Act, “school nurse” has
the meaning given that term in ORS 342.455.
(2) The Department of  Education, in collaboration with the State School Nursing Con-
sultant, shall establish a program to develop and maintain statewide school  nursing services
to:
(a) Meet the student to school nurse ratios set forth  in section 5, chapter 843, Oregon
Laws 2009;
(b) Assist school districts in funding school nurse positions; and
(c) Create a school nurse sustainability plan with each school district.
SECTION 2.
The Department of Education, in collaboration with the  State School Nurs-
ing Consultant described in ORS 413.084, shall  develop statewide nursing standards of prac-
tice that include outcome measures for  school health transformation and academic
performance, and shall create an integrated  school health services delivery model to foster
and promote a noncompetitive and collaborative strategy.
SECTION 3. The State Board of Education may adopt rules to  carry out the provisions
of sections 1 and 2 of this 2017 Act.
SECTION 4. (1) The Oregon Health Authority shall amend the state plan  for medical as-
sistance or take any other  steps necessary to secure federal financial participation in the
cost of  care provided by school nurses to students enrolled in the state medical assistance
program.
(2) The authority shall establish  a process for a school district to submit a claim for re-
imbursement for care services provided by school nurses to  students enrolled in the medical
assistance program.
