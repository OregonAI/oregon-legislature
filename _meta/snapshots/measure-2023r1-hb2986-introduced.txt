82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2986
Sponsored by Representatives MARSH, OWENS, Senators DEMBROW, GOLDEN; Senator FREDERICK (Presession
filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs State Forestry Department to  establish by rule Prescribed Fire Capacity Grant Program.
Establishes Prescribed Fire  Capacity Fund in State Treasury, separate and distinct from General
Fund, to fund program.
Appropriates  moneys  to department out of General Fund for deposit into Prescribed Fire Ca-
pacity Fund.
Sunsets January 2, 2029.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to prescribed fires; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The  State Forestry Department shall establish by rule a Prescribed Fire
Capacity Grant Program to:
(a) Increase the pace and scale of the use of prescribed fire and cultural burning.
(b) Reduce barriers for conducting prescribed fires and cultural burning.
(2) Under the program the department may award grants that:
(a) Support workforce development.
(b) Add capacity to conduct prescribed fires.
(c) Assist local governments and  nongovernmental organizations with funding equipment
and personnel that are needed for conducting prescribed fires.
(3) To implement the program, the department:
(a) May publish a notice that describes eligibility for the grants.
(b) Shall  collaborate with other relevant state agencies, cultural fire practitioners, the
Oregon Prescribed Fire Council and Certified Burn Managers,  as defined in ORS 526.005, to
establish guidelines governing the program and requirements for grantee  reporting and im-
plementation of grant-funded projects.
SECTION 2.
The Prescribed  Fire Capacity Fund is established in the State Treasury,
separate and distinct from the General Fund. Interest earned by the Prescribed Fire Capacity
Fund shall be credited to the fund. Moneys in the fund are continuously appropriated  to the
State Forestry Department for the purposes described in section 1 of this 2023 Act.
SECTION 3.
(1) Sections 1 and 2 of this 2023 Act are repealed on January 2, 2029.
(2) Any moneys remaining in the Prescribed Fire Capacity Fund that are unexpended  and
unobligated on the date specified in subsection (1) of this section shall revert  to the General
Fund.
