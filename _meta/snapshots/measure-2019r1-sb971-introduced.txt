80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
Senate Bill 971
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Commission on Indian Services to study tribes. Requires report to interim  committee
of Legislative Assembly related to judiciary.
Sunsets January 2, 2020.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to tribes; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Commission on Indian Services shall study tribes in this state. The
commission shall submit findings and  recommendations for legislation to an interim com-
mittee of the Legislative Assembly related to the  judiciary not later than December 31, 2019.
SECTION 2. Section 1 of this 2019 Act is repealed on January 2, 2020.
SECTION 3. This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2019 Act takes effect
on its passage.
