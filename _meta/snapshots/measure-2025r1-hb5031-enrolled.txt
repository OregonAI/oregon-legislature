AN ACT
Relating to the financial  administration of the Oregon Public Defense Commission; and declaring an
emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. There are appropriated to the Oregon Public  Defense Commission, for the
biennium beginning July 1, 2025, out of the General Fund, the following amounts,  for the
following purposes:
(1) Executive Division ....................  $ 3,356,449

Commission, for the following purposes:
(1) Juvenile Trial Division.............. $ 7,393,486

(3) Court Mandated Expenses ........ $ 6,547,938

peace, health and safety, an emergency  is declared to exist, and this 2025 Act takes effect
July 1, 2025.
