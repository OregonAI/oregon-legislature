80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
Senate Bill 700
Sponsored by Senator KNOPP
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Authorizes certain providers of water from source within Deschutes River Basin  to collect fee
from water recipients on behalf of nonprofit  corporation. Requires provider of water to forward
collected fee moneys to nonprofit corporation.
Authorizes nonprofit corporation to use collected fee  moneys to purchase mitigation credits, to
purchase temporary transfers of water or  to engage in mitigation projects to increase water flow
or water quality  within portions of Deschutes River or tributaries. Requires nonprofit corporation
to distribute acquired water to providers of water collecting fee for forwarding to  nonprofit corpo-
ration.
Requires Water Resources Department to adopt rules establishing guidelines  for water-related
activities of nonprofit corporation and provide ongoing monitoring of nonprofit corporation activ-
ities.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to Deschutes River Basin water.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used in this section,  “provider of water” means a city, a quasi-
municipal corporation, a domestic water supply district formed under ORS chapter 264, a
water supplier as defined in ORS 448.115 or a water authority  formed under ORS chapter 450.
(2) A provider of water appropriated from a source within the Deschutes River  Basin
may collect a  fee of three and one-half cents per 100 cubic feet from recipients of the water
on behalf of a nonprofit corporation described in section 2 of this 2019 Act.  A provider of
water shall forward all moneys collected by the provider of water under this  section, without
reduction, to the nonprofit corporation.
SECTION 2.
(1) A nonprofit corporation that is organized  under ORS chapter 65 and ex-
empt from taxation under  section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code may accept moneys
collected and forwarded under section 1 of this 2019 Act  and accept gifts, grants or donations
of moneys from any other public or  private source for purposes consistent with this section.
A public body, as defined in ORS 174.109, may not be a member of or participate in the
management or operation of a nonprofit corporation described in this section, other than  as
a distributee under subsection (3) of this  section. The nonprofit corporation shall use moneys
received by the nonprofit corporation:
(a) To purchase mitigation credits or  to purchase temporary transfers of water under
ORS 540.585; or
(b) If the purchase of mitigation credits or temporary transfers  is not practicable due to
lack of supply or excessive cost,  to engage in projects designed to increase the flow or water
quality of the upper or middle portions of  the Deschutes River or tributaries to those
portions of the Deschutes River.
