83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 2365
Sponsored by Representative LEVY B; Representatives OSBORNE, RESCHKE, WRIGHT, Senators NASH,  WEBER
(Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act  lets more health care providers get the rural provider income tax credit. (Flesch
Readability Score: 65.7).
Adds mental  health professionals, naturopathic physicians, pharmacists, medical laboratory sci-
entists and medical laboratory technicians to the  types of providers eligible for the tax credit al-
lowed to rural medical care providers. Removes the requirement of hospital  consulting privileges
applicable to an optometrist claiming the credit.  Applies to tax years beginning on or after January
1, 2026.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to rural medical provider tax credits; creating new provisions;  amending ORS 315.616 and
315.619; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:

SECTION 2.
The amendments to ORS 315.616 by section 1 of this 2025 Act apply  to tax
years beginning on or after January 1, 2026.
SECTION 3. (1) The Office of Rural Health shall establish criteria for certifying the fol-
lowing individuals as eligible for the tax credit authorized by ORS 315.616:
(a) Naturopathic physicians licensed under ORS chapter 685;
(b) Pharmacists licensed under ORS chapter 689;
(c) Psychologists licensed under ORS 675.010 to 675.150;
(d) Clinical social workers licensed under ORS 675.530;
(e) Licensed professional counselors licensed under ORS 675.715;
(f) Marriage and family therapists licensed under ORS 675.715; and
(g) Medical laboratory scientists and medical laboratory technicians  certified by the
American Society for Clinical Pathology, or its successor organization.
(2) The office shall certify an individual described in subsection (1) of  this section as eli-
gible for the tax credit authorized by  ORS 315.616 if, after receiving the individual’s applica-
tion for the credit, the office finds that the individual:
(a) Is or  will be providing, to one or more rural communities, the services the individual’s
license or certification authorizes the individual to provide; and
(b) Otherwise meets the eligibility criteria established by the office.
