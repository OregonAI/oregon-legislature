79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 3211
Sponsored by Representatives SPRENGER, CLEM
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Oregon Business Development Department to study barriers to development  of indus-
trially zoned lands  and report to appropriate committee or interim committee of Legislative Assem-
bly no later than September 1, 2018.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to industrially zoned lands.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Oregon Business Development Department shall study barriers  to the
development of industrially zoned lands and shall report to an appropriate  committee or in-
terim committee of the Legislative Assembly no later than September 1, 2018.
