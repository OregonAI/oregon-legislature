81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
House Bill 3090
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON WATER (at the request of Representative Ken Helm)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys to Department of Environmental Quality to award grant for  on-site septic
system low-interest loan programs and to cover related administrative costs.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to on-site septic system financing; and declaring an emergency.
Whereas in 2016  the Legislative Assembly enacted ORS 454.779, which directs the Department
of Environmental Quality  to award grants for developing and administering loan programs to pro-
vide low-interest loans for the repair, replacement, upgrade or evaluation of  residential or small
business on-site septic systems; and
Whereas since 2016,  the Clean Water Loan program funded by a grant from the department
under ORS 454.779 has served over 186 families, 104  communities and 35 counties, financing projects
that are now treating an estimated total of 24 million gallons of wastewater annually; and
Whereas most  commercial banks have stopped or greatly reduced issuing second mortgage loans;
and
Whereas nearly one out of every three families served by the Clean  Water Loan program has
a family income that falls below 80 percent of the county’s area median income, and  most borrowers
use the program because they struggle to obtain a conventional loan; and
Whereas demand remains high for low-interest loans for the repair, replacement, upgrade  or
evaluation of residential or small business on-site septic systems; and
Whereas the COVID-19 pandemic has increased the need for septic repairs; and
Whereas the program helps people and families in emergency  situations, thereby promoting
community resilience, housing stabilization and water quality generally, including during  extreme
events of fire, flood, pandemic or other natural or human-made disasters; and
Whereas in July 2020 the program was temporarily suspended for lack of available loan funds;
and
Whereas the department estimates that  over 45,000 septic systems fail each year; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Department of Environmental Quality, for the biennium beginning  July 1, 2021,
out of the General Fund, the amount of $2,000,000 for the purposes of:
(1) Not later  than September 1, 2021, awarding a grant under ORS 454.779 in an amount
of not less than $1,950,000; and
(2) Covering administrative costs necessary to implement ORS 454.779.

This 2021 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
