81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
House Bill 2608
Sponsored by Representative GOMBERG (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Department of Education to establish and administer pilot program to assist school dis-
tricts in providing age-appropriate instruction on natural disasters.
Sunsets pilot program on January 2, 2027.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2021.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to natural disaster education; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Department of Education shall  establish and administer a pilot pro-
gram to assist school districts in providing age-appropriate instruction on natural disasters.
(2) Instruction provided under the pilot program established by this section must:
(a) Enable students to prepare  for a natural disaster and for survival in the days and
weeks after a natural disaster; and
(b) Be specific to the natural disasters that may occur in the areas served by  the schools
participating in the pilot program, which may  include earthquakes, tsunamis, water short-
ages, floods and heavy snow.
(3) Any school district in this state may apply to  participate in the pilot program estab-
lished by this section. The department shall select no more than five  school districts to
participate in the pilot program. When selecting school  districts to participate in the pilot
program, the department shall give priority to school districts that  represent geographic and
demographic diversity.
(4) For school districts participating in the pilot program established  by this section, the
department shall:
(a) Develop curriculum that may be used by the school districts; and
(b) Provide any necessary technical assistance to the school districts.
(5) The department  shall submit a report to the interim committees of the Legislative
Assembly related to education no later than:
(a) September 15, 2024, for a preliminary report; and
(b) September 15, 2026, for a final report.
SECTION 2.
Section 1 of this 2021 Act is repealed on January 2, 2027.
SECTION 3. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Department of Education,  for the biennium beginning July 1, 2021, out of the
General Fund, the amount of $_______, which may  be expended for developing curriculum and
providing technical assistance as described in section 1 of this 2021 Act.
