80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 3327
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND ENVIRONMENT (at the request of Representative Paul Holvey)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Creates Oregon Independent Science Review Board.
Establishes Independent Scientific Review Secretariat as administrative section within Institute
for Natural Resources at Oregon State University.
Establishes Independent Scientific Review  Fund. Continuously appropriates moneys in fund to
Higher Education Coordinating Commission for distribution to Oregon State University for  purposes
of board and secretariat.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to independent scientific review.
Whereas Oregon’s natural resource agencies, legislators and the public would benefit from in-
dependent science reviews; and
Whereas for  an independent science review program to be beneficial, independent science re-
views need to be focused appropriately and carried out in a deliberate, transparent manner; and
Whereas most single-agency science review needs can be met  with existing state, federal and
academic  resources,  but review practices and capacity for conducting science reviews vary consid-
erably among state agencies; and
Whereas there is a need  for independent science review of natural resource issues that are
complex and multidisciplinary,  that require the involvement of multiple agencies and that are rele-
vant to stakeholders and lawmakers; and
Whereas existing state resources are not adequate to address  the needs presented by such
complex, multidisciplinary natural resource issues; and
Whereas independent science review mechanisms  and structures that are used for natural re-
source policymaking in other states and at the federal level can inform  a process for independent
science reviews in Oregon; and
Whereas there is also a need for Oregon to develop a tailored approach to independent  science
reviews, drawing upon the lessons learned from other state and  federal independent science review
processes in order to reduce the potential risks of independent science reviews; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Oregon Independent Science Review Board is  created. The board
consists of seven members appointed by the Governor.
(2) Persons appointed members of the board must possess the following attributes:
(a) Scientific or technical expertise, which may  include legal or policy expertise, relevant
to a broad range of natural resource disciplines;
(b) A willingness to engage with stakeholders  in natural resource issues and maintain
transparency with regard to the work of the board;
(c) An ability to communicate complex technical issues in a straightforward manner;

(e) Objectivity and familiarity with independent scientific review processes;
(f) An ability to chair an independent science review process that involves effectively  and
efficiently receiving input from a large number of experts;
(g) Familiarity with the state of  Oregon’s natural resources and the communities and

(h) An  ability to identify and engage the needed expertise for each issue being addressed

(3) The term of office of each  member of the board is four years, but a member serves
at the pleasure of the Governor. Before the expiration of the term of a  member, the Gover-
nor shall appoint  a successor whose term begins on January 1 next following. A member is
eligible for reappointment, but may serve as a member for no more than two consecutive
terms.  If  there is a vacancy for any cause, the Governor shall make an appointment to be-
come immediately effective for the unexpired term.
(4) At the first meeting of each year, the board shall elect one of  its members to serve
as chairperson. The chairperson shall have a term of  one year, with duties and powers that
the board determines are necessary for the performance of the functions that  the board as-
signs to the chairperson.
(5) A majority of the members of the board  constitutes a quorum for the transaction of
business.
(6) Official action by the board requires the approval of a majority of the members of  the
board.
(7) The board shall meet at times and places  specified by the call of the chairperson or
of a majority of the members of the board.
(8) The board may adopt rules necessary for operation of the board.
(9) A member of the board is entitled to  compensation and expenses as provided in ORS
292.495.
SECTION 2.
The Oregon Independent Science Review Board shall:
(1) Receive, evaluate and direct the Independent Scientific Review Secretariat to convene
independent scientific review panels to address requests by state agencies, local governments
and private persons for independent scientific review of complex,  multidisciplinary natural
resource issues that require the involvement of multiple agencies.
(2) Assist the secretariat in identifying, recruiting and selecting persons to serve on in-
dependent scientific review panels.
(3) Assist in framing the questions to be addressed by independent scientific review pan-
els.
(4) Provide  for leadership and management of independent scientific review panels by
developing a process by which individual members of the board shall be assigned to serve as
chairperson for individual independent scientific review panels.
(5) Provide other advice and direction  as necessary to support the activities of the
secretariat.
(6) Approve the final membership of each independent scientific review panel as desig-
nated by the secretariat.
(7) Establish policy and procedures for the secretariat and programs and  activities ad-

SECTION 3. (1) The Independent Scientific Review Secretariat is established  as an ad-
ministrative section  within the Institute for Natural Resources established at Oregon State
University under ORS 352.808, subject to the supervision of the Administrator  of the Inde-
pendent Scientific Review Secretariat and the policies  and procedures established by, and
recommendations of, the Oregon Independent Science Review Board.
(2) The secretariat consists  of the administrator and all personnel employed in the
secretariat.
(3) Subject to subsection (1) of this  section, the secretariat shall develop and administer
programs as set forth in this section and section 4 of  this 2019 Act and administer funds
available in the Independent Scientific Review Fund created in section 6 of this 2019 Act.
(4) The secretariat shall provide the  board with staff and other assistance as necessary
for the board to perform its duties.
SECTION 4. (1) The Independent Scientific Review Secretariat shall:
(a) Assist the Oregon Independent Science Review Board in identifying  the type of inde-
pendent scientific review necessary to address a particular issue presented to the board.
(b) Serve as the primary  point of contact for members of the public for matters related
to the activities of the board and the secretariat.
(c) Supervise and assist in establishing  the scope of work and other administrative mat-
ters applicable to independent scientific review panels.
(d) Maintain a list of experts available to participate as members of independent scientific
review panels.
(2) The secretariat may:
(a) Conduct hearings and conferences to develop facts, to explain programs and  activities
and to obtain advice.
(b) Enter into agreements with other public bodies, as defined in  ORS 174.109, or associ-
ations or private persons for services to assist the secretariat and the board.
(c) Enter  into agreements with other public bodies, as defined in ORS 174.109, the federal
government, associations or private persons for  cooperative endeavors that further pro-
grams administered by the secretariat.
(d) Obtain assistance or data from any state agency.
(e) Perform any other act necessary to carry out the duties of the secretariat.
SECTION 5.
(1) The Governor, upon consultation with  the Oregon Independent Science
Review Board, shall appoint an Administrator of the Independent Scientific Review
Secretariat who shall serve at the pleasure of the Governor.
(2) The Governor shall set the compensation of the administrator.
(3) The administrator shall be the administrative head of the secretariat.
(4) The administrator shall hire the staff necessary to allow the secretariat to carry out
its duties. In accordance with the State Personnel  Relations Law, the administrator and any
manager hired by the administrator shall be in the unclassified service.
SECTION 6.
(1) The Independent  Scientific Review Fund is established in the State
Treasury, separate and distinct from the General  Fund. Moneys in the Independent Scientific
Review Fund are continuously appropriated to the Higher Education Coordinating Commis-
sion to  be distributed to Oregon State University for the purposes of sections 1 to 5 of this
2019 Act.
