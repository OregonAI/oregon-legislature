83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 2040
Sponsored by Representative NOSSE (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Tells DCBS and  OHA to create a complaint process for health care providers. (Flesch
Readability Score: 69.9).
Establishes a complaint process for health care providers to submit complaints to the  Depart-
ment of Consumer and Business Services or the Oregon Health Authority regarding  health insurers
or coordinated care organizations.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to a health care provider complaint process; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2025 Act is  added to and made a part of the Insurance Code.
SECTION 2. (1) For purposes of this section, “health insurer” means an insurer  offering
a health benefit plan, a health  maintenance organization or a health care services contrac-
tor.
(2) The Department of Consumer  and Business Services shall establish a process for a
health care provider, as defined in ORS 192.556, to submit a complaint alleging that a  health
insurer has violated a provision  of the Insurance Code or rule established by the department
as authorized by the Insurance Code.
(3) The department may establish or  increase any licensing or application fee imposed
on health insurers  to carry out the provisions of this section. The department shall deposit
all moneys collected under this section into the Consumer and Business Services Fund cre-
ated in ORS 705.145. Any fee adopted  by the department under this section must be based
on the department’s reasonable cost of administering this section.
(4) The department shall adopt rules to implement the provisions of this section.
SECTION 3.
(1) The Oregon Health Authority shall establish  a process for a health care
provider, as defined in ORS 192.556, to submit a complaint alleging that a coordinated care
organization, as defined in ORS 414.025, has violated a provision of ORS chapter 414 or rule
established by the authority as authorized by ORS chapter 414.
(2) The authority may impose a fee on coordinated care organizations to carry  out the
provisions of this section. Any fees collected  shall be paid to the Oregon Health Authority
Fund established in ORS 413.101. Any fee adopted  by the authority under this section must
be based on the authority’s reasonable costs of administering this section.
(3) The authority shall adopt rules to implement the provisions of this section.
SECTION 4.
Sections 2 and 3 of this 2025 Act become operative on July 1, 2026.
