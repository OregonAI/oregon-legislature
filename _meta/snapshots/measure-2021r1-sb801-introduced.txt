81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
Senate Bill 801
Sponsored by Senator GORSEK
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Department of Consumer and  Business Services to study issues relating to workers’
compensation claims processing and report to appropriate committee or  interim committee of Leg-
islative Assembly on or before September 15, 2022.
Sunsets January 2, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to processing procedures for workers’ compensation claims.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Department of  Consumer and Business Services shall conduct a
study that examines claims processing procedures and results  for workers’ compensation
claims in this state and present the department’s findings in a report  to an appropriate
committee or interim committee of the Legislative Assembly in the manner provided under
ORS 192.245 on or before September 15, 2022.
(2) In the study described in subsection (1) of this section, the department shall:
(a) Investigate whether significant  disparities exist between how quickly or cooperatively
self-insured employers respond to workers’ compensation claims and how quickly or
cooperatively workers’ compensation insurers respond to workers’ claims;
(b) Determine the rate and frequency at which  self-insured employers approve or deny
workers’ compensation claims compared with the  rate and frequency at which workers’
compensation insurers approve or deny workers’ claims; and
(c) Identify, to the extent possible, the likely causes and sources of any  differences or
disparities that the department uncovers in examining the  data from investigations under
paragraphs (a) and (b) of this subsection.
(3) If the department finds significant disparities as a  result of the department’s inves-
tigations under subsection (2) of  this section, the department in the report described in
subsection (1) of this section  shall recommend changes in statutes, administrative rules,
internal policies or enforcement measures that may be necessary to reduce  or correct the
disparities.
(4) In conducting investigations  under subsection (2) of this section, the department may
interview witnesses, require the production of documents and records and otherwise  take
actions necessary to develop information to complete the  study described in subsection (1)
of this section.
(5) An insurer or self-insured  employer that is subject to ORS chapter 656 shall cooperate
with the department in the department’s  investigations under subsection (2) of this section.
