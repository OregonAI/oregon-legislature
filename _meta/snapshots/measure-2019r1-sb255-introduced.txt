80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
Senate Bill 255
Sponsored by Senator ROBLAN, Representatives  SMITH DB, MCKEOWN; Senator HEARD, Representative
GOMBERG (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys from  General Fund to Higher Education Coordinating Commission for
Oregon Institute of Marine Biology at University of Oregon.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to Oregon Institute of Marine Biology funding; and declaring an emergency.
Whereas the University of Oregon has been teaching and conducting research in  marine biology
on the Southern Oregon coast since the 1920s; and
Whereas in 1929 the Army Corps of Engineers deeded  a portion of the Coos Head Military
Reservation to the University of Oregon to serve as the permanent home of the  university’s marine
biology program; and
Whereas, aside from a brief interruption during World War  II, the location and facilities have
supported research from both the University of Oregon and Oregon State University for  nearly 100
years; and
Whereas since the late 1960s the University of Oregon  has operated the Oregon Institute of
Marine Biology as a year-round research and teaching facility; and
Whereas the Oregon Institute  of Marine Biology was instrumental in the establishment of the
South Slough National Estuarine Research Reserve (SSNERR) and maintains  a substantial collab-
oration with the organization concerning both research and public education; and
Whereas the research done by faculty and students at  the Oregon Institute of Marine Biology
complements the academic inquiry that occurs at Oregon  State University’s Hatfield Marine Science
Center in Newport, Oregon; and
Whereas the Oregon Institute of Marine Biology and its Charleston Marine Life Center  serve
as cornerstone educational facilities for K-12, undergraduate, graduate and public education; and
Whereas the Oregon Institute  of Marine Biology brings significant federal funding and numerous
visitors to the Oregon Coast and is a cornerstone of the economy  in the Charleston, Coos Bay and
North Bend communities; and
Whereas the Oregon Institute of  Marine Biology offers the only undergraduate marine biology
major in this state; and
Whereas the undergraduate marine biology major offered by the  Oregon Institute of Marine
Biology is rapidly growing and highly ranked nationally; and
Whereas the teaching programs at the  Oregon Institute of Marine Biology prepare Oregon stu-
dents with the skills and knowledge to enter the workforce in natural resource fields and to  work
for state and federal public agencies; and

Whereas modern, safe research vessels are essential to ensuring that students and faculty have
access to important research and teaching sites in Oregon estuaries and the  Oregon territorial sea;
and
Whereas the Oregon Legislative Assembly has demonstrated a commitment to marine
infrastructure for public universities; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There is appropriated to the Higher Education  Coordinating Commission, for
the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, out of the General Fund, the amount of $500,000, for  the
Oregon Institute of Marine Biology at the University of Oregon for the purpose of  leveraging
private investment and replacing a research vessel at the institute.
SECTION 2. This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2019 Act takes effect
on July 1, 2019.
