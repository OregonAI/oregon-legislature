82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
Senate Bill 1006
Sponsored by Senator SMITH DB, Representatives OSBORNE, BOICE; Representatives GOODWIN, MORGAN
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Oregon  Department of Administrative Services for
distribution to Umpqua Fishery Enhancement Derby to conduct fish habitat restoration  projects at
specified locations.
Takes effect on 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the impacts of sediment on the Rogue River; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Department of  Administrative Services, for the biennium beginning July
1, 2023, out of the General Fund, the amount of $1,400,000, for distribution to Umpqua Fishery
Enhancement Derby to conduct restoration projects,  and associated educational components,
at the following locations to mitigate  the impacts of sediment on fish habitat in the Rogue
River:
(1) The Patterson Riparian Corridor;
(2) Mailboat Slough; and
(3) Williams Ranch.
SECTION 2.
This 2023 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on  which the 2023
regular session of the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
