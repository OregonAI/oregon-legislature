83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 2737
Sponsored by Representative HUDSON (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Directs ODE to conduct a study to help school districts that transport  students who live
outside of the district. (Flesch Readability Score: 67.3).
Requires the Department of Education to conduct a study  to identify methods for assisting
school districts that are required to provide transportation to students  who do not live in district
boundaries. Directs the department to submit findings to the interim committees of the  Legislative
Assembly related to education not later than September 15, 2026.
Sunsets on January 2, 2027.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to student transportation.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Department of Education shall conduct a study  to identify methods
for assisting a school district that provides transportation to any student who lives  outside
of the boundaries of the  school district but for whom the school district is responsible for
transporting because of requirements under state or federal  law to maintain the school of
origin for the student.
(2) The study conducted under this section shall review:
(a) Methods  for a school district to reduce the administrative and financial impact of
providing transportation described in subsection (1) of this section;
(b) Methods for a school  district to provide transportation for a student described in
subsection (1) of this section who needs individual transportation; and
(c) The attendance data for student populations that  are transported across district
boundaries as described in subsection (1) of this section.
(3) The department shall submit a report in the manner provided  by ORS 192.245, and
may include recommendations for legislation, to the interim  committees of the Legislative
Assembly related to education no later than September 15, 2026.
SECTION 2.
Section 1 of this 2025 Act is repealed on January 2, 2027.
