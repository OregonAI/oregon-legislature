79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 2891
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Department of Veterans’ Affairs to submit  report to interim legislative committees, no
later than July 1, 2018, regarding delivery of veterans’ services.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the Department of Veterans’ Affairs; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) No later than July 1, 2018, the Department of Veterans’ Affairs shall
submit a  report to the interim legislative committees of the Legislative Assembly on
veterans’ affairs regarding the provision of services to veterans in this state.
(2) The report  shall include a summary of implementation barriers and may include rec-
ommendations for legislative changes.
SECTION 2. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2017 Act takes effect
on its passage.
