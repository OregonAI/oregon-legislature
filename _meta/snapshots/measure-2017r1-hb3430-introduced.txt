House Bill 3430
Sponsored by Representatives SOLLMAN, VIAL, SMITH DB; Representatives GOMBERG, HELM,  HERNANDEZ,
MCLAIN, PILUSO, SMITH WARNER
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Provides  that  Department of State Lands shall distribute certain moneys that have been in
Common School Fund Account for 25 years or more. Requires any claims for moneys be paid  from
General Fund.
Directs department to describe disposition of those moneys on claim forms.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to proceeds from the sale of unclaimed property;  creating new provisions; and amending
ORS 98.392 and 98.396.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.  ORS 98.396 is amended to read:
98.396. (1) The Department of State Lands shall consider  any claim filed under ORS 98.392 and
may hold a hearing and receive evidence concerning the claim. If a hearing is  held, the department
shall prepare findings and a decision in writing on  each claim filed, stating the substance of any
evidence heard by the department and the reasons for the decision. The decision shall  be a public
record.
(2)(a) Except as provided  by paragraph (b) of this subsection,  if the claim allowed is for
property deposited in the Common School Fund Account, the department shall return the  property
or make payment of the proceeds of the sale of the  property to the claimant[ .] from the Common
School Fund Account.
(b) After proceeds of the sale of property  have been in the Common School Fund Account
for 25 years or more, the department shall:
(A) Make the amounts of the  proceeds available for distribution through the Common
School Fund; and
(B) Pay any claims for the amounts  of the proceeds as though the proceeds had been
deposited in the General Fund, as provided by subsection (3) of this section.
(3) If the claim allowed is for funds deposited in the General Fund  or  for claims to be paid
as provided by subsection (2)(b) of this section , the department shall  pay the claim and file a
request for reimbursement  with the State Treasurer. The State Treasurer shall reimburse the de-
partment within five working days from the fund against  which the check or order represented in
the claim was issued.
SECTION 2. The amendments to ORS 98.396 by section 1 of this 2017 Act apply to pro-
ceeds from the sale of  property that are deposited in the Common School Fund Account be-
fore, on or after the effective date of this 2017 Act.
