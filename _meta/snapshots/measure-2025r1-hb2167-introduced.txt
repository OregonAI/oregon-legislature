83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 2167
Introduced and  printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the request of House Interim Committee
on Agriculture, Land Use, Natural Resources, and Water for Representatives Ken Helm, Mark Owens)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act gives money to HECC to give to OSU for  veterinary programs. (Flesch Read-
ability Score: 63.4).
Appropriates moneys from the General Fund to the Higher Education  Coordinating Commission
for distribution to Oregon State University for veterinary programs.
Declares an emergency, effective July 1, 2025.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to state financial administration; and declaring an emergency.
Whereas Oregon  has six locations federally identified as suffering a shortage of veterinary
medical doctors; and
Whereas Oregon’s agriculture  is a significant economic sector producing 13 percent of the
state’s gross domestic product; and
Whereas zoonotic diseases are a persistent and growing public health threat; and
Whereas diseases and pathogens  threaten Oregon’s wildlife and can impact recreation opportu-
nities; and
Whereas the Carlson College of Veterinary Medicine at Oregon  State University is the only
accredited veterinary college in Oregon, with  a mission to advance the health of animals, people and
the environment through inclusive  and equitable education, research and services to the public; and
Whereas the college’s Lois Bates Acheson Veterinary  Teaching Hospital develops industry-
leading professionals through research and the practice of evidence-based medicine; and
Whereas the college also houses the Oregon Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory, the state’s  only
laboratory providing a full range of animal disease diagnostic services, which works  closely with
local, state and federal agencies to support public health and agricultural biosecurity through diag-
nostic testing and surveillance of zoonotic and foreign animal diseases; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Higher Education Coordinating Commission, for the biennium beginning  July 1,
2025, out of the General Fund, the amount of $10,000,000 for  distribution to the Carlson Col-
lege of Veterinary  Medicine at Oregon State University to be used to support veterinary ed-
ucation programs and the college’s Lois Bates Acheson Veterinary Teaching Hospital.
SECTION 2. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Higher Education Coordinating Commission, for the biennium beginning  July 1,
2025, out of the General Fund, the amount of $3,500,000 for  distribution to the Oregon
Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory at Oregon State University to  be used to support faculty
