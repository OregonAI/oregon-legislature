82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 3480
Sponsored by Representative OWENS
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board  to study budget of board. Directs board to
submit findings to interim committees of Legislative  Assembly related to ways and means not later
than September 15, 2024.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to financial administration of the Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board shall study the budget of the
board. The board shall submit a report in the  manner provided by ORS 192.245, and may in-
clude recommendations for  legislation, to the interim committees of the Legislative Assem-
bly related to ways and means no later than September 15, 2024.
SECTION 2. Section 1 of this 2023 Act is repealed on January 2, 2025.
