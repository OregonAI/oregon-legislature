81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
Senate Joint Resolution 26
Sponsored by Senator KNOPP
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Proposes amendment to  Oregon Constitution to prohibit expenditure of moneys received by Fund
for Student Success for any purpose other  than as appropriated in chapter 122, Oregon Laws 2019,
or expended for allowable purposes but in different proportion than authorized in chapter  122,
Oregon Laws 2019.
Refers proposed amendment to people for their approval or  rejection at special election held on
same date as next primary election.
JOINT RESOLUTION
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
PARAGRAPH 1.
 The Constitution of the State of Oregon is amended by creating a new  section
16 to be added to and made a part of Article IX, such section to read:
SECTION 16. Moneys received by the Fund for Student Success established under chapter
122, Oregon Laws 2019, may not be expended:
(1) For any purpose other than as appropriated in chapter 122, Oregon Laws 2019; or
(2) For allowable purposes  but in a different proportion than authorized in chapter 122,
Oregon Laws 2019.
PARAGRAPH 2. The amendment proposed by this resolution shall be submitted to  the
people for their approval or rejection at a special  election held throughout this state on the
same date as the next primary election.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
