82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
Senate Bill 815
Sponsored by Senators BONHAM, GORSEK, Representative HELFRICH; Senator  HANSELL, Representatives
BOSHART DAVIS, RUIZ
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to  Oregon Department of Administrative Services for
expenses related to Bridge of the Gods seismic strengthening.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the Bridge of the Gods; and declaring an emergency.
Whereas the Bridge of the Gods is the only interstate bridge between Portland and  Hood River
to provide passage over the Columbia River; and
Whereas the Bridge of  the Gods is a key transportation link that serves the northern Oregon
regional economy; and
Whereas the Bridge of the Gods is in a location that has been  identified as seismically vulner-
able; and
Whereas the Bridge of the  Gods is in need of seismic strengthening in order to continue to
provide safe and reliable service for the region; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services,  for the biennium beginning July
1, 2023, out of the General Fund,  the amount of $6,000,000, for distribution to the Port of
Cascade Locks for the Bridge of the Gods seismic strengthening project.
SECTION 2. This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2023 Act takes effect
July 1, 2023.
