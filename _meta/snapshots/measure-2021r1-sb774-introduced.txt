81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
Senate Bill 774
Sponsored by Senator DEMBROW
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs division of Oregon Department of Administrative Services that serves as  department’s
office of economic analysis to estimate savings to General  Fund in biennium beginning July 1, 2023,
resulting from certain prison closures.
Directs Legislative Assembly to appropriate such savings for  certain purposes in biennium be-
ginning July 1, 2023.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the criminal justice system; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) On or before June 1, 2023, the division of the Oregon Department  of Ad-
ministrative Services that serves as the department’s office of economic  analysis shall esti-
mate the amount of savings to the General Fund in the biennium beginning July 1, 2023,
resulting from  the closures of Mill Creek Correctional Facility, Shutter Creek Correctional
Institution and Warner Creek Correctional Facility.
(2) The Legislative Assembly  shall, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, appropriate
a total amount from the General Fund at least equal  to the amount estimated under sub-
section (1) of this section for the following purposes, without supplanting  previous levels of
funding for such purposes:
(a) The Justice Reinvestment Program established under section 53, chapter  649, Oregon
Laws 2013;
(b) Youth  development programs targeted at reducing youth involvement in the legal
system;
(c) Prison education, training, mental health and addiction programs; and
(d) Prisoner reentry programs.
SECTION 2.
This 2021 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2021 Act takes effect
on its passage.
