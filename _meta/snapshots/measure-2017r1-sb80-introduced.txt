79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 80
Printed pursuant to  Senate Interim Rule 213.28 by order of the President of the Senate in conformance with pre-
session filing rules,  indicating neither advocacy nor opposition on the part of the President (at the request
of Governor Kate Brown for Department of Veterans’ Affairs)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Director of Veterans’ Affairs to provide aid and assistance to federally recognized  In-
dian tribes in Oregon with regard to programs of service for Native American veterans  and ac-
creditation of federal tribal veterans representatives.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to assistance to veterans who are members of federally recognized Indian tribes.
Whereas the nine federally  recognized Indian tribes in Oregon are the Confederated Tribes of
the Warm Springs  Indian Reservation, Confederated Tribes of the Umatilla Indian Reservation,
Burns-Paiute Tribe, Confederated Tribes of  Siletz Indians of Oregon, Confederated Tribes of the
Grand Ronde, Cow Creek Band of Umpqua Indians, Confederated Coos,  Lower Umpqua and Siuslaw
Tribes, Klamath Tribe and Coquille Tribe; and
Whereas throughout America’s  history to the present day, Native Americans have served and
continue to serve in the United  States armed services at a higher rate than any other ethnic group;
and
Whereas during World War II, the entire population of Native Americans in the United States
was less than 350,000, of which 44,000 served in the armed services; and
Whereas more than 22,000 Native  Americans have served since September 11, 2001, or are cur-
rently serving in Operation Iraqi Freedom  (Iraq) or Operation Enduring Freedom (Afghanistan); and
Whereas Native Americans have a higher concentration of  active female service members than
any other ethnic group, and  female service members account for 20 percent of active Native Amer-
ican service members, compared to 15 percent for all other ethnic groups; and
Whereas according to the 2010 federal decennial  census, the estimated population of Native
Americans in Oregon is  109,223, the population of Native American veterans in Oregon as of 2014
is 3,856 and veterans constitute approximately 3.5 percent of all Native Americans in Oregon; and
Whereas the Department of Veterans’ Affairs and the federally recognized Indian  tribes in
Oregon have forged a partnership to assist Native American veterans; and
Whereas the State of Oregon and  the Department of Veterans’ Affairs recognize and honor the
service and sacrifice of Native American veterans; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In recognition and honor of the service and sacrifice of Oregon veterans who
are Native Americans, the Director of Veterans’ Affairs shall  provide aid and assistance to
federally recognized Indian tribes in Oregon with regard to:
(1) Programs of service for Native American veterans overseen by the tribes; and
