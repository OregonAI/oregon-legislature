83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 2196
Sponsored by Representative RESCHKE (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act makes a  new crime about harmful activity with a minor. (Flesch Readability
Score: 67.7).
Creates the crime of harmful communication with a minor. Punishes by a maximum of five
years’ imprisonment, $125,000 fine, or both.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to crimes involving minors; creating new provisions; and amending ORS 161.005.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) A person commits the crime of harmful communication with a minor if
the person is at least 18 years of age and knowingly engages in  a pattern of written or verbal
communication with a  minor that includes explicit and detailed descriptions or narrative
accounts of sexual conduct in a manner that causes harm to the minor.
(2) Harmful communication with a minor is a Class C felony.
(3) It is not a defense to a prosecution under this section that the person did not  know
the minor’s age, that the person reasonably believed that the minor was  at least 18 years
of age, or that the minor misrepresented the minor’s age to the person.
(4) As used in this section:
(a) “Minor” means a person under 18 years of age.
(b) “Sexual conduct” has the meaning given that term in ORS 163.472.
