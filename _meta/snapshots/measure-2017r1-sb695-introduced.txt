79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 695
Sponsored by Senator KNOPP
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires State Forester to manage state forestlands to provide for timber sales each year in
amount that equals  or exceeds 125 percent of 2014 timber sales amount. Provides for moneys from
timber sales exceeding 2014 timber sales amount to be used for funding high school and community
college career and technical education,  STEM Investment Grant Program distribution to public
universities, and kindergarten to grade 12 education.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to timber sales from state forestlands.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used in this section, “state forestlands” means lands managed by  the
State Forester under ORS 530.050.
(2) In carrying out state forestland management under  ORS 530.050, the State Forester
shall ensure that timber is sold each calendar year for harvesting from those lands  in an
amount that, on a board feet basis,  is equal to or greater than 125 percent of the amount
of timber sold for harvesting from state forestlands during the 2014 calendar year.
(3) The State Forester shall  annually estimate the board feet of timber that will be sold
for harvesting from state  forestlands during the calendar year. The State Forester shall
complete the annual estimate prior to the first sale that year of timber for  harvesting from
state forestlands. The State Forester shall determine, based upon the estimated board  feet
of timber to be sold during the year, the fraction of the  estimated timber sales that repres-
ents an increase from the amount of timber that was sold for harvesting from  state
forestlands in 2014.
(4) For any moneys received from a timber sale under ORS 530.050,  the State Forester
shall determine the sum obtained  by multiplying the amount of the moneys by the fraction
determined by the State Forester  under subsection (3) of this section for the year of the sale.
Notwithstanding ORS 530.110, when moneys  from the timber sale are paid into the State
Treasury and  credited to the State Forestry Department Account, the sum determined as
provided in this subsection shall be placed in a separate subaccount and distributed as  fol-
lows:
(a) Twenty-five percent  to the Department of Education, for awarding grants under the
Career and Technical Education Revitalization Grant Program established by ORS 344.075.
(b) Twenty-five percent to the Higher  Education Coordinating Commission, for deposit in
the Community College  Support Fund to be distributed to community colleges and commu-
nity college service districts for career and technical education programs.
(c) Twenty-five percent to the Department of Education for deposit to the  STEM In-
vestment Grant Program, to be distributed as described  in ORS 327.380 to public universities.
