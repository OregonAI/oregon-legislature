80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 2212
Introduced and  printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the request of House Interim Committee
on Higher Education and Workforce Development)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to  Oregon Department of Administrative Services for
purposes of providing financial assistance to  state agencies that provide workplace accommodations
for employees with disabilities.
Permits department to adopt rules for administering grants.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to employees with disabilities.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Oregon Department of Administrative  Services shall, from the Cen-
tralized Accommodation Fund established under section 3 of this 2019 Act, distribute  moneys
through grants to  state agencies for the purpose of providing financial assistance to cover
costs related to providing workplace accommodations for employees with disabilities.
(2) The department shall make grant payments to eligible state agencies on  a biennial
basis.
(3) A  grant may be awarded under subsection (1) of this section to state agencies to cover
the costs of providing workplace accommodations that exceed an amount of $______.
(4) The department  may adopt any rules necessary for the administration of grants under
subsection (1) of this section.
(5) As used  in this section, “state agency” has the meaning given that term in ORS
36.110.
SECTION 2. There is appropriated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services,
for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, out of the General  Fund, the amount of $______ to
be deposited in the Centralized  Accommodation Fund established under section 3 of this 2019
Act.
SECTION 3. The Centralized Accommodation Fund is established in the State Treasury,
separate and distinct from the General Fund. Interest on the moneys in the Centralized Ac-
commodation Fund must be credited to the fund. Moneys in the  fund are continuously ap-
propriated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services for  the purposes described
in section 1 of this 2019 Act.NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
