80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 2953
Sponsored by Representatives BONHAM, SMITH  DB, Senator HEARD; Representatives FINDLEY, WALLAN,
WILSON, ZIKA, Senators BAERTSCHIGER JR, HANSELL
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Requires State Forestry Department to report to committee of  Legislative Assembly regarding
environmental and employment impacts of Good  Neighbor Authority Agreement projects funded in
whole or in part by moneys made available to department for project purposes.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the impacts of Good Neighbor Authority Agreement projects.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The State Forestry Department shall report regarding the environmental
and employment  impacts of Good Neighbor Authority Agreement projects described in sec-
tion 1, chapter 96, Oregon  Laws 2018, that are financed in whole or in part with moneys made
available to the department for those projects.  The department shall report regarding each
project to an interim or regular committee of the  Legislative Assembly relating to economic
development in the manner provided by ORS 192.245 no later than six  months following
completion of the project.
