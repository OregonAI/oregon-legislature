83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 3825
Sponsored by Representative CHOTZEN
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act changes laws about fines and fees in  criminal cases. The Act takes effect on the
91st day after sine die. (Flesch Readability Score: 80.3).
Shortens the time period after which judgment remedies for monetary obligations in  criminal
judgments without restitution expire. Provides that the expiration of judgment remedies means that
a person has complied with that portion of a sentence for purposes of expungement.
Directs courts to vacate monetary obligation orders when dismissing a criminal case.
Directs the Judicial Department to conduct a study  on the automatic expiration or vacation of
monetary obligations other than restitution after a specified time period.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to monetary obligations  in criminal cases; creating new provisions; amending ORS 18.180
and 137.225; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:

SECTION 3.
(1) When a court enters a judgment or  order dismissing a criminal pro-
ceeding, the court shall vacate any monetary obligations previously ordered in the  proceed-
ing, including any costs ordered pursuant to ORS 151.505.
(2) The vacation of monetary obligations under subsection (1) of  this section does not
affect any payment made prior to the dismissal, including the payment of costs  and expenses
pursuant to ORS 135.705 or the payment of a fee under ORS 137.533.
SECTION 4. Section 3 of this 2025 Act applies to dismissals occurring on or after the ef-
fective date of this 2025 Act.
SECTION 5. (1) The Judicial Department, in consultation with the Department  of Re-
venue, shall conduct a study on:
(a) The  feasibility of implementing automatic expiration or vacation of monetary obli-
gations other than restitution in criminal judgments after a specified period of time;
(b) Any legislative changes required  for the automatic expiration or vacation of monetary
obligations described in paragraph (a) of this subsection; and
(c) The costs of implementing the automatic  expiration or vacation of monetary obli-
gations described in paragraph (a) of this subsection.
(2) The Judicial Department shall submit a report with the results  of the study in the
manner provided by ORS 192.245, and may include recommendations for legislation, to the
interim committees of  the Legislative Assembly related to the judiciary no later than Sep-
tember 15, 2027.
SECTION 6.
Section 5 of this 2025 Act is repealed on January 2, 2028.
SECTION 7. This 2025 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on which the 2025
regular session of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
