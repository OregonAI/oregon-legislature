79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 722
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Oregon Criminal Justice Commission to  study imposition of fees in criminal cases and
report results to interim committees of Legislative Assembly related to judiciary on or  before Feb-
ruary 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to fees.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Oregon Criminal Justice Commission shall study  the amount of fees
routinely imposed in criminal cases in each county  in the state and present the results of
the study in a report to the  interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to the
judiciary in the manner provided under ORS 192.245 on or before February 1, 2019.
