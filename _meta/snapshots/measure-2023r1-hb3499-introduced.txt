82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 3499
Sponsored by Representative CHAICHI
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Prohibits public universities from charging tuition or mandatory enrollment fees to resident
undergraduate or graduate students.
Prohibits community colleges from charging tuition to students who  are residents of community
college  district.
Appropriates moneys from  General Fund to Higher Education Coordinating Commission for
purpose of eliminating tuition and mandatory enrollment fees.
Requires Higher  Education Coordinating Commission to conduct study to determine best meth-
ods for eliminating governing board at each public university, eliminating bonding  authorities of
public universities and making commission responsible  for direct oversight and management of each
public university.
Declares  legislative  finding that it is not in public interest of state for public universities to
have independent governing boards and declares study is first step towards making  commission re-
sponsible for  direct oversight and management of each public university no later than 2027-2028
academic year.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to higher education; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) Notwithstanding ORS 352.102 or any other provision of law, a public uni-
versity listed in ORS 352.002 may not charge tuition or mandatory enrollment fees.
(2) This section applies to tuition and mandatory enrollment fees for:
(a) Resident undergraduate students who are earning  their first undergraduate degree;
and
(b) Resident graduate students who are earning their first graduate degree.
SECTION 2.
(1) Notwithstanding ORS 341.290 or any other provision of law, a community
college operated under ORS chapter 341 may not charge tuition.
(2) This section applies to tuition for students who are  residents of the community col-
lege district in which the community college is located.
SECTION 3. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the  Higher Education Coordinating Commission, for the biennium beginning July 1,
2023, out of the General Fund, the amount of $____, to be allocated to  public universities
listed in ORS 352.002  and community colleges operated under ORS chapter 341 for the pur-
pose of carrying out the provisions of sections 1 and 2 of this 2023 Act.
SECTION 4. (1) The Higher Education Coordinating Commission shall  conduct a study to
determine the best methods for:
(a) Eliminating the governing board at each public university listed in ORS 352.002;
(b) Eliminating the authorities of public  universities with respect to state bonds that are
set forth in ORS 352.388 to 352.415; and
(c) Making the commission responsible for the direct oversight and management of  each
