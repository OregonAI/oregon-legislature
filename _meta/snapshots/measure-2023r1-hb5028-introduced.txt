82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 5028
Introduced and printed  pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 23 (2023) (at the request of Oregon Department
of Administrative Services)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Land Use Board of Appeals for biennial expenses.
Limits biennial expenditures from fees, moneys  or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Re-
ceipts, but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received by board.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the financial  administration of the Land Use Board of Appeals; and declaring an emer-
gency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There is appropriated to the Land Use Board of Appeals, for  the biennium
beginning July 1, 2023, out of the General Fund, the amount of $2,741,598.
SECTION 2. Notwithstanding any other law limiting  expenditures, the amount of $38,019
is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, as the maximum limit for payment of
expenses from fees,  moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts, but ex-
cluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received by the Land Use Board of Ap-
peals.
SECTION 3.
This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2023 Act takes effect
July 1, 2023.
Note: For budget, see 2023-2025 Biennial Budget
