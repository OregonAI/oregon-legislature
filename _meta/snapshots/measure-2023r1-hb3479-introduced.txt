82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 3479
Sponsored by Representative OWENS
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires Water Resources Department  to study budget of department. Directs department to
submit findings to interim committees of Legislative  Assembly related to ways and means not later
than September 15, 2024.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to financial administration of the Water Resources Department.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Water Resources Department shall study the budget of the department.
The department shall submit a report  in the manner provided by ORS 192.245, and may in-
clude recommendations for legislation, to the interim committees of the  Legislative Assem-
bly related to ways and means no later than September 15, 2024.
SECTION 2. Section 1 of this 2023 Act is repealed on January 2, 2025.
