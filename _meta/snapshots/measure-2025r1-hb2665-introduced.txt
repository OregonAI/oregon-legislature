83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 2665
Sponsored by Representative EVANS (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Creates  COU. Takes effect 91 days after the legislative session ends. (Flesch Readability
Score:  62.8).
Establishes Central Oregon University as  a separate public university, distinct from Oregon
State University.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to  the establishment of Central Oregon University; creating new provisions; amending ORS
352.002, 352.011 and 352.054; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
 ORS 352.002 is amended to read:
352.002. The following are established as public universities in the State of Oregon:
(1) University of Oregon.
(2) Oregon State University.
(3) Portland State University.
(4) Oregon Institute of Technology.
(5) Western Oregon University.
(6) Southern Oregon University.
(7) Eastern Oregon University.
(8) Central Oregon University.
SECTION 2.
 ORS 352.011 is amended to read:
352.011. (1) As used in this  section, “professional doctoral degree” means a first professional
degree, excluding a Doctor of Philosophy degree, that:
(a) Represents a category of qualifications in a professional subject  area that requires students
to have previously completed specified undergraduate coursework or obtained previous undergradu-
ate degrees before enrolling in the professional doctoral degree granting program; and
(b) Is  awarded upon completion of a program that provides the knowledge and skills for the re-
cognition, credential or license required for professional practice.
(2) Western Oregon University, Southern Oregon University , [ and] Eastern  Oregon University
and Central Oregon University  are established as comprehensive universities that offer a full
range of baccalaureate programs and graduate programs through  the master’s degree and the pro-
fessional doctoral degree.
SECTION 3.
 ORS 352.054 is amended to read:
352.054. (1) A governing board is established for each of the following public universities:
(a) University of Oregon;

SECTION 4.
Notwithstanding the operative date set forth in section 7 of this 2025 Act,
not later than November  30, 2025, the Governor shall appoint all of the members of the Board
of Trustees of Central Oregon University in the manner set forth  in ORS 352.076 so that the
appointees may be confirmed by the Senate in the manner provided in ORS 171.562 and
171.565 by March 1, 2026.
SECTION 5.
The Board of Trustees of Oregon  State University shall continue to have
jurisdiction over the operations of Oregon State University - Cascades through June  30, 2026.
However, the Board of Trustees of  Central Oregon University shall propose funding requests
pursuant to ORS 350.090  and 352.089, and shall prepare budgets, in cooperation with the
Higher Education Coordinating Commission, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2027.
SECTION 6. (1) Except as otherwise provided in this section, all persons employed by
Oregon State University on the operative date specified in section 7 of this  2025 Act and
whose job functions pertain  to Oregon State University - Cascades shall continue their em-
ployment with Central Oregon University and shall retain any seniority, contractual rights
or tenure granted prior to the  operative date specified in section 7 of this 2025 Act. Nothing
in this section shall affect any term or condition of  any collective bargaining agreement in
effect on the operative date specified in section 7 of this 2025 Act.
(2) All of the duties, functions, powers and lawfully incurred rights and obligations of the
Board of Trustees of Oregon State University that  pertain to Oregon State University -Cas-
cades are  transferred to and vested in the Board of Trustees of Central Oregon University.
The transfer shall include all applicable contractual rights and obligations and title to all
applicable records, property, supplies and materials, including equipment,  books and papers.

(7) Nothing in sections 4 to 6 of this 2025 Act or  the amendments to ORS 352.002, 352.011
and 352.054 by  sections 1 to 3 of this 2025 Act relieves any person, public entity or private

forfeiture, rule, policy, document, record or proceeding.
(8)(a) Notwithstanding any other provision of this section, the lawfully adopted standards
and policies of Oregon State University pertaining to Oregon State University - Cascades that
are in effect on the operative date specified in section 7 of this  2025 Act continue in effect

of Central Oregon University.
(b) References in standards or policies described in paragraph (a)  of this subsection to
Oregon State University, the Board of Trustees of  Oregon State University or an employee
of Oregon State University are considered to be references  to Central Oregon University, the
Board of Trustees  of Central Oregon University or an employee of Central Oregon Univer-
sity.
