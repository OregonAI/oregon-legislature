83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Senate Concurrent Resolution 25
Sponsored by Senator PATTERSON, Representatives ANDERSEN, EVANS; Representatives GOMBERG, MANNIX
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Honors the life and memory of Ruth McEwen. (Flesch Readability Score: 71.8).
Recognizes  and  honors Ruth McEwen for her outstanding service to the people of the State of
Oregon.
CONCURRENT RESOLUTION
Whereas Ruth McEwen was a trailblazing advocate who used  her lived experience as a person
with a physical disability to help dismantle  barriers for older adults and people with disabilities in
Oregon;  and
Whereas Ruth McEwen had a distinguished career in special education, counseling  and program
oversight, and her work spanned four decades of advocacy with NorthWest Senior  and Disability
Services and the Oregon Association of Area Agencies on Aging and Disabilities; and
Whereas Ruth McEwen’s tireless commitment to systemic change and equity for older adults and
people with disabilities helped change the landscape of Oregon’s system of long term care; and
Whereas Ruth McEwen was instrumental in  Oregon’s first-in-the-nation move from institutional
care for seniors and people with disabilities to community-based services; and
Whereas Ruth McEwen’s work and voice  on behalf of seniors and people with disabilities in
Fairview, Oregon, contributed to the decision to move them to community-based care; and
Whereas Ruth McEwen served in numerous leadership positions  on the Governors’ Commission
on Senior Services, the Oregon Disabilities Commission,  the Home Care Commission, the Senior
Advisory Council and Disability  Advisory Council for NorthWest Senior and Disability Services and
a large number of workgroups and committees with the Department of Human Services; and
Whereas Ruth  McEwen was a familiar presence in the Oregon State Capitol, bringing her ex-
pertise and advocacy to policy and budget discussions; and
Whereas Ruth McEwen’s advocacy work was celebrated by countless awards and  recognitions,
including honors from the Department of Human Services and NorthWest Senior  and Disability
Services; and
Whereas Ruth McEwen was an indispensable ally to the communities  for whom she advocated,
but to her colleagues at NorthWest Senior and Disability Services, she was family; and
Whereas Ruth McEwen passed away on December 25, 2024, and will be greatly missed;  now,
therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of the Eighty-third  Legislative Assembly, recognize and honor Ruth
McEwen for her outstanding service to the people of the State of Oregon; and be it further
