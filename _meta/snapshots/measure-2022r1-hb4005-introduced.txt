81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2022 Regular Session
House Bill 4005
Introduced and  printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the request of House Interim Committee
on Early Childhood for Representative Karin Power)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Extends date for transition  of Early Learning Division to Department of Early Learning and
Care.
Extends date by which Early Learning Council is required to establish  by rule process for de-
termining true cost of child care compared to market  rate cost for purposes of Employment Related
Day Care subsidy program.
Clarifies authority of Early Learning Division related to day care  resource and referral system.
Extends date by which Early Learning Division is required to submit report on proposed  im-
plementation of prohibition on suspensions or expulsions of children from certain publicly funded
early childhood care or education programs.
Clarifies entities with which Early Learning Division may share specified  information related
to child care.
Corrects certain statutory cross-references related to Central  Background Check Registry.
Modifies Employment Related  Day Care subsidy program to require certain individuals associated
with subsidized care provider to be enrolled in Central Background Registry.
Authorizes Early Learning  Division to use moneys in Early Learning Account to pay for costs
incurred in conducting biennial evaluation related to early childhood support grant program.
Directs Department of Human  Services to adopt rules to establish reimbursement rates under
Employment Related Day Care  subsidy program that are specified percentile of market rate cost for
child care.
Delays applicability of any  requirements related to automatic residential fire sprinkler systems
in child care facilities.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.

REIMBURSEMENT RATE FOR EMPLOYMENT RELATED DAY CARE
SECTION 32.
(1) No later than June 1, 2022, the  Department of Human Services shall
adopt rules to provide reimbursement rates  under the Employment Related Day Care subsidy
program, operated as provided by ORS 329A.500, that are at least:
(a) For child care provided in a  certified or registered family child care home or in a
certified child care center,  the 90th percentile of the 2020 Oregon Child Care Market Price
Study rate.
(b) For child care provided by an exempt child care center that  is enhanced, 85 percent
of the rate that  applies to child care provided in a certified child care center, as calculated
based on the Department of Human Services maximum rates in effect on January 1, 2022.
(c) For child care provided by  an exempt child care center that is not enhanced, 75 per-
cent of the rate  that applies to child care provided in a certified child care center, as calcu-
lated based on the Department of Human Services maximum  rates in effect on January 1,
2022.
(d) For an exempt family  child care home, regardless of enhanced or not enhanced, an
18 percent increase to the rates for those providers,  as calculated based on the Department
of Human Services maximum rates in effect on January 1, 2022.
(2) As used in this section:
(a) “Enhanced” means a child care  provider that meets training requirements prescribed
by the Department of Human Services by rule.
(b) “Exempt” means  a child care provider or center that is exempt from certification by
the Office of Child Care under rules adopted by the Early Learning Council.
