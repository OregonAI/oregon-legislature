83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Senate Bill 543
Sponsored by Senator SOLLMAN (at the request of Centro Cultural de Washington County) (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act tells ODOT to  translate student driver education and instructor guides into
Spanish. ODOT must put  the materials on its website. ODOT must also offer printed copies but may
charge a fee to cover the costs of printing. ODOT must update the guides every  three years. (Flesch
Readability Score: 61.7).
Directs the Department of  Transportation to translate student driver education curriculum and
student driver instructor curriculum into the Spanish language and post the materials  on the
department’s website.  Directs the department to provide printed copies and authorizes the depart-
ment to charge a fee to cover the printing costs.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to traffic safety education.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Department of Transportation shall translate the student driver  ed-
ucation curriculum and the student driver education instructor curriculum into the  Spanish
language and provide links to the curriculum on the department’s website.
(2) The department shall also make printed copies available for purchase and may charge
a fee to cover the printing costs.
(3) The translated materials must be updated at least once every three years.
