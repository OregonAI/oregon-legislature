83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 2591
Sponsored by Representative PHAM H (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act makes a new tax credit for CRNAs and doctors who  do anesthesiology. The Act
also tells OHA to make rules to let CRNAs and those doctors be  part of the health care provider
incentive program. (Flesch Readability Score: 69.1).
Establishes an income tax credit for physicians who practice anesthesiology and for certified
registered nurse anesthetists who do not qualify for rural health care provider tax credits.
Applies to tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026.
Directs the Oregon Health Authority to  include as eligible providers physicians who practice
anesthesiology and certified registered nurse anesthetists in the health care provider incentive pro-
gram.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to incentives for individuals who practice anesthesiology;  creating new provisions; amend-
ing ORS 315.613; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2025 Act is added to and made a part of ORS chapter 315.
SECTION 2. (1) A resident or nonresident individual who is not entitled  to the tax credit
described in ORS 315.613 shall be allowed a credit against taxes that are otherwise due under
ORS chapter 316 if during the tax year the individual is:
(a)(A) A physician licensed under ORS chapter 677 who practices anesthesiology; or
(B) A certified registered nurse anesthetist licensed under ORS 678.279; and
(b) Engaged in  the practice of anesthesiology for at least 20 hours per week, averaged
over the month, during the tax year.
(2) The amount of the credit allowed under this section shall be $3,000.
(3) The credit allowed under this section may not exceed the tax  liability of the taxpayer
and may not be carried forward to another tax year.
(4) A nonresident shall be  allowed the credit under this section in the proportion provided
in ORS 316.117. If a change in the status of a taxpayer  from resident to nonresident or from
nonresident to resident occurs, the credit allowed by this section  shall be determined in a
manner consistent with ORS 316.117.

consistent with ORS 316.117.

a partnership, only the time of the individual shareholder or partner shall  be considered and the full
amount of the credit shall be allowed to each shareholder or partner who qualifies in an individual
capacity.

SECTION 4.
Section 2 of this 2025 Act and the amendments to ORS 315.613 by  section 3
of this 2025 Act apply to tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026.
SECTION 5. The Oregon Health Authority  shall adopt rules to include as qualified health
care providers eligible for participation in the health care provider incentive program created
under ORS 676.454 an individual who is, or who is training to become:
(1) A physician licensed under ORS chapter 677 who practices anesthesiology; and
(2) A certified registered nurse anesthetist licensed under ORS 678.279.
SECTION 6.
This 2025 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on which the 2025
regular session of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
