CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to brassica production in the Willamette Valley Protected District; amending  section 4,
chapter 370, Oregon Laws 2023; repealing section 1, chapter 370, Oregon Laws 2023; and de-
claring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
 Section 4, chapter 370, Oregon Laws 2023, is amended to read:
Sec. 4.  [ Sections 1 and 3  of this 2023 Act are ] Section 3, chapter 370, Oregon Laws 2023, is
repealed on July 1, 2024.
SECTION 2. Section 1, chapter 370, Oregon Laws 2023, is repealed on January 2, 2028.
SECTION 3. This 2024 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency  is declared to exist, and this 2024 Act takes effect
on its passage.
