79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 2590
Sponsored by Representative HAYDEN,  Senators KRUSE, PROZANSKI; Representatives LIVELY, NEARMAN
(Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Oregon Department of  Administrative Services to issue grant to organization dedicated
to addressing community needs relating  to October 1, 2015, shooting on campus of Umpqua Com-
munity College.
Appropriates moneys to Oregon Department of Administrative Services.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to community recovery after the tragedy at Umpqua Community College; and declaring an
emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services,  for the biennium beginning July
1, 2017, out of the  General Fund, the amount of $3 million for the purposes of carrying out
the requirements of section 2 of this 2017 Act.
SECTION 2. (1) The Oregon Department of  Administrative Services shall issue a grant
in the amount of $3  million to an organization that is dedicated to addressing community
needs relating to  the October 1, 2015, shooting on the campus of Umpqua Community College.
(2) The organization shall use grant funds to assess and address community needs.
SECTION 3.
This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2017 Act takes effect
on its passage.
