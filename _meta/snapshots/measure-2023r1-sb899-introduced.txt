82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
Senate Bill 899
Sponsored by Senators SMITH DB, FREDERICK, WEBER
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys to Invasive Species Council, out of General Fund, for certain purposes.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the Invasive Species Council; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Invasive Species Council, for the biennium beginning July  1, 2023, out of the
General Fund, the amount of $480,000, for the following purposes:
(1) $80,000 for emergency purposes.
(2) $100,000 for outreach and education.
(3) $300,000 for the operation of the council.
SECTION 2.
This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2023 Act takes effect
on its passage.
