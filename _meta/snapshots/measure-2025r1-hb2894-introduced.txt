83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 2894
Sponsored by Representative EVANS (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act would direct a portion of lottery revenue for tuition waivers for certain soldiers.
The Act takes effect 91 days after sine die. (Flesch Readability Score: 60.7).
Allocates $500,000 of lottery revenue to the Oregon  Military Department for purposes of pro-
viding tuition waivers for  eligible post-secondary institutions to members of the Oregon National
Guard.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the Oregon Military Department; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) There is allocated for the  biennium beginning July 1, 2025, from the Ad-
ministrative Services Economic Development Fund, to  the Oregon Military Department, the
amount of $500,000.
(2) Notwithstanding any other law limiting expenditures:
(a) The amount of $475,000 is established for  the biennium beginning July 1, 2025, as the
maximum limit for payment of expenses by the Oregon Military  Department from lottery
moneys allocated from the Administrative Services Economic Development Fund  for the
purpose of providing tuition waivers to  members of the Oregon National Guard as provided
in ORS 399.275.
(b) The amount of $25,000 is established  for the biennium beginning July 1, 2025, as the
maximum limit for payment of expenses by the Oregon Military  Department from lottery
moneys allocated from the Administrative Services Economic Development Fund  for the
purpose of paying administrative costs  associated with providing tuition waivers to members
of the  Oregon National Guard as provided in ORS 399.275 from the funds allocated under
subsection (1) of this section.
(3) The department may not pay  for a tuition waiver for a member of the Oregon Na-
tional Guard from the funds allocated under subsection (1) of  this section unless the member
has exhausted all federal tuition assistance programs available to the member.
SECTION 2.
This 2025 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on  which the 2025
regular session of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
