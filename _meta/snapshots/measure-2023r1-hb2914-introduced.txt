82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2914
Sponsored by Representative GOMBERG (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Establishes Task Force on Abandoned and Derelict Vessels. Directs task  force to examine is-
sues related to seizing and disposing of abandoned vessels and derelict vessels.
Directs State Marine Board and Department of State Lands  to jointly identify and prioritize
abandoned vessels and derelict vessels for disposal.
Directs Department of State Lands to  develop web-based mapping tool for receiving information
about location of suspected and confirmed abandoned vessel or derelict vessel.
Takes effect on 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to marine vessels; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Task Force on Abandoned and Derelict Vessels is established.
(2) The task force consists of 18 members appointed as follows:
(a) The President of the Senate shall  appoint one member from among members of the
Senate.
(b) The Speaker of the House of Representatives shall appoint one member from  among
members of the House of Representatives.
(c) The Governor shall appoint as members one representative  from each of the follow-
ing:
(A) The Department of Environmental Quality.
(B) The Department of State Lands.
(C) The State Department of Fish and Wildlife.
(D) The State Marine Board.
(E) The Oregon State Police.
(d) The Governor shall appoint:
(A) One member representing the Oregon State Sheriffs’ Association.
(B) One member representing the Oregon Association Chiefs of Police.
(C) Three members representing the Oregon Public Ports Association.
(D) Two members representing private marinas in this state.
(E) Three members representing the recreational and commercial fishing industry.
(F) One member representing a nonprofit environmental advocacy organization.
(3) The purpose of the task force is  to study and make recommendations on laws gov-
erning the seizing and disposing of abandoned vessels and derelict  vessels. Specifically, the
task force shall prepare a report that addresses:
(a) Funding  for an abandoned and derelict vessel program in this state that includes
moneys for the seizure, removal and disposal of abandoned vessels and derelict vessels.

(b) Coordination of efforts  between state and local officials to seize, remove and dispose
of abandoned vessels and derelict vessels.
(c) Recommendations for disposing abandoned vessels and derelict vessels  in a cost-
effective manner.
(d) Whether additional insurance coverage for vessels is recommended.
(e) Whether the state should require hull integrity inspections before the  sale of vessels
over a certain size in this state.
(f) Whether the state should sponsor a “vessel turn-in program.”
(g) Whether the existing penalties for the abandonment of vessels are adequate.
(h) Owner accountability for the costs of the seizure, removal, salvage and disposal of
abandoned vessels and derelict vessels in Oregon.
(i) Any other recommendations the task force considers important.
(4) A  majority of the voting members of the task force constitutes a quorum for the
transaction of business.
(5) Official action by the task force requires the approval of a majority  of the voting
members of the task force.
(6) The task force shall elect one of its members to serve as chairperson.
(7) If there  is a vacancy for any cause, the appointing authority shall make an appoint-
ment to become immediately effective.
(8) The task force shall meet at times and places specified by the call of  the chairperson
or of a majority of the voting members of the task force.
(9) The task force may adopt rules necessary for the operation of the task force.
(10) The task force shall submit a report in the  manner provided by ORS 192.245, and
may include recommendations for  legislation, to an interim committee of the Legislative
Assembly related to natural resources no later than September 15, 2024.
(11) The Legislative Policy and Research Director shall provide  staff support to the task
force.
(12) Members of  the Legislative Assembly appointed to the task force are nonvoting
members of the task force and may act in an advisory capacity only.
(13) Members of the task force who are not members of the Legislative  Assembly are not
entitled to compensation or reimbursement for expenses and serve as volunteers on the  task
force.
(14) All agencies of state government, as  defined in ORS 174.111, are directed to assist
the task force in the performance of  the duties of the task force and, to the extent permitted
by laws relating to confidentiality, to furnish information and advice the members of the task
force consider necessary to perform their duties.
SECTION 2.
Section 1 of this 2023 Act is repealed on December 31, 2024.
SECTION 3. Section 4 of this  2023 Act is added to and made a part of ORS 830.908 to
830.948.
SECTION 4. The State Marine Board and the Department of State Lands shall jointly:
(1) Identify the locations of abandoned vessels and derelict vessels; and
(2) Prioritize seizure and disposal of abandoned vessels and derelict vessels in the  order
of the hazard the vessels present.
SECTION 5.
Section 6 of this  2023 Act is added to and made a part of ORS 274.376 to
274.388.

SECTION 6.
(1) The Department of State Lands shall develop and regularly update  a
web-based mapping tool for receiving information about the locations of suspected and con-
firmed abandoned vessels and derelict vessels.
(2) The web-based mapping tool must  permit members of the general public and law
enforcement to  report to the department the location of a suspected and confirmed aban-
doned vessel or derelict vessel.
SECTION 7.
This 2023 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on  which the 2023
regular session of the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
