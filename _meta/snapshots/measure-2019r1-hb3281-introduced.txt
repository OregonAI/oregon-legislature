80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 3281
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY (at the request of Representative Jeff Barker)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires Oregon Health Authority to research, study and  compare behavioral health treatment
in other states  and report findings to appropriate interim committees of Legislative Assembly no
later than September 15, 2020.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to behavioral health.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Oregon Health  Authority shall research, study and compare behavioral
health treatment in other states and report  the authority’s findings, in the manner provided
in ORS 192.245, to the appropriate  interim committees of the Legislative Assembly no later
than September 15, 2020.
