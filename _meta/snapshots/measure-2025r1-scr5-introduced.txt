83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Senate Concurrent Resolution 5
Sponsored by Senator PROZANSKI, Representative LIVELY (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Honors baseball teams from Thurston High School. (Flesch Readability Score: 66.7).
Commends the 2023 and 2024 back-to-back state  champion Thurston High School baseball teams
for outstanding seasons.
CONCURRENT RESOLUTION
Whereas on June 1, 2024, the Thurston High School Colts baseball team  from Springfield capped
an outstanding  regular season by winning the Oregon School Activities Association (OSAA) Class
5A state championship at PK Park in Eugene; and
Whereas the Colts defeated West Albany High School 2-0 in the title game; and
Whereas the Colts rode a brilliant starting pitching performance  from Grady Saunders and
stellar relief pitching from Eli Christ to seal the title game victory; and
Whereas the 2024 title was  a remarkable repeat state championship for Thurston, which also
defeated West Albany to win the 2023 title; and
Whereas five members of the 2023 Colts team graduated, presenting the 2024 team with a  hefty
challenge in their quest for back-to-back titles; and
Whereas Colts coach Dennis Minium said of his 2024 team: “They play really well with a big
target on their back and they’re going to make somebody beat us, for sure.”; and
Whereas the 2024 Colts were nearly perfect all season long, losing only once  to an in-state team
and finishing the season with a superb overall record of 28-3; and
Whereas Maddox Molony was honored as the 2023 Class  5A co-player of the year, Connor
Molony was honored as the  pitcher of the year and Dennis Minium was honored as coach of the
year; and
Whereas also earning 2023 Class 5A all-state honors were Eli Christ, Easton McDonald, Adam
Elliott, Grady Saunders and Collin Hernandez; and
Whereas Eli Christ was honored as the 2024 Class  5A player of the year and Grady Saunders
was honored as the pitcher of the year; and
Whereas also earning 2024 Class 5A all-state honors were Brock Johnson, Adam Elliott,  Kyle
Miller, Connor Molony and Collin Hernandez; and
Whereas Colts baseball players and coaches exhibited outstanding character and  sportsmanship
on and off the playing field throughout the 2023 and 2024 seasons; and
Whereas the back-to-back state championships were the second and third state titles  for
Thurston in program history, following their title in 2009; and
Whereas the 2023 and  2024 Thurston baseball teams thrilled Colts fans and earned the respect
of fans around  the state with their skill, courage and fighting spirit, refusing to be eliminated until
