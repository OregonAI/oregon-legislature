80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 2819
Sponsored by Representative BONHAM
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires Water Resources Department to establish pilot program to make  conserved Columbia
River water available to holder of water right in critical ground water area in exchange for holder
reducing appropriation of ground water.  Requires department to monitor ground water level changes
in pilot program area.
Requires department  to annually report on pilot program to interim committee of Legislative
Assembly.
Sunsets pilot program January 2, 2025.
Sunsets reporting requirement January 2, 2026.
Appropriates moneys to department for biennium  beginning July 1, 2019, for grants to implement
and operate pilot program.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to a  Water Resources Department pilot program for ground water conservation; and de-
claring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Water Resources Department shall establish a pilot program  to de-
termine the feasibility of making Columbia River water available in critical  ground water
areas of the Umatilla River  Basin to reduce depletion of, and allow recovery of, underground
aquifers.
(2) To the extent practicable, the department shall make Columbia River water available
for the pilot program by promoting, expediting and providing support  and assistance for
conservation projects and  efforts by holders of existing Columbia River water rights.
Notwithstanding ORS 537.470 (3), any conserved water that is allocated to the state shall, for
the duration of the pilot program, be  used solely for pilot program purposes. A holder of an
existing Columbia River water right  may lease conserved water described in this subsection
that is allocated to the holder under ORS 537.470 to the department for a period that  does
not exceed the duration of the pilot program.  The leasing of conserved water to the depart-
ment under this section is a  beneficial use of the water by the holder and may not be con-
sidered nonuse for purposes of abandonment or forfeiture of the water right.
(3) The department shall identify the critical ground water areas of the  Umatilla River
Basin that are accessible by existing water pumping infrastructure. The  department shall
offer to provide holders of water rights in accessible critical  ground water areas with
Columbia River water conserved under subsection (2) of this section in  exchange for the
holders appropriating a reduced amount of ground water under the water rights. The de-
partment may not require a holder  of a water right in a critical ground water area to par-
ticipate in the program or require that participating holder to reduce appropriation of ground
water by an amount that exceeds the amount of Columbia River water supplied to the  holder

SECTION 2. The Water Resources Department shall report to an  interim committee of
the Legislative Assembly relating to natural resources, in the manner provided under ORS
192.245, no later than September 15th each year  regarding the pilot program required under
section 1 of this 2019 Act.
SECTION 3.
Section 1 of this 2019 Act is repealed on January 2, 2025.
SECTION 4. Section 2 of this 2019 Act is repealed on January 2, 2026.
SECTION 5. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Water Resources Department, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, out of  the
General Fund, the amount of $500,000, which may be expended by the department for  grants
to Umatilla County  and Morrow County for the purpose of activities related to the imple-
mentation and operation  of the department pilot program required under section 1 of this
2019 Act.
SECTION 6. This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2019 Act takes effect
July 1, 2019.
