82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2194
Sponsored by Representatives SMITH DB, OWENS (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates  moneys  to State Department of Agriculture, out of General Fund, for purpose of
developing grant program to fund construction of meat rendering plants.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to meat rendering plants; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the State Department of Agriculture, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, out
of the General Fund, the amount of $4,000,000, for the purpose  of developing a grant program
to fund the construction of meat rendering plants.
SECTION 2.
This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2023 Act takes effect
on its passage.
