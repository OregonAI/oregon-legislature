79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 143
Sponsored by Senators MONNES ANDERSON, BOQUIST (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Department of Veterans’ Affairs to develop and implement one or more programs  state-
wide to establish campus veteran resource centers and campus veteran resource coordinators, or
expand and enhance existing centers and coordinators, on campuses of  community colleges and
public universities to help veterans successfully transition from military service to college life, suc-
ceed in college, complete educational goals and transition  from college to workforce and community.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Department of Veterans’ Affairs for purpose of  im-
plementing programs.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2017.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to provision of campus veteran resources; and declaring an emergency.
Whereas in fiscal year 2015, expenditures by the United  States Department of Veterans Affairs
for Oregon veterans and their families totaled $2.6 billion; and
Whereas in fiscal year 2015, almost 13,500 Oregon veterans received more than $145.9  million
in education benefits from the United States Department of  Veterans Affairs that included financial
resources in support of attending community colleges, universities  and certification and training
programs; and
Whereas one of the priorities in  Oregon’s Higher Education Coordinating Commission 2016-2020
Strategic Plan is to focus on outcomes that promote life successes; and
Whereas the purpose  of a campus veterans program is to provide resources to help veterans
transition from the military into the community college or  university experience, complete their
certificate or degree program and transition into the workforce and community; and
Whereas there are currently campus veteran  resource centers and campus veteran coordinators
at five community colleges and universities in Oregon  that provide a place for veterans to connect
with other veterans,  study, network, connect with local, state, county and federal resources and
obtain help and support from campus veteran coordinators; and
Whereas a strong veterans program on a community college or university campus can help at-
tract  and  retain veterans and provide assistance to complete their education, which will result in
increasing and retaining federal educational funds for veterans; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used in this section:
(a) “Community college” has the meaning given that term in ORS 341.005.
(b) “Public university” has the meaning given that term in ORS 352.002.
(c)(A) “Veteran” has the meaning given that term in ORS 408.225.
(B) “Veteran” includes a “disabled veteran” as defined in ORS 408.225.
(2) The Department of Veterans’ Affairs shall develop and implement one or more  pro-
grams statewide to  establish or expand campus veteran resource centers and campus vet-

The purpose of the programs is to help  veterans successfully transition from military service

(3) The department shall implement programs  under this section by awarding grants to
community colleges or public universities that:
(a) Establish new, or expand and enhance existing, campus veteran resource centers on
campus premises;
(b) Recruit and employ campus veteran resource coordinators who  can serve as liaisons
to provide advocacy, understanding and resource connections for veterans;
(c) Attract veterans to enroll in and attend educational programs  at community colleges
and public universities;
(d) Provide assistance, guidance and support to veterans in completing educational goals
and objectives;
(e) Provide resources to college administration, faculty and staff  to facilitate an under-
standing and appreciation of the strengths, unique challenges and needs of veterans and their
families;
(f) Refer  campus veterans to local county veterans’ service officers appointed under ORS
408.410 who assist veterans in obtaining federal and state veteran benefits;
(g) Assist veterans in successfully transitioning to work and community life  by connect-
ing veterans with workforce and employment resources; and
(h) Provide resources and matching funds in an amount to be determined by the depart-
ment.
(4) The department shall provide two types of grants to community colleges  and public
universities under this section as follows:
(a) Grants to campuses with existing campus veteran resource  centers and campus vet-
eran resource coordinators for expansion and enhancement including, but not limited to,
training for  campus veteran coordinators, computer and other equipment and supplies, ad-
ditional staff, veteran events,  access to workforce and community resources that were not
previously available and meeting other identified needs for the successful  and continued op-
eration of the existing centers and coordinators; and
(b) Grants to campuses that do not currently have campus veteran resource centers  or
campus veteran resource coordinators to establish new centers  and recruit and employ co-
ordinators.
(5) In addition to awarding grants, the department shall:
(a) Create opportunities for campus veteran resource coordinators to network  with each
other to share best practices, resources and other relevant information;
(b) Provide training to campus veteran resource coordinators on specific topics that  will
benefit veterans before, during and after college life; and
(c) Provide technical  assistance in establishing and maintaining centers and coordinators
on campuses.
(6) Services and assistance provided through the campus  veteran resource centers and
campus veteran resource coordinators may not duplicate services provided  by county
veterans’ service officers appointed under ORS 408.410, as described  in ORS 406.450, but may
provide additional information and aid that is not available  through county veterans’ service
