Sec. 6.  (1) The Oregon Department of Administrative Services shall establish a  process for al-
locating available moneys in the Administrative  Services Economic Development Fund on a quar-
terly basis to fulfill the biennial allocations made by sections 1 to  13 [of this 2021 Act ], chapter 661,
Oregon Laws 2021. The department shall set the quarterly allocations.
(2) If, in  any quarter, the moneys transferred from the State Lottery Fund to the Administrative
Services Economic Development Fund are  insufficient to pay for the quarterly allocations made by
sections 1 to 13 [ of this 2021 Act ], chapter 661,  Oregon Laws 2021,  or any other 2021 Act, prior
to making any other allocations pursuant to sections  1 to 13 [ of this 2021 Act ], chapter 661, Oregon
Laws 2021, the department shall satisfy lottery bond debt service obligations in the  amount of
[$278,511,961] $273,885,133 for outstanding lottery bonds.
