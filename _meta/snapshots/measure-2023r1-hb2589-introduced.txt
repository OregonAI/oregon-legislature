82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2589
Sponsored by Representatives MORGAN, GOODWIN (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires Department of Human Services to determine  staffing levels needed to complete as-
sessments within 20 days for individuals seeking enrollment  in programs for all-inclusive care for
elderly, and to submit agency request budget each biennium that  reflects resources needed to meet
determined staffing levels.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to  department for hiring 15 additional full-time staff to
conduct  assessments.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to programs of all-inclusive care for the elderly; and declaring an emergency.
Whereas as  a result of insufficient staffing levels, employees of the Department of Human Ser-
vices who conduct assessments for individuals seeking enrollment in programs of all-inclusive  care
for the elderly (PACE  programs) are overworked and are taking, on average, 40 to 45 days to com-
plete the assessments; and
Whereas as a result of the delays in completing assessments, medically fragile individuals seek-
ing enrollment in PACE programs are denied needed intensive care and wrap-around services  and,
while the assessments are pending, cannot be discharged from hospitals and skilled nursing facilities;
and
Whereas the delays  in discharging medically fragile individuals from hospitals and skilled nurs-
ing facilities has limited the capacity of hospitals  and skilled nursing facilities to accept new pa-
tients; and
Whereas adding more staff to conduct assessments for PACE programs will  shorten the pro-
cessing times for enrollment in  PACE programs benefiting PACE program participants and individ-
uals in need of inpatient hospital and skilled nursing facility care; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used in this section:
(a) “Assessment” means an assessment of an individual’s  health status and a determi-
nation of whether the individual needs the level  of care required for enrollment in a PACE
program.
(b) “PACE program” means a program of all-inclusive care for the elderly as  described
in 42 U.S.C. 1396u-4.
(2) Each biennium, the Department of Human Services  shall determine the number of
full-time staff needed to ensure that each assessment can be  completed no later than 20 days
after the date the assessment is requested.

Administrative Services in accordance with  ORS 291.201 to 291.222, shall include a request for
