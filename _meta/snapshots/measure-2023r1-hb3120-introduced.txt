82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 3120
Sponsored by Representative LIVELY
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires Higher Education Coordinating Commission to conduct  study to determine best method
for connecting public higher education to workforce and economic development needs  of Oregon.
Directs  commission  to submit findings to interim committees of Legislative Assembly related to
higher education not later than September 15, 2024.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to higher education.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Higher Education Coordinating Commission  shall conduct a study to
determine how best to connect public  higher education to the workforce and economic de-
velopment needs of this state. The  commission shall submit a report in the manner provided
by ORS 192.245, and may include recommendations for legislation, to the interim committees
of the Legislative Assembly related to higher education no later than September 15, 2024.
SECTION 2. Section 1 of this 2023 Act is repealed on January 2, 2025.
