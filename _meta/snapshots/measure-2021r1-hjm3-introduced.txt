81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
House Joint Memorial 3
Sponsored by Representatives SMITH DB, LEWIS, OWENS (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Urges Congress to pass and President to sign  Emergency Wildfire and Public Safety Act of 2020.
JOINT MEMORIAL
To the President of the United States and the Senate and the House of Representatives of the United
States of America, in Congress assembled:
We, your memorialists, the Eighty-first Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon, in legisla-
tive session assembled, respectfully represent as follows:
Whereas 2020 has been a year of unprecedented wildfires throughout the western United  States,
including in Oregon; and
Whereas the 2020 Oregon wildfire season has been the most destructive season on record; and
Whereas Oregon wildfires killed at  least 11 people, burned more than 1 million acres and de-
stroyed thousands of homes; and
Whereas the pattern and  magnitude of wildfire-induced destruction in Oregon was repeated in
California and throughout the western United States, where a total of more than  6 million acres
have burned; and
Whereas backlogs in adequate forest management coupled with drier,  hotter conditions, have
resulted in unhealthy, overly dense forests; and
Whereas when fires inevitably occur, current forest conditions result in  larger, more cat-
astrophic fires that are difficult to control, destructive to both urban and rural communities and
pose great threats to both private property and human life; and
Whereas the bipartisan Emergency Wildlife and Public Safety Act of  2020 (S.4431) has been in-
troduced in the United States Senate  to begin to address the catastrophic wildfire conditions that
persist in Oregon and other western states; and
Whereas the Emergency Wildfire and Public Safety Act of 2020 is the  result of over one year
of extensive deliberation, stakeholder engagement and outreach across party  lines to ensure a bi-
partisan, pragmatic approach to reduce the risk of wildfire; and
Whereas the  Emergency Wildfire and Public Safety Act of 2020 will provide federal agencies
with new tools to reduce hazardous  fuels, protect communities, support forestry jobs, biomass de-
velopment and smarter energy practices; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of the Eighty-first Legislative  Assembly, urge the Congress of the United
States to pass and the President of the United States to  sign the Emergency Wildfire and Public
Safety Act of 2020; and be it further
