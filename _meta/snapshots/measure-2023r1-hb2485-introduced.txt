82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2485
Sponsored by Representative NOSSE (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires Portland State University, subject to sufficient moneys being  appropriated, to enhance
state’s capacity to educate public mental  health and addiction treatment providers by July 1, 2028,
by materially increasing number of graduates from relevant degree programs.
Appropriates moneys to Higher Education Coordinating Commission for allocation  to Portland
State University.
Declares emergency, effective on July 1, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to Portland State University; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) Subject to the appropriation of sufficient moneys by the  Legislative As-
sembly, by July 1, 2028, Portland State  University shall enhance the state’s capacity to edu-
cate public mental health and addiction treatment providers in a self-sustaining manner by:
(a) Materially  increasing the number of individuals who graduate from the university
each year with a bachelor’s degree in social work;
(b) Materially increasing the number of individuals who graduate from the  university
each year with a master’s degree in social work; and
(c) Materially increasing the number of individuals who graduate from the university
each year who are prepared to  provide mental health and addiction treatment and become
certified alcohol and drug counselors.
(2) Portland State University shall  accomplish the increase of graduates described in
subsection (1) of this section by:
(a) Increasing the  number of student scholarships in order to reduce or eliminate student
debt for students who graduate with social work degrees;
(b) Increasing the number of faculty, university  staff and field staff supporting students
who are seeking social work degrees;
(c) Awarding stipends to  students who are seeking social work degrees and who are
working with community organizations; and
(d) Any other method determined by Portland  State University to be necessary to en-
hance the state’s capacity to educate public mental health and addiction  treatment providers
in the manner described in subsection (1) of this section.
SECTION 2. Section 1 of this 2023 Act is repealed on July 2, 2029.
SECTION 3. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Higher Education Coordinating Commission, for the biennium beginning  July 1,
2023, out of the General Fund, the amount of $2,783,000, to be allocated  to Portland State
University for the purposes set forth in section 1 of this 2023 Act.
