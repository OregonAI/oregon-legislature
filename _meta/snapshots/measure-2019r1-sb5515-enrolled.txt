AN ACT
Relating to the financial administration of the Department of Justice; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There are appropriated  to the Department of Justice, for the biennium be-
ginning July 1, 2019, out of the General Fund, the following amounts, for the following pur-
poses:
(1) Office of the Attorney General
and Administration ................... $ 744,939
(2) Appellate Division ..................... $ 350,507
(3) Criminal Justice Division.......... $ 14,940,476
(4) Crime Victim and Survivor
Services..................................... $ 14,341,791
(5) Defense of Criminal
Convictions................................ $ 27,892,449
(6) Division of Child Support .......... $ 36,352,778
(7) Child Support Enforcement
Automated System .................... $ 2,137,146
(8) Debt service and related
costs .......................................... $ 14,165,382

of expenses from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts, but  ex-
cluding lottery  funds and federal funds, collected or received by the Department of Justice,
for the following purposes:
(1) Office of the Attorney

(7) Trial Division ............................ $ 39,042,838

are established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, as the maximum limits for payment

following purposes:
(1) Civil Enforcement Division ....... $ 4,992,035

(3) Crime Victim and Survivor

(4) Division of Child Support .......... $119,535,778
