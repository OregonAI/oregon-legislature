79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 730
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs State Court Administrator to study  most common types of lawsuits brought in Oregon
courts and report to appropriate committee or  interim committee of Legislative Assembly no later
than September 1, 2018.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to lawsuits.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The State Court Administrator shall study the most common types of
lawsuits brought in the Oregon courts and report to an appropriate committee or  interim
committee of the Legislative Assembly no later than September 1, 2018.
