SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act gives money to the DOJ  to fund various programs that help victims of crime.
The Act goes into effect when the Governor signs it. (Flesch Readability Score: 79.7).
Appropriates moneys to the Department of Justice to fund  certain programs that assist victims
of crime.
Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to victims; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Department of Justice,  for the biennium ending June 30, 2025, out of the General
Fund, the amount of $ , to assist  victims of domestic violence and sexual assault
with housing needs.
SECTION 2. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Department of Justice,  for the biennium ending June 30, 2025, out of the General
Fund, the amount of  $ , for the Crime Victim and Survivor Services Division, for
distribution to eligible children’s advocacy centers under ORS 418.746 to 418.796.
SECTION 3. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Department of Justice,  for the biennium ending June 30, 2025, out of the General
Fund, the amount of $ , for deposit in the Oregon Domestic and  Sexual Violence
Services Fund established under ORS 147.453.
SECTION 4. This 2024 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2024 Act takes effect
on its passage.
