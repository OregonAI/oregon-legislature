82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2024 Regular Session
House Concurrent Resolution 201
Sponsored by Representative SCHARF, Senator BOQUIST (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Honors the birth  and growth of the City of Dallas, Oregon, for its 150th birthday. (Flesch
Readability Score: 61.8).
Commemorates the sesquicentennial anniversary of the City of Dallas, Oregon.
CONCURRENT RESOLUTION
Whereas the settlement that became known as Dallas,  Oregon, was first settled by pioneers on
the north side of Rickreall Creek in the 1840s, and was then known as Cynthian or Cynthiana; and
Whereas the town was renamed Dallas in 1852  as the result of a petition seeking to honor for-
mer Vice President of the United States George Mifflin Dallas; and
Whereas Dallas was  relocated to the south side of Rickreall Creek, where water was more
plentiful, around 1855; and
Whereas in 1865, businesses in the Dallas  area consisted of a saw and planing mill, a flour mill,
a saddle tree maker, six or seven general  stores and the prosperous woolen mill at nearby Ellendale;
and
Whereas in 1874, Dallas was incorporated as the  Town of Dallas, and its first form of govern-
ment was established with a five-member board of trustees; and
Whereas at the time of its incorporation, the population of Dallas was less than 600 residents,
and it has since grown to nearly 18,000; and
Whereas Dallas’ volunteer fire department was  established in 1878, and it has been a critical
part of the Dallas community ever since; and
Whereas in 1880, the narrow-gauge Oregonian Railway arrived in Dallas; and
Whereas in 1901, Dallas was incorporated as the City of Dallas and adopted a council  form of
government, with mayors serving two-year terms; and
Whereas Dallas is the county seat of Polk County and  is the 19th-oldest incorporated city in
Oregon; and
Whereas on  Saturday, February 17, 2024, the City of Dallas began a yearlong commemoration
of Dallas’ significant  place in Oregon history by hosting a city-wide celebration featuring a parade,
a birthday cake and fun activities for all; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly, recognize and honor the  City
of Dallas on the  150th anniversary of its founding, and we celebrate its significant contributions to
the State of Oregon.
