83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Senate Bill 1027
Sponsored by Senator SMITH DB
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act directs JLAC  to audit the SOS to assess election integrity in this state. (Flesch
Readability  Score: 61.8).
Directs the Joint  Legislative Audit Committee to cause an audit of the Secretary of State
Elections Division to be performed to assess the  integrity of elections in Oregon. Establishes dates
by which the audit is to commence and audit findings are to be reported to the committee.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to election integrity audits  of the Secretary of State Elections Division; and prescribing an
effective  date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The  Joint Legislative Audit Committee shall cause to be performed an
audit of  the Secretary of State Elections Division to assess the integrity of elections con-
ducted in Oregon.
(2) The audit shall be commenced no later than , 2025.
(3) The Legislative Fiscal Officer may hire individuals with appropriate  audit and
elections experience to assist the committee in the performance of the audit.
(4) Findings of the audit shall be presented to the committee no  later than
, 2026.
SECTION 2. This 2025 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on which the 2025
regular session of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
