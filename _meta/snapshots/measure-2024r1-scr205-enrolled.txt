82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2024 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Concurrent Resolution 205
Sponsored by Senators WAGNER, LIEBER;  Senators JAMA, TAYLOR, Representative HOLVEY
(Presession filed.)
Whereas Dick Springer was born in  Portland, Oregon, on January 25, 1948, to Joshua “Sam”
and Vera Springer; and
Whereas Dick Springer first stepped into the  world of politics when United States Senator
Maurine Neuberger made him a Senate page as a high school sophomore in 1964, as the Senate de-
bated what eventually became the Civil Rights Act of 1964; and
Whereas Dick Springer graduated from Cleveland High School  in Portland in 1966, earned a
bachelor’s  degree  in politics from Princeton University in 1970 and was an officer in the Reserve
Officer Training Corps; and
Whereas as a lieutenant in the United States Navy for four years, Dick Springer served on  the
aircraft carrier U.S.S. Coral Sea, stationed off the coast of Vietnam; and
Whereas after his discharge from  the Navy, Dick Springer began law school at the University
of Oregon in 1974 and earned his law degree in 1978; and
Whereas Dick Springer was a deputy district attorney in Multnomah County  before joining the
law firm Pozzi Wilson Atchison in Portland, where he worked for 17 years; and
Whereas in 1980, Dick Springer won the first of  four terms in the Oregon House of Represen-
tatives from a Portland district, rising to the chair of the House Judiciary Committee; and
Whereas Dick Springer held a seat on  the board of Portland Community College from 1985 to
1988; and
Whereas in 1988, Dick Springer won the first of two terms in the Oregon Senate; and
Whereas in 1993, after the Democratic party lost  four seats and lessened their majority from 20
to 16, Dick Springer became Senate Majority Leader; and
Whereas Dick Springer was a champion of  the environment who served on the board of Friends
of the Columbia Gorge and worked for the  better part of a decade, until his retirement in 2015, on
environmental issues as District  Manager for the West Multnomah Soil and Water Conservation
District; and
Whereas Dick Springer passed away on Sunday, April 9, 2023; and
Whereas Dick Springer is survived  by his companion of 20 years, Jan Coulton; his former wife,
Diane Linn; two children, Josh and Tess; and two grandchildren, Madeline and Miles Springer; now,
therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly, celebrate  the life and accom-
plishments of Dick Springer, and we recognize and honor  his contributions to the people of this
state; and be it further
Resolved, That a copy of this resolution shall be  presented to the family of Dick Springer as an
expression of our sympathy and condolences.
