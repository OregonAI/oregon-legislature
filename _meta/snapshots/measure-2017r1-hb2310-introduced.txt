79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 2310
Introduced and printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the request of  Governor Kate Brown for
Oregon Health Authority)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Modifies provisions regarding schedule by which local public health authorities must submit lo-
cal plans for applying foundational public health capabilities and implementing  foundational public
health programs.
Directs  Oregon  Public Health Advisory Board to establish accountability metrics for purpose
of evaluating progress in achieving statewide public health goals.
Establishes process by which governing body  of county may transfer to state duties of county
with respect to public health laws of state.
Permits local public health authorities to enter into agreement with Oregon Health  Authority
under which  local public health authority receives state funding for purpose of applying foundational
capabilities and implementing foundational programs.
Authorizes Oregon Health Authority to establish fees for providing public health data for  public
health purposes.
Becomes operative January 1, 2018.
Takes effect on 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the provision of public health services; creating new provisions; amending ORS  431.115,
431.123, 431.132, 431.148, 431.380, 431.382 and 431.417 and section 115, chapter  736, Oregon Laws
2015; and prescribing an effective date.

431.382. [If the Oregon Health  Authority fails to distribute an amount of moneys to a local public

health authority under ORS 431.001 to 431.550 and 431.990 and the duties  of a local public
health authority under the other public health  laws of the state, the governing body of a
county must adopt an ordinance that transfers the responsibility after fulfilling the require-
ments of subsection (2) of this section. An ordinance adopted under  this section may not
transfer part of the duties of a local public health authority under ORS  431.001 to 431.550 and
431.990 or part of the duties of  a local public health authority under the other public health
laws of the state. An ordinance adopted under this section must  transfer all of the duties
of a local public health authority under ORS 431.001 to 431.550  and 431.990 and all of the du-
ties of a local public health authority under the other public health laws of the state.
(2) Before adopting an ordinance under subsection (1)  of this section, the governing body
of a county must, not less than 180 days before adopting the ordinance:
(a) Provide written notice to each household where residents of the county reside, ex-
plaining the reason for the transfer and the consequences of the  transfer and explaining the
way in which a resident may provide public comment about the transfer to the  governing
body of the county; and
(b) Provide the Oregon Health Authority, in a form and manner prescribed by the au-
thority, with written notice of the intent to adopt the ordinance.
(3) If the governing  body of a county adopts an ordinance under this section, the gov-
erning body must provide the Oregon Health Authority, in a form  and manner prescribed by
the authority, with the text of the ordinance.
(4) An ordinance  adopted under this section may not take effect sooner than 180 days
after the date on which the governing body of the county adopts the ordinance.
(5) If the governing body of a county adopts an ordinance  under this section, the local
public health authority that provides  public health services for the county is not eligible to
receive any funds under ORS 431.380.

FEES FOR PROVISION OF DATA
SECTION 9. Section 10 of this 2017 Act  is added to and made a part of ORS 431.001 to
431.550.
SECTION 10. For purposes of fulfilling the duties and functions  and executing the powers
conferred on the Oregon Health Authority by ORS 431.001 to 431.550 and 431.990, the  Oregon
Health Authority may  by rule establish fees for providing data to academic institutions and
nongovernmental entities that request the data for public health purposes.  Fees established
under this section must be reasonably calculated to pay the cost of providing the data.
