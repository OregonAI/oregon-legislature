79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 2763
Sponsored by  Representatives WHISNANT, SMITH WARNER, Senator KNOPP, Representative HACK, Senator
DEMBROW; Representative MCLAIN
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Establishes Accomplished Teaching  Program within Department of Education to encourage and
assist teachers in earning national board certification. Establishes Accomplished Teaching Program
Fund.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2017.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to teachers; and declaring an emergency.
Whereas teachers play a critical  role in preparing students to achieve the high academic stan-
dards expected under the Oregon Educational Act for the 21st Century; and
Whereas it is important to identify and reward  teaching excellence and to encourage good
teachers to become excellent teachers; and
Whereas the National Board for Professional  Teaching Standards was created to improve the
quality of teaching; and
Whereas the National Board for Professional Teaching Standards has established high and  rig-
orous standards for accomplished teaching  and has developed a national voluntary system for as-
sessing and  certifying teachers who demonstrate teaching excellence by meeting those standards;
and
Whereas this voluntary, advanced certification does not replace Oregon teacher licensing; and
Whereas 20 years  of research has shown that students taught by teachers who hold national
board certification have improved the learning  outcomes of students, especially minority and low-
income students; and
Whereas currently only one percent  of the licensed teachers in this state have achieved national
board certification; and
Whereas this state should aspire to having at least 10 percent of its licensed teachers achieve
national board certification; and
Whereas it is the intent of the Legislative Assembly to provide incentives  for teachers to seek
national board certification and to  reward teachers who demonstrate teaching excellence by at-
taining national board certification and sharing their expertise with other teachers; and
Whereas it is the intent of  the Legislative Assembly to increase the number of national board
certified teachers in this state to five percent of all public school teachers by 2022; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Accomplished Teaching Program is established within the  Depart-
ment of Education.
(2) The department shall distribute moneys under this section for the  purpose of en-
couraging teaching excellence in the public schools of this state by:

(ii) Participate in the national board  certification program during the school year for

(A) Completes the certification program but fails to earn national board certification and
meets the one-year teaching requirement; or

(i) Be considered to have consented, as a condition of employment of the  state or any

within 60 days of service of a notice of  default. The department may not require the person
to pay more than 10 percent of the person’s wages or  salary per pay period under the re-
payment schedule.
(B) If a person fails  to establish a repayment schedule or fails to comply with a repay-
ment schedule, the person has breached an essential condition of employment  and is subject
to involuntary withholding of wages or salary for the repayment of the subsidy.
(C) A person who is employed by the state or any  of its political subdivisions may not
be dismissed for having defaulted on the repayment of the subsidy.

(5)(a) The department shall provide  a teacher with $160 to be used toward the costs of

are for teachers interested in pursuing national board certification.
(b) Incentives provided under this subsection may not be duplicated  at any time for any

(6)(a) The  department shall distribute to each school district or public charter school an
amount that allows the district or school to provide to a teacher:
(A) An annual bonus of $5,000, if the teacher holds national board  certification and sat-

(B) An additional annual bonus  of $5,000, if the teacher teaches in a school that is con-

SECTION 2.
(1) The Accomplished Teaching Program Fund is established in the  State
Treasury, separate and  distinct from the General Fund. Interest earned on moneys in the
Accomplished Teaching Program Fund shall be credited to the fund.
(2) The Department of Education may accept from any source any  grant, donation or gift
of money or other valuable item made to the department for purposes of the fund.
(3) Moneys credited to the fund are continuously appropriated to the department for  the
purposes of the Accomplished Teaching Program established under section  1 of this 2017 Act.
SECTION 3. No later than  April 1, 2020, the Department of Education shall submit a re-
port to  the legislative committees of the Legislative Assembly related to education. The re-
port must:
(1) Summarize the effects on the educational outcomes of  this state as a result of the
increased number of teachers who hold national board certification;
(2) Summarize  the progress of reaching the goal of increasing the number of teachers
who hold national board certification in this state to five percent of all public school teachers
by 2022; and
(3) Identify the level of funding necessary  to increase the number of teachers who hold
national board certification in this state to 10 percent of all public school teachers.
SECTION 4. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Department of Education, for the biennium beginning July  1, 2017, out of the
General Fund, the  amount of $1,200,000, for the Accomplished Teaching Program Fund es-
tablished under section 2 of this 2017 Act.
SECTION 5. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2017 Act takes effect
July 1, 2017.
