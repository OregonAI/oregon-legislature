79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 320
Sponsored by Senator OLSEN (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Oregon Military Department for Oregon  Military
Museum.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2017.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to state financial administration; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There is appropriated to  the Oregon Military Department, for the biennium
beginning July 1, 2017, out of the General Fund, the amount of $3,500,000 for:
(1) Creation of exhibits at the Oregon Military Museum; and
(2) Capital expenditures directly related to the establishment and maintenance of the
museum.
SECTION 2.
This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2017 Act takes effect
July 1, 2017.
