Extends the sunset for the tax credit for pension income.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to tax credits for pension income; amending section 36, chapter 913, Oregon Laws 2009.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
 Section 36, chapter 913, Oregon Laws 2009, as amended by  section 9, chapter 750,
Oregon Laws 2013, and section 27, chapter 579, Oregon Laws 2019, is amended to read:
Sec. 36.  A credit may not be claimed under  ORS 316.157 for tax years beginning on or after
January 1, [ 2026] 2032.
