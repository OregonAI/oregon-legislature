80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
Senate Bill 292
Sponsored by Senator HANSELL (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Declares state policy regarding fighting of wildfires on unprotected land.
Authorizes State Fire Marshal to enter into agreements with public  and private entities for use
as needed of equipment capable of utilization for  fighting wildfires on unprotected land. Authorizes
State Fire Marshal to dispatch equipment under agreement as  State Fire Marshal deems appropri-
ate. Authorizes Governor,  upon recommendation of State Fire Marshal, to dispatch available public
or private resources of any type for  purpose of assisting persons fighting wildfire on unprotected
land.
Creates Wildfire Resource Fund. Appropriates fund  moneys for use by State Fire Marshal in
providing equipment or firefighters to assist persons fighting wildfire on unprotected land.
Appropriates moneys to Wildfire Resource Fund for biennium beginning July 1, 2019.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to wildfires; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
It is the policy of this state that:
(1) The state is responsible for fighting wildfires on unprotected lands;
(2) The State Fire Marshal  is the appropriate party to recommend to the Governor the
resources that can best assist local personnel in fighting wildfires  on unprotected lands; and
(3) The Governor should, to the  extent practicable, dispatch any resources recommended
by the State Fire Marshal for fighting wildfires on unprotected lands.
SECTION 2.
(1) The State Fire Marshal  may enter into agreements with state and federal
agencies and other public or  private sources for the availability, as needed, of equipment that
can be utilized for fighting wildfires. The equipment may include, but  need not be limited to,
aircraft and earthmoving equipment.
(2) The State Fire  Marshal may order the dispatching of any equipment described in an
agreement under subsection (1)  of this section that the State Fire Marshal deems appropri-
ate for use in assisting local  personnel who are fighting a wildfire on unprotected land. In
addition to any authority of the Governor under ORS 476.510 to 476.610 and 477.190  and
477.200, upon recommendation  by the State Fire Marshal, the Governor may dispatch avail-
able public or private resources of any type to assist local personnel who are  fighting a
wildfire on unprotected land.
SECTION 3. The Wildfire Resource Fund is established in the State Treasury, separate
and distinct from the General Fund. Interest earned by the Wildfire Resource Fund shall
be credited to the fund. Moneys in the fund  are continuously appropriated to the State Fire
Marshal for the purpose of dispatching equipment or firefighters as  the State Fire Marshal
deems appropriate to assist local personnel in the fighting of wildfires on unprotected  lands.
