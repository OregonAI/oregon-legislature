80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
Senate Bill 1049
Sponsored by Senator COURTNEY, Representative KOTEK
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Public Employees Retirement Board to study issues relating to Public  Employees Re-
tirement System and  report to appropriate committee or interim committee of Legislative Assembly
no later than September 15, 2020.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to public employee retirement.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Public Employees Retirement Board shall study  issues relating to the
Public Employees Retirement System and report to an appropriate committee or  interim
committee of the Legislative Assembly no later than September 15, 2020.
