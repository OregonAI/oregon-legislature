80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 3235
Sponsored by Representative HELT
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Establishes School Support Fund consisting  of moneys transferred from Oregon Marijuana Ac-
count. Directs  Department of Education to use moneys in fund for programming to prevent bullying,
substance abuse, violence in schools and suicide.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to school funding; creating new provisions; and amending ORS 475B.759.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The  School Support Fund is established in the State Treasury, separate
and distinct from the General Fund. Interest earned by the School Support  Fund shall be
credited to  the fund. The School Support Fund shall consist of moneys transferred from the
Oregon Marijuana Account established under ORS 475B.759. Moneys in the fund are  contin-
uously appropriated to the Department of Education for  the purposes described in subsection
(2) of this section.
(2) The Department of Education shall use the moneys in the fund to:
(a) Develop  programming designed to prevent bullying, substance abuse, violence in
schools and suicide;
(b) Work collaboratively with community partners engaged in the  prevention of bullying,
substance abuse, violence in schools and suicide;
(c) Support any existing programming designed  to prevent bullying, substance abuse, vi-
olence in schools and suicide;
(d) Develop and implement an age-appropriate health education curriculum that focuses
on the prevention of bullying, substance abuse, violence in schools and suicide;
(e) Provide  training and resources for school personnel on the implementation of
evidence-based programming designed to encourage healthy school culture; and
(f) Create a community marijuana resource officer position in each school district.
