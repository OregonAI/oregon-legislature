House Bill 3580
Sponsored by Representatives GOMBERG, JAVADI; Representatives ANDERSEN, HELM, Senators ANDERSON,
FREDERICK, GOLDEN, PHAM K, SOLLMAN, TAYLOR, WEBER
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: This Act creates the Task Force  on Eelgrass Resources. (Flesch Readability Score 94.3).
Establishes the Task Force on Eelgrass  Resources. Provides duties of the task force and directs
the task force to submit a report to the Legislative Assembly no later than September 15, 2027.
Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to eelgrass resources in state waters; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Task Force on Eelgrass Resources is established.
(2) The task force consists of at least 19 members who shall be appointed by  the Director
of the Department of Land Conservation and Development as follows:
(a) Two members to represent conservation groups;
(b) Two members to represent the seafood and aquaculture industries;
(c) At least two members to represent academic institutions  and estuarine or eelgrass
researchers;
(d) One member to represent local governments;
(e) One member to represent restoration practitioners;
(f) Two members to represent estuary partnerships, including  the South Slough National
Estuarine Research Reserve;
(g) One member to represent ports;
(h) One member to represent the angler or recreational fishing community;
(i) One member to represent the coastal tourism industry;
(j) One member to represent Oregon Sea Grant;
(k) At least one member to represent the Department of  Land Conservation and Devel-
opment;
(L) At least one member to represent the State Department of Fish and Wildlife;
(m) At least one member to represent the Department of State Lands;
(n) At least one member to represent the Department of Environmental Quality; and
(o) At least one member to represent the State Department of Agriculture.
(3) In addition to the members appointed under subsection (2) of this section:
(a) The Director of the Department of Land Conservation and Development  shall invite
one member from each  of the nine federally recognized Indian tribes in this state to serve
as members of the task force, as willing and able to participate.
(b) The director may appoint up to five additional members with experience in:

SECTION 2.
The Task Force on Eelgrass Resources shall:
(1) Identify relevant data sources and develop a strategy to  conduct an assessment of the
current and historic extent of eelgrass resources in this state’s estuaries.
(2) Develop recommendations for  statewide targets for the protection, conservation and
enhancement of eelgrass resources by  2040, informed by the best available scientific infor-
mation and current and future  predictions of habitat suitability. The targets must be
commensurate with the level of observed and anticipated eelgrass habitat decline  and aim
to achieve net ecological gain of eelgrass resources and habitat function.
(3) Develop actions  and recommendations that the state and local governments should
undertake to maintain existing eelgrass beds and address eelgrass decline.
(4) Examine the existing regulatory framework for eelgrass resource  management across
state agencies, including monitoring, leasing and enforcement authorities, to identify  ineffi-
ciencies and barriers to meeting the conservation and restoration targets  developed under
subsection (2) of this section.
(5) Make policy and regulatory  recommendations to achieve the statewide targets devel-

(12) Solicit recommendations from state  agencies on any other topic related to eelgrass
and estuary management, research,  mapping and monitoring, mitigation, state policy,
enforcement, agency coordination, stressors and restoration.
(13) Develop cost estimates for implementing recommended  actions described in this
section and identify potential sources of funding.
SECTION 3.
In carrying out the duties described in section 2 of this 2025 Act, the  Task
Force on Eelgrass Resources shall:
(1) Consult with communities, industries, businesses and other  stakeholders impacted by
eelgrass habitat loss and the policy proposals and recommendations of the task force.
(2) Consult with federally recognized tribes in this state on the  cultural and ecological
importance of eelgrass meadows.
(3) As far is as practicable, consult  and collaborate with relevant federal government
agencies.
(4) Consult and collaborate with  the Oregon Climate Action Commission and any other
relevant state agencies.
SECTION 4. Sections 1 to 3 of this 2025 Act are repealed on December 31, 2027.
