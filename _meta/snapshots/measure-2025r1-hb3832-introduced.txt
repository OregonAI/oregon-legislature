House Bill 3832
Sponsored by Representative OWENS
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: This Act tells DAS to give a grant to create a collaborative center in  Harney County.
(Flesch Readability Score: 68.9).
Directs the Oregon Department of Administrative Services to provide  a grant to a nonprofit
organization to establish a collaborative center in Harney County.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the creation of a collaborative center; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used in  this section, “collaborative center” means a facility operated
by a nonprofit organization that provides services  to encourage and develop collaborative
problem solving approaches that support  community-based decision-making and build overall
local community capacity.
(2) The Oregon Department of Administrative Services shall award a  grant to a nonprofit
organization for expenses related to establishing and staffing a collaborative center  in
Harney County to support place-based collaborative approaches to addressing  public issues.
Services provided by the collaborative center may include, but need not be limited to:
(a) Opportunities for individuals or organizations to experience and participate in ongoing
collaborative processes involving a variety of industries, including economic and ecological
endeavors, in southeastern Oregon;
(b) Third party facilitation contracting;
(c) Collaborative consultation;
(d) Collaborative process training;
(e) Collaborative mentoring;
(f) Third party monitoring; and
(g) Capacity building to support community-based collaborative efforts.
(3) The operator of the collaborative center shall  consult and coordinate with existing
state-sponsored collaborative programs to:
(a) Avoid duplication of efforts;
(b) Leverage existing resources; and
(c) Ensure that unmet needs for collaborative services in  southeastern Oregon are being
fulfilled.
