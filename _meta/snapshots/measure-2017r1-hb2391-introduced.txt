79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 2391
Introduced and  printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the request of House Interim Committee
on Health Care)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Requires Oregon Health Authority to submit blueprint  for basic health plan to Centers for
Medicare and Medicaid Services by December 31, 2017.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to access to health care; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Oregon Health Authority  shall, no later than December 31, 2017,
submit for certification by  the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services a blueprint for a
basic health plan in accordance with 42 U.S.C. 18051 and 42 C.F.R. part 600.
(2) Immediately after receiving certification of  the blueprint by the Centers for Medicare
and Medicaid Services, the authority shall notify the committees or interim committees re-
lated to health in the House of Representatives and the Senate.
SECTION 2. Section 1 of this 2017 Act is repealed December 31, 2019.
SECTION 3. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2017 Act takes effect
on its passage.
