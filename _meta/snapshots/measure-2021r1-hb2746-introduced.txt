81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
House Bill 2746
Sponsored by Representative NOBLE (at the request of Diana Gutman) (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs State Court Administer to develop and implement Hope Card Program for issuance  of
information cards to persons who are protected by restraining orders.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to protective order information cards.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The State Court Administrator shall develop and implement the Hope
Card Program. The Hope  Card Program shall provide an information card to each person
who is issued a restraining order under ORS 30.866, 107.700 to  107.735, 124.005 to 124.040,
163.730 to 163.750 or 163.760 to 163.777.
(2) The information card issued under this section must:
(a) Be a durable, wallet-sized card  containing essential information about the restraining
order, including the identifying information and characteristics of the person subject to the
restraining order, the case number, the issuance and expiration date  of the restraining or-
der, the terms of the restraining order  and the names of any other persons protected by the
restraining order.
(b) Include a statement in substantially the following form:
_______________________________________________________________________________________
If you see the person who you are protected from within your radius,  immediately call 911.
You cannot violate your own restraining order. Do not expect help from strangers.  Present
your case number from the other side of this card to  the 911 dispatcher and the responding
police officer.
_______________________________________________________________________________________
(3) The Chief Justice of  the Supreme Court may adopt rules to carry out the purposes
of this section.
SECTION 2.
The Hope Card Program shall provide the information cards described  in
section 1 of this 2021 Act to  persons who are issued restraining orders on or after the ef-
fective date of this 2021 Act.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
