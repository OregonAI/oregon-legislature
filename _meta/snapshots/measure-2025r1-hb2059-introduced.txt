83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 2059
Sponsored by Representative NOSSE (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act tells OHA to create a unit focused on building new mental health and SUD fa-
cilities. (Flesch Readability Score: 65.1).
Directs the Oregon Health Authority  to establish a unit dedicated to developing sufficient adult
behavioral health facilities to serve the needs of each trauma system area in this state.
Sunsets on January 2, 2031.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to adult behavioral health facilities.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used in this section, “adult behavioral health facility” means:
(a) An inpatient psychiatric facility, including the Oregon State Hospital;
(b) An inpatient psychiatric unit in a hospital;
(c) A residential treatment facility as defined in ORS 443.400;
(d) A secure residential treatment facility as described in ORS 443.465;
(e) A residential treatment home as defined in ORS 443.400;
(f) An adult foster home as defined  in ORS 443.705 that serves individuals with mental
illness;
(g) A residential substance use disorder treatment program, including a withdrawal
management program; and
(h) A crisis facility, as defined by the Oregon Health Authority by rule.
(2) The Oregon Health Authority shall establish  a unit within the authority dedicated to
developing sufficient adult  behavioral health facilities to ensure that each trauma system
area, as  identified by the authority by rule, has the number and types of adult behavioral
health facilities needed to serve the trauma system area’s population.
(3)(a) The unit  established under this section shall develop plans for and provide funding
for the construction and staffing of new adult behavioral health facilities.
(b) In developing plans under this subsection, the unit shall:
(A) Use a phased approach that takes into account the priorities of each trauma  system
area, construction time frames and costs and staffing needs; and
(B) Collaborate with:
(i) Local public health authorities as defined in ORS 431.003;
(ii) Community mental health programs;
(iii) Hospitals;
(iv) Treatment providers at adult behavioral health facilities;
(v) Education service districts;
