CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to state financial administration; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of ORS 291.055 (1)(e), the
following new or increased fees,  adopted by the Oregon Board of Dentistry and approved by
the Oregon Department of Administrative Services, are approved:
(1) Dental therapist
application fee........................... $ 180
(2) Dental therapist biennial
licensure fee.............................. $ 230
SECTION 2. This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist, and  this 2023 Act takes effect
July 1, 2023.
