80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 3170
Sponsored by  Representative MEEK, Senator THOMSEN; Representatives EVANS, WILLIAMS, Senators
BAERTSCHIGER JR, GIROD, HANSELL, HEARD, KNOPP, THATCHER, WINTERS
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates  moneys  from General Fund to Oregon Health Authority for Senior Farm Direct
Nutrition Program and Women, Infants and Children Farm Direct  Nutrition Program. Directs
Oregon Health Authority to include appropriations for programs in biennial agency  request budgets.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to state financial administration; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There is appropriated to the Oregon Health Authority, for the biennium be-
ginning July 1, 2019, out of the General Fund, the amount of  $286,888 for the Senior Farm
Direct Nutrition Program.
SECTION 2. There is appropriated to the Oregon Health Authority, for the biennium be-
ginning July 1, 2019, out of the General Fund, the  amount of $1,227,421 for the Women, In-
fants and Children Farm Direct Nutrition Program.
SECTION 3. In each biennium, the Oregon Health Authority  shall, in its agency request
budget filed under ORS 291.208, request  appropriations to fund the Senior Farm Direct Nu-
trition Program and the Women, Infants and Children Farm Direct Nutrition Program in at
least the amounts of the appropriations made by sections 1 and 2 of this 2019 Act.
SECTION 4.
This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2019 Act takes effect
on July 1, 2019.
