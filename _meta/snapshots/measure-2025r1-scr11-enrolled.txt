Whereas the National Conference of State Legislatures (NCSL) was founded in  1975 and has
evolved during the past half-century to become the  premier organization solely dedicated to serving
state and territorial legislators and legislative staff; and
Whereas NCSL was created from the merger of three organizations that  served or represented
state legislatures and that shared the belief that legislative service is one of democracy’s  worthiest
pursuits; and
Whereas NCSL is a bipartisan organization with three  objectives: to advance the effectiveness,
independence and integrity of state legislatures; to foster interstate  communication and cooperation;
and to ensure states have a strong, cohesive voice in the federal system; and
Whereas state legislatures are America’s laboratories  of democracy, and they have shown
throughout our nation’s great history that they are  the bodies best equipped to tackle the emerging
challenges faced by communities across the nation; and
Whereas state legislatures are where people  from very different backgrounds, representing very
different communities, can come together and find common ground; and
Whereas NCSL has facilitated  the exchange of ideas, provided critical research and information
and encouraged  a rigorous review of the complex issues confronting our communities, states and
nation; and
Whereas NCSL strives to strengthen the bonds  between America’s state legislatures and the
international community; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we,  the members of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly, recognize and honor the Na-
tional Conference of State Legislatures for its  superb leadership and its commitment to the legisla-
tive institution as it celebrates its 50th anniversary in 2025; and be it further
Resolved, That a copy of this resolution shall be presented to the National Conference of State
Legislatures as an expression of our great appreciation  for its contributions to the State of Oregon.
