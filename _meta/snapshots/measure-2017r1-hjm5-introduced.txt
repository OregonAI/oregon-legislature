79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Joint Memorial 5
Sponsored by Representative HOLVEY, Senator BEYER
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Urges Congress to continue to appropriate funds for Hanford Nuclear Reservation  cleanup, with
specific focus on funding for remediation of ground water contamination.
JOINT MEMORIAL
To the President of the United States and the Senate and the House of Representatives of the United
States of America, in Congress assembled:
We, your memorialists, the Seventy-ninth Legislative Assembly of  the State of Oregon, in legis-
lative session assembled, respectfully represent as follows:
Whereas the United States Department  of Energy and its predecessor agencies’ activities
producing plutonium at the Hanford  Nuclear Reservation at Richland, Washington, created large
amounts of high-level radioactive waste; and
Whereas the methods used for handling high-level radioactive waste from the Hanford  Nuclear
Reservation resulted in extensive ground water contamination; and
Whereas some of the contaminated ground water  from the Hanford Nuclear Reservation enters
the Columbia River, causing localized contamination where plumes enter the river; and
Whereas cleanup of the Hanford Nuclear Reservation has been underway since 1989, and  will
likely continue for another 40 years or more; and
Whereas eliminating the flow of contaminants in ground water to the  Columbia River and
eventually cleaning the ground water is one of the most difficult  challenges for the Hanford Nuclear
Reservation cleanup; and
Whereas federal funding for the Hanford Nuclear Reservation cleanup is not keeping pace  with
actions needed to  prevent ground water contamination from reaching the Columbia River; now,
therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
(1) The Congress of the United States is respectfully  urged to continue to appropriate and ex-
pend funds as necessary to support the long-term disposal of high-level radioactive waste at  the
Hanford Nuclear Reservation, with a specific focus on remediation of ground water contamination.
(2) A copy of this memorial shall  be sent to the President of the United States, to the Senate
Majority Leader, to the Speaker of the House of Representatives and to each member of the Oregon
Congressional  Delegation.
