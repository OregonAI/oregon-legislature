80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 2309
Sponsored by Representative REARDON (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Department of Transportation to develop  and implement program to lend moneys to
school districts for incremental costs of purchasing electric-powered school buses.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to electric-powered school buses.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Department of Transportation shall develop and implement a pro-
gram to lend moneys to school districts to aid  in the acquisition of electric-powered school
buses. Under the program:
(a) The department shall enter into agreements with school districts  under which the
department provides loans for the incremental costs of  purchase of electric-powered school
buses. As used in this paragraph, “incremental costs” means the difference between the  cost
of purchasing  a diesel-powered school bus and the cost of purchasing an electric-powered
school bus.
(b) The  department shall make loans from moneys made available for that purpose out
of the Electric Bus Loan Fund established under  section 2 of this 2019 Act. The department
shall deposit moneys repaid under the loans, including interest upon the loans, into  the fund.
(c) The maximum term of a  loan is ______ years. Loans may bear interest at a maximum
rate of ______ percent.
(d) The department shall develop a process for school districts to apply for loans. The
department shall prioritize loans to school districts with  the greatest demonstrated need for
replacement of school buses.
(2) The department may adopt rules as necessary to carry out the  provisions of this
section.
SECTION 2. The Electric Bus Loan Fund  is established in the State Treasury, separate
and distinct  from the General Fund. The Electric Bus Loan Fund consists of moneys from
repayments of loans made under section 1  of this 2019 Act, including interest upon such
loans, and may include moneys appropriated, allocated, deposited  or transferred to the fund
by the Legislative Assembly or otherwise. The moneys in the fund are continuously appro-
priated to the Department of Transportation for the purposes specified in section 1 of this
2019 Act.
