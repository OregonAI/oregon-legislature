CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to local authority over regulated utilities.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) This section applies to the City of Bandon, the City  of North Bend and
the City of Reedsport.
(2) Notwithstanding any provision of the  city’s charter or code to the contrary, fees,
rates or any other charges imposed for municipal water and  wastewater services provided
by the city may be set by a majority vote of the governing body of the city.
