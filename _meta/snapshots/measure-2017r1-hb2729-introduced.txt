79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 2729
Sponsored by Representatives WHISNANT, REARDON, JOHNSON; Representatives HAYDEN, NEARMAN
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires Higher Education Coordinating Commission to work regularly with public  universities
and community colleges to analyze and evaluate effectiveness of previously adopted  open educa-
tional resources and to increase number of open educational resources used in courses.
Appropriates $700,000 from General Fund to fund open educational resources.
Requires Higher Education Coordinating Commission to  report to Legislative Assembly on
amount of moneys open educational resources program has saved students and  on amount of moneys
commission anticipates program will save students in future.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2017.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to open educational resources  at post-secondary institutions of education; and declaring an
emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2017 Act is added to and made a  part of ORS 348.750 to
348.757.
SECTION 2. The Higher Education Coordinating Commission shall work regularly with
public universities listed in ORS 352.002 and community colleges to:
(1) Analyze and evaluate the  effectiveness of the open educational resources that have
been adopted and that are used in courses at public universities listed in ORS  352.002 and
community colleges; and
(2) Increase the number of open educational  resources that are used in courses at public
universities listed in ORS 352.002 and community colleges.
SECTION 3. The Higher Education Coordinating Commission shall report on the  effec-
tiveness of the open educational resources program established in ORS 348.750 to 348.757 to
the committees of  the Legislative Assembly relating to higher education by February 15,
2019. The report shall include:
(1) An evaluation of the amount of moneys students have saved as a result  of the pro-
gram; and
(2) An evaluation of the amount of moneys the commission anticipates future students
will save as a result of the program.
SECTION 4.
There is appropriated to the Higher Education  Coordinating Commission, for
the biennium beginning July 1, 2017, out of the General  Fund, the amount of $700,000 for the
purpose of carrying out the provisions of ORS 348.750 to 348.757  and section 2 of this 2017
Act.
SECTION 5. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2017 Act takes effect
on July 1, 2017.
