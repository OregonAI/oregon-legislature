80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
Senate Bill 941
Sponsored by Senator HEARD,  Representatives EVANS, SMITH DB; Senators PROZANSKI, WAGNER, Represen-
tatives CLEM, DOHERTY, FAHEY, GORSEK, HERNANDEZ, LEIF, SCHOUTEN, WITT
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to  Oregon Department of Administrative Services for
distribution to Curry Health District  for purposes of acquiring ambulance for maternity care and
opening emergency room in Curry Medical Center.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to appropriations for the benefit of Curry Health District; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services,  for the biennium beginning July
1, 2019, out of the General Fund, the amount of $2,000,000, to  be distributed by grant or loan
to Curry Health District for the following purposes:
(1) $1,700,000 to open an emergency room in Curry Medical  Center in Brookings, Oregon;
and
(2) $300,000 to acquire an ambulance that is used exclusively for transport  related to
maternity care.
SECTION 2. This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2019 Act takes effect
July 1, 2019.
