80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2020 Regular Session
House Bill 4018
Introduced and  printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the request of House Interim Committee
on Rules for Representaive Tina Kotek)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Oregon Department of Administrative Services to submit report on  state budgetary
procedures to Joint Interim Committee on Ways and Means.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to state finance; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
No later than December  1, 2020, the Oregon Department of Administrative
Services shall submit a report to the Joint Interim  Committee on Ways and Means regarding
procedures for developing the biennial state budget. The  report may include recommen-
dations for legislative changes.
SECTION 2. This 2020 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2020 Act takes effect
on its passage.
