83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Senate Bill 768
Sponsored by Senator NASH, Representative LEVY B (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act tells the state to match each donation that  is at least $10,000 to the OSU
Foundation for ag  research. The Act requires the match to be 50% of each specified donation if
money allows for it. (Flesch Readability Score: 60.6).
Requires the State of Oregon to contribute a 50 percent match for every donation  of $10,000 or
more made to the Oregon State  University Foundation for the express purpose of supporting agri-
cultural research with emphasis on cereal grains, to the extent moneys are available.
Appropriates moneys to the Higher Education  Coordinating Commission for allocation to the
Oregon State University Foundation.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to agricultural research.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) To support essential agricultural research, for every donation of $10,000
or more that is made directly to the Oregon State  University Foundation for the express
purpose of supporting agricultural research  with an emphasis on cereal grains, the State of
Oregon shall, to the  extent moneys are available, separately contribute 50 percent of the
amount of the donation to the foundation. The total amount of moneys contributed by  the
State of Oregon under this section may not exceed $3,000,000 per biennium.
(2) An individual or  entity may make a direct donation of $10,000 or more to the Oregon
State University Foundation for the purposes of  this section through a series of donations.
The State  of Oregon may not make a 50 percent matching contribution under subsection (1)
of this section until the total amount of moneys donated by a specific individual  or entity to
the foundation is $10,000 or more.
(3)(a) The Higher Education Coordinating Commission shall enter  into an agreement with
the Oregon State University Foundation to establish:
(A) The method and  timing by which the foundation shall inform the commission of the
total number and amount of  donations that qualify for a state match under this section; and
(B) Methods for the commission to ensure  the accuracy of information reported under
subparagraph (A) of this paragraph.
(b) The commission may adopt rules to implement this subsection.
