80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2020 Regular Session
House Bill 4168
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON RULES (at the request of Governor Kate Brown)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Adds statutes regarding woody biomass to chapter regarding forestry administration.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to forestry.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
ORS 526.277 and 526.280 are added to and made a part of ORS chapter 526.
