80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 5042
Introduced and printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the  request of Oregon Department of
Administrative Services)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Limits biennial expenditures for certain purposes from fees, moneys  or other revenues, including
Miscellaneous Receipts but excluding lottery funds  and federal funds, collected or received by State
Treasurer.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the financial administration of the State Treasurer; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Notwithstanding any other law limiting expenditures,  the following amounts
are established for  the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, as the maximum limits for payment
of expenses from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts,  but ex-
cluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received by  the State Treasurer, for the
following purposes:
(1) Administrative expenses of
State Treasury operations ........ $ 82,966,849
(2) Administrative expenses of
the Oregon 529 Savings
Network .................................... $ 7,519,310
(3) Administrative expenses
related to the Oregon
Retirement Savings Board ........ $ 4,162,966
SECTION 2.
This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2019 Act takes effect
July 1, 2019.
Note: For budget, see 2019-2021 Biennial Budget
