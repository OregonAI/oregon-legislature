80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 2729
Sponsored by Representatives  WITT, HELM, Senator HANSELL; Representatives BONHAM, CLEM, GOMBERG,
LEWIS, MCLAIN, SALINAS, SMITH DB, Senators ROBLAN, THOMSEN
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board  for purposes
related to programs for grants from Oregon Agricultural Heritage Fund.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to programs for grants from  Oregon Agricultural Heritage Fund; and declaring an emer-
gency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board, for the biennium beginning  July 1, 2019,
out of the General  Fund, the amount of $738,652 for the purposes of hiring personnel and
contracting for services to carry out the provisions of ORS 541.977 to 541.989.
SECTION 2. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board, for the biennium beginning  July 1, 2019,
out of the General Fund, the amount of $9,250,000,  to be deposited in the Oregon Agricultural
Heritage Fund established under ORS 541.978.
SECTION 3. This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2019 Act takes effect
July 1, 2019.
