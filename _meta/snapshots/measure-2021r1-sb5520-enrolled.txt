CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to the financial administration of the Office of the Governor; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There is appropriated to the Office of the Governor,  for the biennium be-
ginning July 1, 2021, out of the General Fund, the amount of $18,588,115.
SECTION 2. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Office of the Governor, for the biennium beginning  July 1, 2021, out of the Gen-
eral Fund, the amount of $50,000 for the necessary expenses of  the Governor in connection
with the duties imposed by law and for such other expenses as may be incumbent  upon or
requested by the Governor.
SECTION 3. Notwithstanding any other law limiting expenditures,  the amount of
$4,625,139 is  established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2021, as the maximum limit for
payment of expenses from lottery moneys allocated from the  Administrative Services Eco-
nomic Development Fund to the Office of the Governor for the Regional Solutions Program.
SECTION 4. Notwithstanding any  other law limiting expenditures, the amount of
$4,421,059 is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2021, as the maximum limit  for
payment of  expenses from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts,
but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received by the  Office of the
Governor.
SECTION 5. This 2021 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency  is declared to exist, and this 2021 Act takes effect
July 1, 2021.
