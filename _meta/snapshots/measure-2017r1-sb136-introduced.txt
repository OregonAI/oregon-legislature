79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 136
Sponsored by Senator MONROE (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Moves up sunset date for authority of school districts to establish post-graduate scholar pro-
grams to June 30, 2019.
Directs Department of Education to make recommendations for legislation to fund post-graduate
scholar programs with moneys that are not distributed from State School Fund.
Prohibits  school  districts from receiving or expending moneys distributed from State School
Fund to pay for community college costs incurred by, or on behalf of, post-graduate scholars.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to post-graduate  scholars; creating new provisions; amending sections 5 and 9, chapter 109,
Oregon Laws 2016; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.  Section 5, chapter 109, Oregon Laws 2016, is amended to read:
Sec. 5.  No later than November 1, 2018, the Department of Education shall:
(1) Provide a report to the interim legislative committees on education that includes:
(a) The amount of moneys received by school districts  from the State School Fund under ORS
327.013 for post-graduate scholars participating in a program  established under section 2 [ of this

Fund for post-graduate scholar programs. The consideration must include:
(A) Methods by which funds would be distributed to school districts;

SECTION 2.
 Section 9, chapter 109, Oregon Laws 2016, is amended to read:
Sec. 9.  Section 2 [ of this 2016 Act ], chapter 109, Oregon  Laws 2016,  is repealed on June 30,
[2021] 2019.
SECTION 3. Section 4 of this 2017 Act is added to and made a  part of ORS 327.006 to
327.133.
SECTION 4. (1) As used in this section:
(a) “Community college costs”  means costs incurred for a course at a community college,
including tuition, fees or books for the course.
(b) “Post-graduate scholar” means a student who:
(A) Has been in grades 9 through 12 for more than a total of four school years; and
(B) Has satisfied the requirements for a high school diploma as provided in ORS  329.451
(2).
(2) A school district may not receive or expend moneys distributed from the  State School
Fund under ORS 327.013 to pay for community  college costs incurred by, or on the behalf of,
a post-graduate scholar.
SECTION 5.
(1) Section 4 of this 2017 Act becomes operative on July 1, 2021.
(2) Section 4 of this 2017 Act applies to State School Fund distributions commencing  with
the 2021-2022 distributions.
SECTION 6. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2017 Act takes effect
on its passage.
