80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
Senate Bill 552
Sponsored by Senator KNOPP, Representative HELT; Representatives BOLES, ZIKA (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates to Department of Education for State  School Fund amount determined by Quality
Education Commission to be sufficient to ensure that state’s system of kindergarten through  grade
12 public education meets quality goals.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to funding for public education; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Department of Education, for the biennium beginning July  1, 2019, out of the
General Fund, the amount of $10,733,900,000, for the State School Fund.
SECTION 2. This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2019 Act takes effect
July 1, 2019.
