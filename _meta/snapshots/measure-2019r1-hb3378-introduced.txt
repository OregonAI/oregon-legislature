80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 3378
Sponsored by  Representative WILLIAMS, Senator THOMSEN, Representative SMITH G; Representatives ALONSO
LEON, BONHAM, GOMBERG, GORSEK, HELM, HERNANDEZ, HOLVEY, KENY-GUYER, MEEK,  PRUSAK,
REARDON, WILDE, WITT, Senator DEMBROW
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys to add bicycle and pedestrian lane to Bridge of the Gods.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the Bridge of the Gods; and declaring an emergency.
Whereas the Bridge of the Gods is  a key regional transportation link in north central Oregon
and the Columbia River Gorge; and
Whereas the seismic resiliency of the bridge is critical to the transportation infrastructure  of
the region; and
Whereas hiking and biking are key components of the recreation-based economy in the region;
and
Whereas the State of Oregon and the federal  government are investing significant resources in
expanding biking opportunities in the Columbia River Gorge; and
Whereas the Pacific Crest Trail crosses the Columbia River  on the Bridge of the Gods and in
2018 over 7,000 hikers used the bridge crossing; and
Whereas hikers and bikers must share the narrow 1926 bridge with vehicular traffic; and
Whereas there does not  exist a safe passageway for hikers and bikers to cross the Columbia
River between Portland and The Dalles; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services,  for the biennium beginning July
1, 2019, out of the General Fund,  the amount of $1,500,000, for distribution to the Port of
Cascade Locks for the purpose of adding a bicycle  and pedestrian lane to the Bridge of the
Gods.
SECTION 2. This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2019 Act takes effect
July 1, 2019.
