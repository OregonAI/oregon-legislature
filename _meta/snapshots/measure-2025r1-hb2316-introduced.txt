83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 2316
Sponsored by Representative MANNIX (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: This Act  allows DAS to designate state lands for homes for sale to first-time home
owners and OHCS to  designate locally identified land for homes for sale to lower income home
owners. Designated lands are exempt from land use laws and, for five years, exempt  from tax based
on the value of the land. (Flesch Readability Score: 61.9)
Allows designation of Home Start Lands to be used for housing. Exempts Home Start Lands from
statewide land use laws. Requires that the  Home Start Lands designated by the Housing and
Community Services Department be used for affordable housing. Requires that the  Home Start
Lands designated by the  Oregon Department of Administrative Services be developed for housing
by the department and sold to first-time home buyers under a lottery-based prioritization system.
Exempts dwellings created on Home Start Lands from property tax assessments on  the value
of the land for a period of five years following the sale to  a homeowner. Applies to property tax
years beginning July 1, 2028, and before July 1, 2034.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to lands designated for housing.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Definitions. As used in sections 2 to 10 of this 2025 Act:
(1) “Home Start Lands” mean lands designated  by the Oregon Department of Adminis-
trative Services under  section 2 of this 2025 Act or nominated by a local government under
section 3 of this 2025 Act.
(2) “Local government” means:
(a) For land within a city’s boundaries, the city; or
(b) For all other land not described in paragraph  (a) of this subsection, the county in
which the land is located.
SECTION 2.
State designation of Home Start Lands.  (1) As used in this section:
(a) “Lands owned by the state” means real property or interest in real property that is:
(A) Subject to ORS 270.100;
(B) Controlled by the State Department of Fish and Wildlife;
(C) State forestlands that the State Forestry Department controls; or
(D) Controlled by the Department of Transportation.
(b) “Lands owned by the state” does not include real property that is:
(A) State lands  as classified in ORS 273.251, or is otherwise a part of the Common School
Fund;
(B) Land held by the state as a trustee; or
(C) Lands whose use  under sections 2 to 10 of this 2025 Act would be unconstitutional
or prohibited by or inconsistent with any federal law.
(2) The Oregon Department of Administrative  Services shall identify lands owned by the
state and shall designate the property as  Home Start Lands if, in the department’s dis-

SECTION 3.
Housing and Community  Services Department designation of Home Start
Lands. (1) The Housing and Community Services  Department may designate real property
as Home Start Lands if the department finds that the  property complies with the require-
ments under subsection  (2) of this section and that the local government and property own-
ers have identified a plan or funding source that is substantially likely to increase the  supply
of affordable housing in this state.
(2) Local governments may, by ordinance, nominate real  property for designation as
Home Start Lands, provided that:
(a) The property is  all or a portion of a tract, as defined in ORS 215.010, that is contig-
uous to, or across a road from, an urban growth boundary;
(b) The property is subject to an affordable housing covenant as described in ORS 456.270
to 456.295 that will make the property, including each lot  or parcel created from the property
by division of land, developable only for housing  that will be made available for
homeownership and affordable to  middle or low income households for a period of no less
than 30 years from date of sale to a homeowner; and
(c) All owners of the property have consented to the designation.
(3) The department may adopt rules to administer this section.
SECTION 4.
Planning and uses of Home Start Lands.  (1) Home Start Lands may be zoned,
divided, developed and used only for one single-family dwelling.
(2) Except for laws applicable only to the procedures for dividing land under ORS  92.010
to 92.192, Home Start Lands are not subject to any provision of:
(a) ORS chapter 195, 197, 197A, 215 or 227;
(b) A statewide land use planning goal;
(c) Rules or orders adopted by the Land Conservation and Development Commission;
(d) A comprehensive plan; or
(e) Land use regulations, except as provided in subsection (3) of this section.
(3) A  local government may adopt, by ordinance, siting or development regulations con-
sistent with this section for the specific purpose of regulating Home Start Lands.
(4) An application for development of Home Start Lands under this section must be:
(a) Approved or rejected by the governing body of the local government;
(b) Approved or rejected within 100 days from the date of the application; and
(c) Appealed only as a writ of review under ORS 34.010 to 34.100.

SECTION 5.
Termination of Home Start Lands designation.  The designation of real
property as Home Start Lands is terminated for real property if the  property has not re-
ceived authorization from the local  government to use the property for residential develop-
ment under section 4 of this 2025 Act and:
(1) For property designated under section 2 of this 2025 Act,  the Oregon Department of
Administrative Services has issued an order terminating the designation; or
(2) For property designated under section 3 of this 2025 Act:
(a) The local government has adopted an ordinance terminating the designation; or
(b) The Housing and Community Services Department has issued  an order terminating
the designation.
SECTION 6.
State housing development on Home Start Lands.  (1) For Home Start Lands
designated by Oregon Department of Administrative Services under section 2 of this  2025
Act, the department shall:
(a) Work to diligently  prepare affordable housing by taking necessary steps and engaging
contractors to:
(A) Partition, subdivide or replat the lands for the development  of housing under this
section; and
(B) Prepare the land for  housing, through grading, surveying, planning, installing
infrastructure for residential development on the land and other activities;
(b) As necessary, disperse or lend moneys for purposes of  capital improvements, as de-
fined in ORS 223.299, which may be in addition to or  in lieu of paying system development
charges, to ensure sufficient infrastructure capacity for anticipated housing on the land;
(c) Develop single-family housing on the land; and
(d) Promote and convey the  homes and real property as provided in section 9 of this 2025
Act.
(2) Home Start  Lands may not be made a part of a planned community. Any property of
Home Start Lands to  be maintained as a park, as an open area or for infrastructure purposes
must be retained by the department or must be conveyed by the state to a city, county,
special district or public utility.

(C) Express interest in specific properties.
(b) Establish a random lottery  system to assign priority numbers to eligible buyers of
housing on Home Start Lands.
(2) To be eligible to join the portal established under  subsection (1) of this section and
to register for the lottery, and individual must:
(a) Be at least 18 years of age;
(b) Be a current resident of Oregon;

SECTION 8.
Initial development of portal and lottery.  (1) No later than January  1, 2027,
the Oregon Department of Administrative Services shall develop  the electronic portal under

(2) No later than July 1, 2027, the department shall admit initial eligible  registrants and

under subsection (2) of this section.

who, within  three days, agrees to sign a purchase agreement. As part of the purchase
agreement, the registrant must agree to:
(a) Accept the department’s standard purchase terms;
(b) Within five business days, deposit $500 in escrow;
(c) Within 10 business days, obtain written preapproval from a lender  for the full pur-

SECTION 10.
Occupancy requirements for dwellings sold by state.  (1) For purposes of this
section:
(a) “Original purchase price” means the price paid by  the grantee to the state, excluding
any closing costs or taxes.
(b) “Primary residence” means the place  where an individual actually lives for at least
nine months of the  calendar year and that the individual continues to maintain as the
individual’s principal residence.
(2) Each deed conveying real property under section 9 of this 2025 Act must:
(a) Require that the property be used as  the grantee’s primary residence for a period of
no less than five years; and
(b) Grant to the state,  through the Oregon Department of Administrative Services, an
option to  purchase the property at the original purchase price that may be executed only if,
at any time within five years of the date of the deed:
(A) The grantee attempts to  sell, transfer or otherwise voluntarily convey the property;
or
(B) The grantee fails to maintain the property as the grantee’s primary residence.
(3) The option to purchase under subsection (2) of this section:
(a) Must be exercised by the state  under subsection (2)(b)(A) of this section within 30
days of receiving written notice from the grantee of intent to sell, transfer or convey;
(b) May be exercised by the state under subsection (2)(b)(B) of this section upon:
(A) Determining that the grantee has failed to maintain the property as the  grantee’s
primary residence; and
(B) Providing written notice to the grantee and any lender of record at least 60 days prior
to exercising the option;
(c) If exercised, requires closing within 60 days of providing notice  under paragraph (b)
of this subsection;
(d) Automatically terminates five years after the date of the deed; and
(e) Is subordinate to any first position purchase money mortgage or trust deed.

(a) The state shall pay off any outstanding balance  on any purchase money mortgage or
trust deed securing the property;
(b) The grantee shall be entitled to any remaining  proceeds after satisfying all liens and

SECTION 11.
Section 12 of this  2025 Act is added to and made a part of ORS chapter 308.
SECTION 12. Property taxes for dwellings  on Home Start Lands. (1) This section applies
only to a dwelling on Home Start Lands, as defined in section 2 of this 2025 Act, that is
conveyed:
(a) To the first homeowner under a deed subject to section 3 (2)(b) of this 2025 Act; or
(b) By the state under section 9 of this 2025 Act.
(2) For a dwelling described in subsection (1) of this section:
(a) The county  assessor shall subtract from the assessed value and the maximum as-
sessed value the real market value of the land for the  purposes of calculating the property’s
taxes and special assessments.
(b) The subtraction is calculated for tax years beginning on July  1 of the property tax
year first following the conveyance by the state to the homeowner and ends  after five con-
secutive tax years.
(c) If the property was acquired by the state under section 10 of  this 2025 Act, the five-
year period described under paragraph (b) of this subsection begins anew  for the new pur-
chaser.
SECTION 13.
Applicability of property tax subtraction. Section 12 of this 2025 Act applies
to property tax years beginning on or after July 1, 2028, and before July 1, 2034.
