House Bill 2435
Sponsored by Representative YUNKER;  Representatives BOICE, WRIGHT, Senators NASH, SMITH DB (Presession
filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Tells the SOS  to publish a monthly report on voter data in each county. Specifies the
data to be included in the report. (Flesch Readability Score: 66.4).
Requires the Secretary of State to publish a monthly statistical report on voter registrations  for
each county in this state. Specifies the statistical information to be included in the monthly report.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to monthly statistical report on voter registrations.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Secretary of State shall publish each month on  the website main-
tained by the secretary a  statistical report on voter registrations for each county in this
state.
(2) The report published under  subsection (1) of this section must include, for the pre-
ceding month:
(a) The number of registration cards, as defined in ORS 247.002, received;
(b) The number of registration cards rejected;
(c) The number of voter registration transactions as follows:
(A) New voter registrations;
(B) Address changes;
(C) Political party affiliation changes; and
(D) Name changes;
(d) The number of registration cancellations for the following reasons:
(A) Residential address changes outside of the county;
(B) Death; and
(C) Any other reason identified by the secretary in the report; and
(e) The number of political party affiliations, listed by political party, as of the end of  the
reporting period, including:
(A) Active registrations;
(B) Inactive registrations; and
(C) Total number of active and inactive registrations.
(3) The secretary shall establish by rule the process by which each  county clerk in this
state shall provide each month to the secretary the information required under subsection
(2) of this section to be published in the report required under  subsection (1) of this section.
