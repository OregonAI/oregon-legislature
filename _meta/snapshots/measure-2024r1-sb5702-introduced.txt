82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2024 Regular Session
Senate Bill 5702
Sponsored by JOINT COMMITTEE ON WAYS AND MEANS
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act  approves certain fees charged by state agencies. (Flesch Readability Score:
75.5).
Approves certain new or increased fees adopted by state agencies.
Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to state financial administration; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of ORS 291.055 (1)(e), the
following new or increased fees, adopted by the Department of Public  Safety Standards and
Training and approved by the Oregon Department of Administrative Services, are approved:
(1) Private Security Entity License Fee: $624
(2) Late Renewal Fee: $50
(3) Duplicate or Replacement License Fee: $24
(4) Non-sufficient Funds Penalty Fee: $25
SECTION 2.
For the purpose of carrying out the  provisions of ORS 291.055 (1)(e), a fin-
gerprinting and background check fee  of $140, adopted by the Higher Education Coordinating
Commission and approved by the Oregon Department of Administrative Services,  is ap-
proved.
SECTION 3. For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of ORS 291.055 (1)(e), the
following new or increased fees, adopted by the State Department  of Agriculture and ap-
proved by the Oregon Department of Administrative Services, are approved:
(1) Hemp Grower Late Fee: $250
(2) Noncommercial Pesticide Applicator License per year, including one category: $50
(3) Noncommercial Pesticide  Applicator License per year, for each additional category:
$7.50
(4) Noncommercial Pesticide Applicator License per year, for each  additional category
after license issued: $12.50
(5) Noncommercial Pesticide Trainee License: $50
SECTION 4.
For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of ORS 291.055 (1)(e), the
following new or increased  fees, adopted by the Department of Environmental Quality and
approved by the Oregon Department of Administrative Services, are approved:
(1) Seismic Risk Assessment Submittal Fee: $39,000
(2) Annual Compliance Fee: $23,000
(3) Risk Mitigation Implementation Plan Submittal Fee: $36,000
