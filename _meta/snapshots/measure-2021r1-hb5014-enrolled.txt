AN ACT
Relating to the financial administration of the Department of Justice; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There are appropriated to the Department of Justice,  for the biennium be-
ginning July 1, 2021, out of the General Fund, the following amounts, for the following  pur-
poses:
(1) Office of the Attorney
General and Administration ..... $ 680,628
(2) Appellate Division ..................... $ 401,704
(3) Criminal Justice Division.......... $ 18,324,749
(4) Crime Victim and Survivor
Services..................................... $ 19,882,698
(5) Defense of Criminal
Convictions................................ $ 38,650,233
(6) Division of Child Support.......... $ 50,716,738
(7) Debt service and related
costs .......................................... $ 8,375,250

of expenses  from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts, but ex-
cluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received by the Department of  Justice,
for the following purposes:
(1) Office of the Attorney
General and Administration ..... $ 44,886,891
(2) Appellate Division ..................... $ 26,237,909
(3) Civil Enforcement Division ....... $124,111,440
(4) Criminal Justice Division.......... $ 9,878,023

Services..................................... $ 28,289,381
(6) General Counsel Division .......... $ 76,431,533
(7) Trial Division ............................ $ 48,764,413
(8) Division of Child Support.......... $ 22,749,638

are established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2021, as the maximum limits for payment
