79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 383
Sponsored by Senator ROBLAN, Representative MCLANE (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates  moneys  from General Fund to Department of Environmental Quality for purposes
of awarding grants to develop and administer loan programs  to provide low-interest loans for pur-
poses related to on-site septic system repairs, replacements, upgrades and evaluations.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2017.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to state financial administration; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Department of Environmental Quality, for the biennium beginning  July 1, 2017,
out of the General Fund, the amount of $1,500,000 for the purposes of:
(1) Awarding grants under section  2, chapter 87, Oregon Laws 2016, in an amount not less
than $1,300,000; and
(2) Administration costs necessary to implement section  2, chapter 87, Oregon Laws 2016.
SECTION 2. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2017 Act takes effect
July 1, 2017.
