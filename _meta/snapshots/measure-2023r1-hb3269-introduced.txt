82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 3269
Sponsored by Representatives HELFRICH, STOUT; Representative WRIGHT
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Department of Education to conduct study to identify best method  for ensuring amount
of moneys appropriated for education is adequate, consistent, stable and sustainable.
Directs department to submit  report to interim committee of Legislative Assembly no later than
September 15, 2024.
Sunsets January 2, 2025.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to funding for public education; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Department of Education shall conduct a study to identify the best
method for ensuring that the  amount of moneys appropriated by the Legislative Assembly
for public education is adequate,  consistent, stable and sustainable. When conducting the
study, the department shall consider:
(a) The  method followed by the Quality Education Commission as provided by ORS
327.506; and
(b) The method of spending at least 33 percent of the amount approved by  the Legislative
Assembly for a biennium on education and public safety.
(2) The department shall report  the results of the study in the manner provided by ORS
192.245, and may include recommendations for  legislation, to an interim committee of the
Legislative Assembly related to education no later than September 15, 2024.
SECTION 2. Section 1 of this 2023 Act is repealed on January 2, 2025.
SECTION 3. This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2023 Act takes effect
on its passage.
