83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 3759
Sponsored by Representative EDWARDS
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: This Act makes OHCS give grants for fees for  moderate income housing developers.
(Flesch Readability Score: 63.4).
Requires the Housing and Community Services Department  to provide grants for the develop-
ment of moderate income housing, paid to cover or offset permitting  fees and charges. Appropriates
moneys for grants.
Declares an emergency, effective July 1, 2025.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to costs of developing housing; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2025 Act is added to and made a  part of ORS 456.515 to
456.725.
SECTION 2. (1) The Housing and Community Services Department shall provide grants
to developers of  moderate income housing for the purposes of paying for permitting fees or
system development charges for the construction of moderate income housing.
(2) The department  may not award more than the total amount of fees and charges as-
sessed for the development.
(3) The department shall distribute grant moneys  awarded under this section directly to
the local government or service district assessing the fee or charge.
SECTION 3. Section 2 of this 2025 Act is repealed on January 2, 2028.
SECTION 4. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Housing and  Community Services Department, for the biennium beginning July
1, 2025, out  of the General Fund, the amount of $15,000,000 to implement section 2 of this 2025
Act.
SECTION 5. This 2025 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2025 Act takes effect
July 1, 2025.
