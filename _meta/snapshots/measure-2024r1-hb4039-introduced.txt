82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2024 Regular Session
House Bill 4039
Sponsored by Representative OSBORNE (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Makes  tax credit for turned-in RV for 2025 to 2030 tax years. Becomes law 91 days from
sine die. (Flesch Readability Score: 63.6).
Establishes an income tax credit for surrender of a motor home or recreational vehicle.
Applies to tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025, and before January 1, 2031.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to an income tax credit for surrender of motor homes; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2024 Act is added to and made a part of ORS chapter 315.
SECTION 2. (1) As used in this section:
(a) “Dismantler” has the meaning given that term in ORS 801.236.
(b) “Motor home” has the meaning given that term in ORS 801.350.
(c) “Recreational vehicle” has the meaning given that term in ORS 174.101.
(2) A resident or nonresident  individual shall be allowed a credit against taxes that are
otherwise due under ORS chapter  316 for the surrender, to a dismantler, of a motor vehicle
that is either a motor home or a recreational vehicle. The credit shall be  in the amount of
$5,000 for each motor vehicle  that is surrendered by the taxpayer during the tax year. In
order for a credit to be allowed under this section, at the time of surrender:
(a) The surrendered motor vehicle must have a model year that  predates by at least 15
years the tax year in which the motor vehicle is surrendered;
(b) The taxpayer must be shown as the owner of  the vehicle on the title to the motor
vehicle and must have physical possession of the title;
(c) The dismantler must hold a valid dismantler certificate issued under  ORS 822.110; and
(d) The dismantler must provide written proof of the surrender to the taxpayer.
(3) The written proof of surrender required under this section must include:
(a) The date on which the motor vehicle is surrendered;
(b) The year, make and model of the motor vehicle;
(c) The registration plate number assigned  by the Department of Transportation to the
motor vehicle, if any;
(d) The name of the vehicle owner;
(e) The vehicle description and vehicle identification number; and
(f) The certificate number of the dismantler certificate issued to the dismantler by  the
Department of Transportation.
