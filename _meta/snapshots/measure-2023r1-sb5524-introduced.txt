82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
Senate Bill 5524
Introduced and printed  pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 23 (2023) (at the request of Oregon Department
of Administrative Services)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates  moneys  from General Fund to Oregon Business Development Department for cer-
tain biennial expenses.
Limits biennial expenditures from fees, moneys or  other revenues, including Miscellaneous Re-
ceipts, but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received  by department for certain
purposes.
Limits biennial expenditures by department from lottery moneys for certain purposes.
Limits biennial expenditures by department from federal funds for certain purposes.
Authorizes certain nonlimited expenditures.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to  the financial administration of the Oregon Business Development Department; and de-
claring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There are appropriated to the Oregon Business Development  Department,
for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, out of the General Fund,  the following amounts, for
the following purposes:
(1) Oregon Arts Commission .......... $ 4,287,169
(2) Operations................................. $ 264,792
(3) Business, innovation
and trade................................... $ 179,241
(4) Debt service .............................. $103,371,404
SECTION 2. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Oregon  Business Development Department, for the biennium beginning July 1,
2023, out  of the General Fund, the amount of $1,000,000, for business, innovation and trade,
for municipal wildfire assistance approved in section 192, chapter 669, Oregon Laws 2021.
SECTION 3. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Business Development Department, for the biennium  beginning July 1,
2023, out of the General Fund, the amount of $13,500,000, for business, innovation and  trade,
for the emerging opportunities program approved in section 287, chapter 669, Oregon Laws
2021.
SECTION 4.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Oregon  Business Development Department, for the biennium beginning July 1,
2023, out of the  General Fund, the amount of $1,584,181, for infrastructure, for the purpose
of making grants to the county entities for financial assistance due to the  2020 wildfire sea-
son for building and planning departments staffing that were approved in section 194, chapter
669, Oregon Laws 2021.
