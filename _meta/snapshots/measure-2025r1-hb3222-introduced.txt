83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 3222
Sponsored by Representative NATHANSON; Representative MCLAIN, Senators FREDERICK, WEBER  (Presession
filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Tells ODE to update a survey on the tests  given to students in this state. (Flesch
Readability Score: 84.4).
Directs the Department of Education to review and update information collected for a  survey
of the academic assessments administered to  the students of this state by school districts. Requires
the department to  submit an updated report to the State Board of Education and to an interim
committee of the Legislative Assembly related to education no later than December 15, 2026.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to academic assessments administered to students by school districts.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Department of Education shall:
(1) Review the information collected from school districts for  the purpose of section 1,
chapter 71, Oregon Laws 2022, and identify information that is incomplete,  clearly inaccurate
or likely inaccurate.
(2) Request the school  district responsible for the provision of the information identified
in subsection (1) of this section  to submit or resubmit information that is complete and ac-
curate. The department shall provide assistance to the school district to ensure that the in-
formation collected is complete and accurate.
(3) Update the report required under section 1 (6), chapter 71, Oregon Laws 2022, to:
(a) Reflect information collected as provided by subsection (2) of this section; and
(b) Include recommendations and best practices  for surveying teachers and classified
school employees about time spent to  plan, prepare and administer academic assessments
required by the school district and the perceived usefulness of the assessments.
(4) Submit the updated report prepared under subsection (3) of this  section to the State
Board of Education and an interim committee of the  Legislative Assembly related to educa-
tion no later than December 15, 2026.
SECTION 2.
Section 1 of this 2025 Act is repealed on January 2, 2027.
