SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Establishes Willamette Falls Locks  Commission as policy-making and advisory body for issues
relating to repair, reopening, operation,  maintenance and future transfer of ownership of naviga-
tional canal and locks.
Establishes Willamette Falls Navigation Infrastructure Program within  Oregon Business Devel-
opment Department to provide assistance and staff support to commission.
Establishes Willamette  Falls Navigation Infrastructure Program Account. Appropriates funds
from General Fund to department for purposes of Act.
Sunsets commission, program and account on December 31, 2023.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to Willamette Falls navigation infrastructure; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) There is established the Willamette Falls Locks Commission as the
policy-making and advisory body for the Willamette Falls  Navigation Infrastructure Program
established under section 5 of this 2017 Act.
(2) The commission shall consist of 11 members appointed as follows:
(a) The Governor shall appoint:
(A) One member who is the chair  or a member of the Clackamas County Board of County
Commissioners, pursuant to a recommendation of the board;
(B) One member who is  the president or a member of the governing body of the metro-
politan service district for the Portland metropolitan area, pursuant to the recommendation
of the governing body;
(C) One member who is a representative of the Confederated  Tribes of the Grand Ronde,
pursuant to a recommendation of the confederated tribes;
(D) One member from the State Parks and Recreation Department;
(E) One  member who is a representative of the Department of Transportation, pursuant
to a recommendation by the Director of Transportation;
(F) One member who is a mayor or a member of the governing body of  a city who re-
presents the  interests of the cities located along the Willamette River that have an interest
in the Willamette Falls navigational canal and locks, pursuant to a  recommendation by the
governing body of the City of West Linn; and
(G) One member who represents the collective interests in the Willamette River of local
businesses, economic development organizations, the tourism industry and user groups.

(b) The President of the Senate shall appoint one  member from among the members of
the Senate.
(c) The Senate Minority  Leader shall appoint one member from among members of the
Senate.
(d) The Speaker of the House of Representatives shall appoint one member from  among
the members of the House of Representatives.
(e) The House Minority Leader shall appoint  one member from among members of the
House of Representatives.
(3) The term of office of each member of the commission  appointed by the Governor is
four years, but  a member serves at the pleasure of the Governor. Before the expiration of
the term of a  member, the Governor shall appoint a successor whose term begins on January
1 of the  following year. A member is eligible for reappointment. If there is a vacancy for any
cause, the appointing authority shall make an appointment  to become immediately effective
for the unexpired term.
(4) Members of the commission who are not members of the Legislative Assembly are
not entitled to compensation, but may be reimbursed for  actual and necessary travel and
other expenses incurred by them in the performance of their official  duties in the manner
and in the amounts provided for in ORS 292.495. Claims for expenses incurred in performing
functions of the commission shall be paid out of funds  available for purposes of the com-
mission.
(5) Members of the Legislative Assembly appointed to the commission are nonvoting
members of the commission and act in an advisory capacity only.
(6) The commission shall  select one of its members as chairperson and another as vice
chairperson, for terms and with duties and  powers necessary for the performance of the
functions of the offices as the commission determines.
(7) Official action by  the commission requires the approval of a majority of the voting
members of the commission.
(8) The  commission shall meet no less than four times per year at times and places
specified by the call of the chairperson or of a majority of the voting members of the  com-
mission.
(9) The provisions of ORS  chapter 192 apply to the meetings and records of the commis-
sion under the same terms as they apply to public bodies other than the state.
SECTION 2. Notwithstanding the term of  office specified by section 1 of this 2017 Act,
of the members first appointed to the commission by the Governor:
(1) One shall serve for a term ending December 31, 2018.
(2) Two shall serve for terms ending December 31, 2019.
(3) Two shall serve for terms ending December 31, 2020.
(4) Two shall serve for terms ending December 31, 2021.

(b) Collaborate with local governments, historic  organizations and stakeholders to de-
velop a plan to promote the locks as a tourist attraction of historic interest;

SECTION 4.
(1) The Willamette Falls Locks Commission shall convene  a stakeholder ad-
visory group to provide information to the commission relating to the repair, reopening, op-
eration and maintenance  of the Willamette Falls navigational canal and locks. The
commission shall appoint members representing:
(a) State agencies;
(b) Nonprofit organizations;
(c) Stakeholders with knowledge  of environmental and economic concerns relating to the
reopening of the locks; and
(d) Members of federally recognized Indian tribes.
(2) The commission may establish  advisory or technical committees as the commission
considers necessary to aid and advise  the commission in the performance of its functions.
The committees  may be continuing or temporary committees. The commission shall deter-
mine the representation, membership, terms and organization  of the committees and shall
appoint the members of the committees.
SECTION 5.
(1) The Willamette Falls Navigation  Infrastructure Program is established
within the Oregon Business Development Department. The program  is subject to the super-
vision of the  Administrator of the Willamette Falls Navigation Infrastructure Program. The
program shall consist of the administrator and all personnel employed in the program.
(2) The program shall provide the Willamette Falls Locks Commission with staff  and
other assistance as necessary for the commission to perform its duties.
SECTION 6. (1) The Director of the Oregon Business  Development Department, in con-
sultation with the Willamette Falls Locks Commission, shall appoint an Administrator of  the
Willamette Falls Navigation  Infrastructure Program, who shall serve at the pleasure of the
director.
(2) As  the administrative head of the Willamette Falls Navigation Infrastructure Pro-
gram, the administrator shall:
(a) Serve as the intermediary to facilitate collaboration between the  United States Army
Corps of Engineers and the Willamette Falls Locks Commission  to evaluate options for the
repair, reopening, operation and maintenance of the  Willamette Falls navigation canal and

SECTION 7. (1) The Willamette Falls Navigation Infrastructure Program Account is es-
tablished in the State Treasury,  separate and distinct from the General Fund. All interest
earned by the account shall be credited to the account. All moneys in the account are  con-
tinuously appropriated to the Oregon Business Development Department for the purposes
of sections 1 to 6 of this 2017 Act.
(2) All  moneys received by the department for the purposes of sections 1 to 6 of this 2017
Act shall be deposited in the account.
(3) The department  may accept gifts, grants or contributions from any source, whether
public or private, for deposit in the account.
SECTION 8.
There is appropriated to the Oregon Business  Development Department, for
the biennium beginning July 1, 2017, out of the General  Fund, the amount of $677,500 for the
purposes of sections 1 to 6 of this 2017 Act.
SECTION 9. (1) Sections 1 to 8 of this 2017 Act are repealed on December 31, 2023.
(2) Any balance  in the Willamette Falls Navigation Infrastructure Program Account that
is unexpended or unobligated on the date of the repeal of section 7 of this 2017 Act by  sub-
section (1) of this section, and  all moneys that would have been deposited in the Willamette
Falls Navigation Infrastructure Program Account had section 7 of this 2017  Act stayed in
effect, shall be transferred to and deposited in the General Fund to be available  for general
governmental purposes.
SECTION 10. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of  the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2017 Act takes effect
on its passage.
