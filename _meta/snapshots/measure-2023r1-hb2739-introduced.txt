82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2739
Sponsored by Representatives NERON, RUIZ; Senators DEMBROW, WOODS (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Oregon Department of Administrative Services to prepare tentative  budget for funding
of State School Fund.  Requires  department to form committee  for purpose of preparing tentative
budget. Prescribes factors to be considered by committee.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the method for funding public education.
Whereas funding for kindergarten through  grade 12 public education accounts for the largest
segment of the state’s General Fund budget; and
Whereas the primary source of funding  for kindergarten through grade 12 public education
comes from appropriations made by the Legislative Assembly from the General Fund  to the State
School Fund; and
Whereas the Legislative Assembly must develop biennial state budgets based on  available re-
sources; and
Whereas the state budgeting process depends on an accurate  analysis of current resources and
uncontrollable cost drivers; and
Whereas the public  relies on fiscal analyses to describe public education fiscal facts with the
greatest accuracy possible; and
Whereas the calculation process for determining the current service level for  public education
that is  prescribed by the Governor’s Executive Order 14-14 has introduced instability to public ed-
ucation funding and relies, in part,  on policies and organizational entities that are no longer in force
or no longer exist; and
Whereas confusion about  the meaning of the current service level and confusion about the level
itself have impacted the stability of school budgets; and
Whereas the state’s calculation of a budget for kindergarten through grade 12 public  education
based on current service levels has not and cannot be developed at the  level of detail as for other
state programs; and
Whereas the Quality Education Commission calculates  the gap between baseline funding for
public education and its recommended full-funding level for public education based on  the current
service level calculation; and
Whereas policymakers  rely on the accuracy of the current service level calculation to gauge
whether proposed appropriation levels will result in stable school budgets; and
Whereas the State of Oregon has an interest in directing the process for determining the current
service level for  kindergarten through grade 12 public schools based on accurate information and
collaboration among all key entities involved in the school finance process; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:

SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2023 Act is added to and made a  part of ORS 327.006 to
327.133.
SECTION 2. (1) Each even-numbered  year, the Oregon Department of Administrative
Services shall prepare a tentative budget for funding the State  School Fund for the next
biennium.
(2)(a) For the purpose of preparing the tentative budget, the department  shall form a
committee. The committee must include:
(A) Four representatives of  state government, which must include one representative
from each of the following:
(i) The Department of Education;
(ii) The Legislative Fiscal Officer, or the designee of the officer;
(iii) The Legislative Revenue Officer, or the designee of the officer; and
(iv) The Governor’s office;
(B) Four representatives of professional  educator organizations, which must include one
representative from each of the following:
(i) School district administrators;
(ii) School district boards;
(iii) A licensed school employees labor union; and
(iv) A classified school employees labor union;
(C) Three representatives of educator professionals, which must include one designee of
each of the following:
(i) School business officials;
(ii) School district human resources personnel; and
(iii) Education service districts; and
(D) The cochairs of the Joint Committee on Ways and Means.
(b) The Legislative Fiscal Officer  and the Legislative Revenue Officer, or their designees,
and the cochairs of the Joint Committee on Ways and Means shall serve as  nonvoting
members on the committee formed under this subsection.
(3)(a) The Oregon Department  of Administrative Services shall review with the commit-
tee formed under subsection (2) of this section the components of the methodology that will
be used to prepare the tentative budget and the calculations for the tentative budget.
(b) The methodology used for the tentative budget must calculate the  current service
level based on:
(A) The methodology identified by the committee;
(B) An assumption that 49 percent of the amounts available for distribution through the
State School Fund were distributed in the first  year of the current biennium, and 51 percent
of the amounts available for distribution  through the State School Fund were distributed in
the second year of the current biennium;
(C) The use of the  amounts distributed through the State School Fund during the second
year of the current biennium as the base amount for  making calculations for the next
biennium; and
(D) An assumption that 49 percent of the amounts available for distribution through  the
State School Fund will be distributed in the first year of the next biennium, and 51 percent
of the amounts available for distribution through the State School Fund  will be distributed
in the second year of the next biennium.

(c) For any components of the methodology not described in paragraph (b) of  this sub-
section, the Oregon Department of Administrative Services shall collaborate  with the com-
mittee to refine the methodology by considering alternative  approaches to the calculations
and other factors  the committee believes may be pertinent to the preparation of the tenta-
tive budget.
(d) The tentative budget shall include:
(A) Projected changes in the cost of personnel services, other services, supplies and
capital expenditures, based on:
(i) Anticipated expenses; and
(ii) Projections of roll-up costs provided by school districts that  represent at least two-
thirds of the number of weighted students (ADMw) in the most recent State School  Fund
calculations;
(B) Forecasted local revenues, as described in  ORS 327.011 and as calculated by the
Oregon Department of Administrative Services, the Legislative  Fiscal Officer, the Legislative
Revenue Officer and the Department of Revenue; and
(C) Projected cost factors that may arise from new laws and administrative rules.
(4) The Oregon Department of Administrative Services shall collaborate with the com-
mittee formed under subsection (2) of this section prior  to finalizing the tentative budget
required under this section.
(5) The Oregon Department of Administrative Services shall present the tentative  budget
prepared under this section to the Governor  and to the Legislative Assembly in conjunction
with the submittal of the agency request budget of the Department of Education.
