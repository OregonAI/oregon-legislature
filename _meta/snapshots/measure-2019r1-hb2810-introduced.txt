80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 2810
Sponsored by Representative NOSSE
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Oregon  Department of Administrative Services for
distribution to Historic Portland Public Market Foundation for  predevelopment expenses for James
Beard Public Market.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the James Beard Public Market; and declaring an emergency.
Whereas the James Beard  Public Market is a proposed permanent, daily, year-round, indoor-
outdoor food market and public gathering place in Portland; and
Whereas the market will feature a food hall with  more than 50 vendors that will showcase the
quality and diversity of Oregon’s farming and food economy; and
Whereas the market’s community center will feature a learning  kitchen and classes on cooking,
healthy eating and wellness; and
Whereas the  foundation seeking to establish the market expects to incur predevelopment costs
of $1.2 million in 2019 and 2020; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services,  for the biennium beginning July
1, 2019, out  of the General Fund, the amount of $400,000 for distribution to the Historic
Portland Public Market Foundation to be  used for predevelopment expenses for the James
Beard Public Market.
SECTION 2. This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2019 Act takes effect
July 1, 2019.
