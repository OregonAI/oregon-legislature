CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to the Oregon Promise program; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Notwithstanding ORS  341.522 (6), the Office of Student Access and Com-
pletion may award a total amount in grants under ORS 341.522 that exceeds $10  million per
fiscal year during the biennium ending on June 30, 2017.
SECTION 2. Notwithstanding any other law, the General Fund appropriation made to the
Higher Education Coordinating Commission by section 4, chapter 697, Oregon Laws 2015,  for
the creation and implementation of the Oregon Promise program is increased by $3,556,302.
SECTION 3. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency  is declared to exist, and this 2017 Act takes effect
on its passage.
