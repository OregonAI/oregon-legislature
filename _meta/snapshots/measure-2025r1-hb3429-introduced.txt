83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 3429
Sponsored by Representatives DIEHL, HELFRICH,  Senators THATCHER, STARR; Representatives JAVADI,
LEWIS, MANNIX, OWENS, SCHARF
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act puts  a time limit on states of emergency that the Governor declares. (Flesch
Readability Score: 65.7).
Provides that a state of emergency  declared by the Governor terminates after 60 days unless the
Legislative Assembly extends the state of emergency by joint resolution. Provides that such exten-
sions may not exceed 60 days.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the Governor’s emergency  powers authority; amending ORS 401.204; and prescribing an
effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
 ORS 401.204 is amended to read:
401.204. (1) The Governor shall terminate the state of  emergency by proclamation when the
emergency no longer exists, or when the threat of an emergency has passed.
(2) The state of emergency [ proclaimed] declared by the Governor may be terminated at  any
time by joint resolution of the Legislative Assembly.
(3) The state of emergency declared by the Governor  shall terminate 60 days after the
Governor’s proclamation under  ORS 401.165, unless the Legislative Assembly by joint resol-
ution authorizes an extension of the state of emergency. An  extension of a state of emer-
gency under this  subsection may not exceed 60 days’ duration, but an extension may be
reauthorized by passage of a joint resolution so providing in 60-day  increments, until the
Legislative Assembly determines that the emergency no longer exists or the  threat of an
emergency has passed.
SECTION 2. This 2025 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on which the 2025
regular session of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
