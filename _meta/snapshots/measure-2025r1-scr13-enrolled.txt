83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Concurrent Resolution 13
Sponsored by Senator NASH, Representative LEVY  B; Senators LIEBER, WEBER, Representatives
OWENS, SMITH G, WALTERS (Presession filed.)
Whereas the State of Oregon has traditionally recognized a variety of official state emblems  as
tangible representations of the proud spirit and heritage of this state; and
Whereas Oregon has designated an official state  nut, state beverage, state fruit, state vegetable
and even a state pie,  but has yet to designate a state steak to recognize the vital role of beef cattle
in Oregon’s development; and
Whereas cattle have been raised in Oregon since 1824; and
Whereas cattle and  calves were Oregon’s second-leading agricultural commodity in 2022, with
a total value of production of nearly $800 million; and
Whereas all  36 Oregon counties participate in producing cattle and have cattle within their
borders; and
Whereas Oregon’s reputation as a national leader in  agriculture is well deserved, and beef has
contributed greatly to this proud legacy; and
Whereas Oregon ranchers are  known for their respect for the land and their efforts to maintain
it for long-term cattle production; and
Whereas beef steak is a food that supports an active and healthy lifestyle, and it  is a source
of such essential nutrients as protein, zinc, B vitamins, iron and riboflavin; and
Whereas the T-bone is a remarkable steak that features two distinct cuts  of beef: the strip steak
(which packs a bold, beefy flavor and has a hearty texture) and the filet (which is renowned for its
exceptional tenderness and mild taste); and
Whereas there’s nothing quite like the mouthwatering smell  of a T-bone sizzling on a grill, then
you get to eat it; and
Whereas an ideal way to enjoy time with family or friends is to share a  meal that includes a
succulent Oregon T-bone paired with an Oregon baked potato or other local vegetables; and
Whereas embracing the excellent qualities of one steak does not diminish the  value of other
steaks; and
Whereas due  to the deep connection that beef has with the State of Oregon, and due to the
unique delectability of  the T-bone, it fully deserves the honor of being our state steak; now, there-
fore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That the T-bone is the official steak of the State of Oregon.
