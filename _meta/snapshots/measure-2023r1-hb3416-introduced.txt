82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 3416
Sponsored by Representatives SMITH G, MARSH, OWENS, GOMBERG
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys to  Oregon Business Development Department for distribution to recipients
for industrial lands projects.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to industrial lands projects; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There is appropriated to the Oregon Business  Development Department, for
the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, out of the General Fund, the amount of $24,500,000 for
the department to distribute according to the following schedule to be used by  each recipient
only for the respective industrial lands project there set forth:
