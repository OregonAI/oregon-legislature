SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act tells OHA to do a study about health care facilities. (Flesch  Readability Score:
67.7).
Requires the Oregon Health Authority to study protections in  health care facilities. Directs the
authority to submit findings  to the interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to health
no later than September 15, 2027.
Sunsets on January 2, 2028.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to protections in health care facilities.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Oregon Health Authority shall  study protections in health care facili-
ties. The authority shall submit a report in the manner provided by  ORS 192.245, and may
include recommendations for legislation, to the interim committees of the  Legislative As-
sembly related to health no later than September 15, 2027.
SECTION 2. Section 1 of this 2026 Act is repealed on January 2, 2028.
