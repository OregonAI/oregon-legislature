Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Land Use Board of Appeals for biennial expenses. 
 Limits biennial expenditures from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts, but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received by board. 
 Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.

## Full text

80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Bill 5028
Introduced and printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession  filed (at the request of Oregon
Department of Administrative Services)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to the financial administration  of the Land Use Board of Appeals; and declaring an emer-
gency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. There is appropriated to the Land Use Board of Appeals,  for the biennium
beginning July 1, 2019, out of the General Fund, the amount of $2,083,485.
SECTION 2. Notwithstanding any other law limiting expenditures,  the amount of $34,981
is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, as the maximum limit for payment
of expenses from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts, but  ex-
cluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received by the Land Use Board of  Ap-
peals.
SECTION 3.
This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency  is declared to exist, and this 2019 Act takes effect
July 1, 2019.
