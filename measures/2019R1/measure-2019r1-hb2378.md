## Full text

80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 2378
Sponsored by Representative SMITH DB (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires State Department  of Fish and Wildlife to establish and operate fish incubation box
program for rearing of salmonids on suitable  waterways in coastal areas of southern Oregon. Re-
quires department  to report to interim committee of Legislative Assembly no later than September
15, 2020, regarding establishment and operation of program.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to department for program purposes.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to incubation boxes for salmonids; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The State Department of  Fish and Wildlife shall establish and operate a fish
incubation box program for the rearing of salmonids  on salmon-bearing or potentially
salmon-bearing waterways located in the Counties of  Coos, Curry or Douglas and within
 miles of the Pacific Ocean.
SECTION 2. The State Department of Fish and Wildlife shall report on  the establishment
and operation of  the fish incubation box program required under section 1 of this 2019 Act,
including but not limited to any findings or  recommendations of the department regarding
the program, to an interim committee of  the Legislative Assembly relating to natural re-
sources in the manner provided by ORS 192.245 no later than September 15, 2020.
SECTION 3. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the State Department  of Fish and Wildlife, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019,
out of the General Fund, the  amount of $_______, which may be expended for establishing and
operating the fish incubation box program required under section 1 of this 2019 Act.
SECTION 4. This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2019 Act takes effect
July 1, 2019.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 1120
