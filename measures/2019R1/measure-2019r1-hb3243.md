## Full text

80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 3243
Sponsored by Representative NEARMAN
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates funds to State  Parks and Recreation Department to distribute to Oregon State Fair
Council to fund assessment of fairground facilities and maintenance, improvement  and enhancement
of fairground facilities.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to funding for the Oregon State Fair Council; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the  State Parks and Recreation Department, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019,
out of  the General Fund, the amount of $_______, which shall be distributed to the Oregon
State Fair Council to fund:
(a) An assessment of fairground facilities; and
(b) Maintenance, improvement and enhancement of the fairground facilities.
SECTION 2.
This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2019 Act takes effect
July 1, 2019.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 3627
