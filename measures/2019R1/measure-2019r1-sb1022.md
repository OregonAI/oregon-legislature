## Full text

80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
Senate Bill 1022
Sponsored by Senator FREDERICK, Representative SOLLMAN
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Oregon Department of Administrative Services to prepare tentative  budget for funding
of State School Fund. Requires department to form committee for purpose of preparing tentative
budget. Prescribes factors to be considered by committee.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the method for funding public education.
Whereas funding for kindergarten through grade 12 public education now comprises  nearly half
of the state’s General Fund budget; and
Whereas the primary source of funding for kindergarten  through grade 12 public education
comes from appropriations made  by the Legislative Assembly from the General Fund to the State
School Fund; and
Whereas the Legislative  Assembly must develop biennial state budgets based on available re-
sources; and
Whereas the state budgeting process depends  on an accurate analysis of current resources and
uncontrollable cost drivers; and
Whereas the public relies on fiscal analyses to describe public education fiscal facts  with the
greatest accuracy possible; and
Whereas the calculation  process for determining the current service level for public education
that is prescribed by the Governor’s Executive Order 14-14 has introduced instability to public  ed-
ucation funding and relies, in part,  on policies and organizational entities that are no longer in force
or no longer exist; and
Whereas confusion about  the meaning of the current service level and confusion about the level
itself have impacted the stability of school budgets; and
Whereas the  state’s calculation of a budget for kindergarten through grade 12 public education
based on current service levels has not  and cannot be developed at the level of detail as for other
state programs; and
Whereas the Quality Education Commission calculates the  gap between baseline funding for
public education and  its recommended full-funding level for public education based on the current
service level calculation; and
Whereas policymakers rely on the accuracy of the current service level calculation to  gauge
whether proposed appropriation levels will result in stable school budgets; and
Whereas the State of Oregon has an interest in directing the process for determining the current
service level for kindergarten through grade 12 public schools based on accurate  information and
collaboration among all key entities involved in the school finance process; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 4305
SECTION 1. Section 2 of this  2019 Act is added to and made a part of ORS 327.006 to
327.133.
SECTION 2. (1) Each even-numbered  year, the Oregon Department of Administrative
Services shall prepare a tentative budget for funding the State  School Fund for the next
biennium.
(2)(a) For the purpose of preparing the tentative budget, the department  shall form a
committee. The committee must include:
(A) Representatives from the Department  of Education, the Legislative Fiscal Office, the
Legislative Revenue Officer and the Governor’s office;
(B) Representatives of school  districts, school budget committees, school business offi-
cials and education labor unions; and
(C) The cochairs of the Joint Committee on Ways and Means.
(b) The representatives from the Legislative Fiscal  Office and the Legislative Revenue
Officer and the cochairs of the  Joint Committee on Ways and Means shall serve as nonvoting
members on the committee formed under this subsection.
(3) In preparing the tentative  budget, the Oregon Department of Administrative Services
shall review with the committee formed under subsection (2) of this section  the components
of the methodology  that will be used to prepare the tentative budget and the calculations for
the tentative budget. The department shall collaborate with  the committee to refine the
methodology by considering alternative approaches to the calculations and other factors the
committee believes may be pertinent to the preparation of the tentative budget. The  de-
partment shall collaborate with  the committee prior to finalizing the tentative budget re-
quired under this section. The tentative budget shall include:
(a) Projected changes in the cost of  personal services, other services, supplies and capital
expenditures, based on anticipated expenses;
(b) Forecasted local revenues, as described in ORS 327.011 and as calculated by  the
Oregon Department of Administrative Services, the Legislative  Fiscal Officer, the Legislative
Revenue Officer and the Department of Revenue; and
(c) Projected cost factors that may arise from new laws and administrative rules.
(4) The Oregon Department of Administrative Services shall present the tentative  budget
prepared under this section to the Governor and  to the Legislative Assembly in conjunction
with submittal of the agency request budget of the Department of Education.
