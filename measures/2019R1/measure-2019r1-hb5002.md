Appropriates moneys from General Fund to State Department of Agriculture for certain biennial expenses. 
 Limits certain biennial expenditures from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts and certain federal funds, but excluding lottery funds and other federal funds, collected or received by department. 
 Limits biennial expenditures by department from lottery moneys. 
 Limits biennial expenditures by department from federal funds. 
 Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2019.

80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Bill 5002
Introduced and printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession  filed (at the request of Oregon
Department of Administrative Services)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to the financial administration of the State Department  of Agriculture; and declaring an
emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. There are appropriated to the State Department  of Agriculture, for the
biennium beginning July 1, 2019, out of the General Fund, the following amounts, for the
following purposes:
(1) Administrative and support
services ..................................... $ 2,371,317
(2) Food safety ............................... $ 9,525,461
(3) Natural resources ..................... $ 9,036,100
(4) Market access ........................... $ 3,374,998
SECTION 2. Notwithstanding any other law limiting  expenditures, the following amounts
are established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, as the maximum limits for payment
of expenses from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts,  and in-
cluding federal funds for contract  services, but excluding lottery funds and federal funds not
described in this section, collected or received by the  State Department of Agriculture, for
the following purposes:
(1) Administrative and support
services ..................................... $ 10,528,380
(2) Food safety ............................... $ 28,366,219
(3) Natural resources ..................... $ 18,296,913
(4) Market access ........................... $ 17,162,536
SECTION 3. Notwithstanding any other law limiting expenditures,  the amount of
$10,636,822 is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, as the maximum limit  for
payment of expenses from lottery  moneys allocated from the Parks and Natural Resources
Fund to the  State Department of Agriculture for the Oregon Plan, to be used for activities
and projects to implement Article XV, section 4b, of the Oregon Constitution.
SECTION 4. Notwithstanding any other law limiting  expenditures, the following amounts
are established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2019, as the maximum limits for  payment
of expenses from  federal funds other than those described in section 2 of this 2019 Act col-
lected or received by the State Department of Agriculture, for the following purposes:
(1) Administrative and support
services ..................................... $ 188,371

SECTION 5. This 2019 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist, and  this 2019 Act takes effect
