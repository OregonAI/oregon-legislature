## Full text

80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2019 Regular Session
House Bill 2570
Sponsored by  Representative NATHANSON, Senator HANSELL, Representatives SMITH G, NOBLE, STARK, Sen-
ator GELSER; Representatives ALONSO LEON, BARKER, BONHAM, DOHERTY, GOMBERG,  GORSEK,
HELT, HERNANDEZ, HOLVEY, LEIF, LIVELY, MARSH, MCLAIN, MEEK,  NERON, NOSSE, POWER,
SALINAS, SMITH DB, SOLLMAN, WILDE,  WITT, Senators BENTZ, BEYER, BOQUIST, DEMBROW,
FREDERICK, HEARD, KNOPP, LINTHICUM, MANNING JR, MONNES ANDERSON,  PROZANSKI, RILEY,
ROBLAN, THATCHER, THOMSEN, WAGNER (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys to Oregon Department of Administrative Services  to increase size and di-
versity of group of court appointed special advocates.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to court appointed special advocates.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Oregon Department of  Administrative Services, for the biennium beginning July
1, 2019, out of the General Fund, the amount of $8,337,637 to be expended for the CASA Vol-
unteer Program, as defined in ORS 419A.004, to recruit, train and supervise a larger  and
more diverse group of court appointed special advocates, as defined in ORS 419A.004.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 2162
