## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2024 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Concurrent Resolution 201
Sponsored by Senator WAGNER; Senator MANNING JR (Presession filed.)
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That the 2024 regular session of the Senate and the House of Representatives of the  Eighty-
second Legislative Assembly is adjourned sine die.
Adopted by Senate March 7, 2024
Obadiah Rutledge, Secretary of Senate
Rob Wagner, President of Senate
Adopted by House March 7, 2024
Julie Fahey, Speaker of House
Enrolled Senate Concurrent Resolution 201 (SCR 201-INTRO) Page 1
