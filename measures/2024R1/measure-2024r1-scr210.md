## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2024 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Concurrent Resolution 210
Sponsored by Senators WAGNER, HANSELL, FREDERICK; Senators JAMA, MANNING JR,  Rep-
resentative LEVY B (Presession filed.)
Whereas Mike Donahue was born in Albany, Oregon, in 1946; and
Whereas Mike Donahue grew up in  Albany and attended Albany Union High School, where he
was elected student body president; and
Whereas following high school graduation, Mike Donahue attended  and graduated from the
University of Oregon with a degree in journalism; and
Whereas Mike Donahue took a job as a reporter for KOIN-TV in  Portland at the age of 22, but
soon thereafter enlisted in the United States Army; and
Whereas while in the Army, Mike Donahue  honed his skills as a writer and reporter by serving
in the Pentagon in Washington, D.C.; and
Whereas Mike Donahue returned to KOIN-TV in 1972, where his talent and  news writing skills
soon found him elevated to the position of lead news anchor; and
Whereas Mike Donahue delivered the news to Oregonians with a  commitment to accuracy, sig-
nificance, context and fairness; and
Whereas Mike Donahue represented true  excellence in journalism and was an inspiration to his
fellow Portland news colleagues over the years, including current  and former Oregon State Senators
Lew Frederick, Mark Hass and Rick Metsger; and
Whereas Mike Donahue covered thousands of stories during his career, including the founding
of the Oregon Department of Environmental Quality, the eruption  of Mount St. Helens, the
Rajneeshee movement in Wasco County and takeover  of the Wasco County town of Antelope by the
Rajneeshees, seven presidential elections and  countless other events of significant contemporary and
historical significance; and
Whereas for over 40 years, Oregonians  relied on Mike Donahue as their nightly source of the
news and events that impacted their lives; and
Whereas when Mike Donahue was asked if he ever considered running for office, he replied,  “I
would rather report the news than be the news”; and
Whereas Mike Donahue was the  longest serving journalist in the history of Portland television
news; and
Whereas Mike Donahue retired in 2012 and  dedicated himself to his family, his church and his
community; and
Whereas Mike Donahue loved Oregon, his television viewers, his  rose garden and the New York
Yankees; and
Whereas Mike Donahue lived by the motto that, in reporting the news, he  would rather be right
than be first; and
Whereas Mike Donahue lost his beloved wife, Susan, just a few months before his own passing;
and
Whereas Mike Donahue and his wife Susan  raised two daughters, Joy and Noelle, in their
Portland area home; and
Whereas Mike Donahue passed away on August 25,  2023, after a courageous bout with cancer;
and
Whereas Mike Donahue’s passion, dedication and attention to  detail earned him the nickname
of “the Walter Cronkite of Oregon”; now, therefore,
Enrolled Senate Concurrent Resolution 210 (SCR 210-INTRO) Page 1
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we,  the members of the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly, honor and celebrate the re-
markable life and career of Mike Donahue  and express our gratitude for Mike Donahue’s service to
the people of the State of Oregon; and be it further
Resolved, That a copy  of this resolution shall be presented to the family of Mike Donahue as
an expression of our sympathy and condolences.
Adopted by Senate February 20, 2024
Obadiah Rutledge, Secretary of Senate
Rob Wagner, President of Senate
Adopted by House February 26, 2024
Dan Rayfield, Speaker of House
Enrolled Senate Concurrent Resolution 210 (SCR 210-INTRO) Page 2
