## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2024 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Concurrent Resolution 203
Sponsored by Senators WAGNER, FREDERICK; Senators JAMA, MANNING JR (Presession filed.)
Whereas Alice Bartelt was born  in Norfolk, Virginia, on August 27, 1947, to Ruth and Gene
Parkhurst; and
Whereas as the daughter of a career  Navy man, Alice Bartelt lived on the island of Cuba for
several years before  moving to San Diego, California, where she attended elementary school and
high school and met her future husband, Richard “Dick” Alan Bartelt; and
Whereas Alice and Dick Bartelt eloped  and were married in 1966 in San Francisco, California;
and
Whereas Alice Bartelt earned her undergraduate degree from San Francisco State University;
and
Whereas Alice and  Dick Bartelt moved to Portland, Oregon, in 1970, where they raised a family
and where Alice  put into earnest action her lifelong passion for supporting local, national and global
women’s rights organizations; and
Whereas Alice Bartelt  graduated with a law degree from Lewis and Clark Law School in 1981
and practiced workers’  compensation law for 25 years before retiring in March 2007 from SAIF
Corporation, where she served as a staff attorney; and
Whereas Alice Bartelt was a Professional Registered Parliamentarian and a member  of the Na-
tional Association of Parliamentarians and the American Institute of Parliamentarians; and
Whereas Alice  Bartelt served as a parliamentarian for numerous organizations, including the
American  Association  of University Women of Oregon, the Democratic Party of Oregon, the
Episcopal Diocese of Oregon, the League  of Women Voters of Oregon, the Oregon Association of
Parliamentarians, the Oregon Republican  Party, the Sons of Norway, the Washington County Dem-
ocrats, WomenFirst and Zonta International; and
Whereas Alice Bartelt was an active  member of, and served on the boards of, the American
Association of University Women (Oregon president,  1998-2000), the American Institute of
Parliamentarians, Americans United  for Separation of Church and State, the Diversity Section of the
Oregon State Bar, the League of Women Voters of Oregon (Advocacy Chair, 2011-2023,  and presi-
dent from 2023 until her death), Pro-Choice Oregon, the Oregon  Association of Parliamentarians,
Oregon Women Lawyers, WomenFirst and Zonta International (from 2016 until her death); and
Whereas Alice Bartelt  served faithfully at the Episcopal Parish of St. John the Baptist in
Portland as an acolyte and reader in worship and as a stalwart volunteer and financial  contributor
to the church’s social ministries, especially in its material support and justice  advocacy for the
people of Western Farm Workers Association in Hillsboro, Oregon; and
Whereas Alice Bartelt was a committed voice for democracy and for voting rights; and
Whereas Alice  Bartelt was always willing to step in to fill a need, to serve as a mentor and to
share her limitless knowledge of Robert’s Rules of Order and parliamentary procedures; and
Whereas Alice Bartelt  spent tens of thousands of hours over the course of her remarkable life
volunteering for the causes dear to her heart; and
Whereas Alice Bartelt died on August 29, 2023; and
Whereas Alice Bartelt is survived  by her daughters Barb Damon and Sandy Meyers; grandchil-
dren Scott and Hayden Meyers; sister Karen Farrington and her children; and her longtime  partner,
Paul Moore; now, therefore,
Enrolled Senate Concurrent Resolution 203 (SCR 203-INTRO) Page 1
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of  the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly, celebrate the life and accom-
plishments of Alice Bartelt, and we recognize and honor her contributions to the people of this state;
and be it further
Resolved, That a copy of this resolution shall be presented to the family of  Alice Bartelt as an
expression of our sympathy and condolences.
Adopted by Senate February 28, 2024
Obadiah Rutledge, Secretary of Senate
Rob Wagner, President of Senate
Adopted by House March 5, 2024
Dan Rayfield, Speaker of House
