## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2024 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Bill 4033
Introduced and printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the request of House In-
terim Committee on Revenue for Representative Nancy Nathanson)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to long term care facilities; amending sections 23 and 31, chapter 736, Oregon Laws 2003.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
 Section 23, chapter 736, Oregon Laws 2003, as amended  by section 8, chapter 757,
Oregon Laws 2005, section 11, chapter 780,  Oregon Laws 2007, section 19, chapter 608, Oregon Laws
2013, and section 2, chapter 66, Oregon Laws 2018, is amended to read:
Sec. 23.  [ Sections 15 to 22, chapter 736, Oregon Laws 2003, ] ORS 409.800 to 409.816 and 409.900
apply to  long term care facility assessments imposed in calendar quarters beginning on or after
November 26, 2003, and before [ July 1, 2026 ] June 30, 2032 .
SECTION 2.  Section 31, chapter 736, Oregon Laws 2003, as amended  by section 9, chapter 757,
Oregon Laws 2005, section 14, chapter 780, Oregon  Laws 2007, section 49, chapter 11, Oregon Laws
2009, section 21, chapter 608, Oregon Laws 2013, and section 3, chapter 66, Oregon  Laws 2018, is
amended to read:
Sec. 31.  [ Sections 15 to 22, 24 and  29, chapter 736, Oregon Laws 2003, ] ORS 409.800 to 409.816
and 409.900 are repealed on January 2, [ 2028] 2034.
