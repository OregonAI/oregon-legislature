## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2024 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Concurrent Resolution 206
Sponsored by Senator WAGNER; Senators JAMA, MANNING JR, Representative  HIEB (Presession
filed.)
Whereas Fred W. Heard was born in Prineville, Oregon, on September 9, 1940; and
Whereas Fred Heard was raised in Klamath Falls,  Oregon, where he attended elementary school
and high school; and
Whereas after high school, Fred Heard  attended the University of Oregon, then received his
Bachelor of Science in Education and his Master of Science in Secondary Education from  Southern
Oregon College (now Southern Oregon University) in Ashland, Oregon; and
Whereas after college, Fred Heard began a long  and fruitful career in education, first as a
teacher at his alma mater, Klamath Union High School; and
Whereas in his time as an educator, Fred Heard  filled positions as diverse as teacher, depart-
ment chair, budget manager, assistant professor, vice principal and principal; and
Whereas as a teacher and assistant professor in junior high  school, high school and college,
Fred Heard taught English, literature, social studies, speech, sociology,  economics and journalism;
and
Whereas in 1968, Fred Heard first ran  for a seat in the Oregon Legislative Assembly, beginning
another chapter of his life with a keen focus on service to the people of Oregon; and
Whereas for the next 14 years, Fred Heard served the people of Oregon faithfully, first as a
member of the House  of Representatives representing Klamath and Lake Counties from 1969 to 1972,
then as a member of the Senate representing Klamath and Deschutes  Counties from 1972 to 1983;
and
Whereas Fred Heard held the post of Senate Majority Leader from 1975 to 1980; and
Whereas Fred Heard was elected Senate President in 1981; and
Whereas as a member of  the Legislative Assembly, Fred Heard worked across party and re-
gional lines to enact legislation that improved the lives of countless Oregonians; and
Whereas Fred Heard resigned his position in the Senate in 1983  to accept the cabinet appoint-
ment of Director of Commerce for Governor Victor Atiyeh, where he  supervised nine divisions, 24
licensing boards and 450 employees; and
Whereas Fred Heard also embarked on a career in ministry,  receiving a Master of Divinity from
Church Divinity School of the Pacific in Berkeley, California; and
Whereas Fred Heard completed his Clinical Pastoral Education at the Oregon State  Hospital
Forensics Unit and his field experience at Prince of Peace in Salem, Oregon; and
Whereas Fred Heard served as interim minister at the Congregational Church in Klamath  Falls,
Oregon, from 1963 to 1966; and
Whereas Fred Heard was ordained  as a transitional deacon in June 2003 at St. Paul’s Episcopal
Church in Salem, and as a  priest at Grace Cathedral in San Francisco, California, in December 2003;
and
Whereas Fred Heard was the associate rector at Trinity Church  in Menlo Park, California, from
July 2003 to July 2008; and
Whereas Fred Heard served as rector at St. Paul’s in Cambria, California, from January  2009
to September 2014; and
Whereas Fred Heard was assisting priest at St. Paul’s  in Salem from September 2014 to De-
cember 2015; and
Enrolled Senate Concurrent Resolution 206 (SCR 206-INTRO) Page 1
Whereas in his time in the Episcopal Diocese of Oregon, Fred Heard was Dean of the Willamette
Convocation,  a  member of the Diocesan Recovery Commission, a member of the Diocesan Council
and a member of the board for Formation and Mission; and
Whereas Fred Heard began his  service as vicar at St. Thomas’ Episcopal Church in Dallas,
Oregon, in January 2016, and retired in June 2022; and
Whereas over the years, Fred Heard was  an active member of the Kiwanis and served as a
board member in both Menlo Park and Salem, as president in Salem, and as a  delegate to the
international convention; and
Whereas in his spare time, Fred Heard loved to  garden, and kept his eye on political happenings
with his faithful cat, Apple; and
Whereas as  a political scientist, Fred Heard was fascinated by the evolution of politics over the
last several years, but as a politician, he was saddened by the  breakdown of decorum, tradition,
compromise and camaraderie; and
Whereas in his retirement, Fred Heard was working on his autobiography, which included  so-
cial, political and religious commentary as well as reflections on his life and his family; and
Whereas Fred Heard was known by some as Mr. Heard, Senator Heard, Father Heard, Dad or
Papa, and by all as a deeply dedicated servant to his fellow man; and
Whereas Fred Heard was a mentor,  a mediator and a forward thinker who approached chal-
lenges with hope and an eye toward finding solutions; and
Whereas Fred Heard was so very proud of his family, and he loved to tell  of the accomplish-
ments of his children and grandchildren; and
Whereas Fred Heard passed away on August 1, 2023; and
Whereas Fred  Heard is survived by his loving wife of 57 years, Adair; his son, Frederick; his
twin daughters, Robin and Heather; his Japanese exchange son, Hiro; and  his grandchildren
Christopher, Matthew, Carter, Jackson, Sawyer, Harrison and Edith; and
Whereas Fred  Heard will always be remembered as a tireless advocate for his students, his
constituents, his parishioners and his family; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly, celebrate  the life and accom-
plishments of Fred W. Heard, and we  recognize and honor his contributions to the people of this
state and to all those whose lives he touched; and be it further
Resolved, That a copy of this resolution shall be presented to  the family of Fred W. Heard as
an expression of our sympathy and condolences.
Enrolled Senate Concurrent Resolution 206 (SCR 206-INTRO) Page 2
Adopted by Senate February 27, 2024
Obadiah Rutledge, Secretary of Senate
Rob Wagner, President of Senate
Adopted by House March 4, 2024
Dan Rayfield, Speaker of House
