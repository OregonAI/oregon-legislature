## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2024 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Concurrent Resolution 209
Sponsored by Senator WAGNER,  Representative BOICE; Senators JAMA, KNOPP, PATTERSON,
SMITH DB, Representatives EVANS, GOMBERG, HOLVEY (Presession filed.)
Whereas Joanne Russell Verger was born in Amite, Louisiana, on July  19, 1930, to Robert
Weldon Russell and Nannie Claire Russell (Lingo); and
Whereas as the last of four children, Joanne Verger’s  upbringing in the throes of the Great
Depression left an indelible mark on her life; and
Whereas growing  up in the Deep South contributed to a rich and full childhood that Joanne
Verger would recall fondly later in life; and
Whereas Joanne Verger’s grandfather was a Methodist minister,  and he imprinted on her a
devout sense of faith, modesty and incredible spiritual and personal discipline; and
Whereas Joanne Verger was an excellent student in high school and  graduated at the age of
16; and
Whereas after high school, Joanne Verger enrolled  at Northwestern State College in Louisiana,
where she excelled as a thespian and Shakespearean actress, starring as  Helena in A Midsummer
Night’s Dream;  and
Whereas while in college, Joanna Verger reconnected with her childhood  friend Lawton, whom
she first met when she was in third grade; and
Whereas Joanne and Lawton Verger were married on November 19, 1950; and
Whereas Joanne  and Lawton Verger later moved to Baton Rouge, Louisiana, where Lawton took
a job as a Pontiac salesman; and
Whereas Joanne and Lawton  Verger welcomed their first child, Kathy, in 1952, followed by their
sons Jim and John in 1955 and 1957 and their daughter Anne Adair in 1960; and
Whereas in 1969, Joanne and Lawton Verger moved  to Coos Bay, Oregon, where they took
ownership of an automobile dealership; and
Whereas Joanne Verger became the tourism director for the City of Coos Bay and later helped
create the Bay Area Fun Festival and the Blackberry Arts Festival; and
Whereas Joanne Verger won a seat on the Coos Bay City Council in 1990; and
Whereas Joanne Verger was elected the City of Coos Bay’s first female mayor in 1992; and
Whereas Joanne Verger was  elected to the Oregon Legislative Assembly in 2000, where she
served as a member of the House  of Representatives from 2001 to 2005 and as a member of the
Senate from 2005 to 2013; and
Whereas Joanne Verger was a tireless advocate for her community  and a champion for rural
Oregonians everywhere; and
Whereas Joanne Verger represented the true spirit of bipartisanship, working across  party lines
to create a better Oregon; and
Whereas Joanne Verger served the  State of Oregon with dignity, honesty, kindness and resolve;
and
Whereas Joanne  Verger wrote two books, Dancing with the Daffodils  and Moonlight on the Pond,
reflecting on her childhood memories in the Deep South; and
Whereas Joanne Verger passed away at the age of 93 on September 22, 2023; and
Whereas Joanne Verger  is survived by her four children and their spouses, Jim and Diane
Verger, Kathy and Jerry Muscus, Anne Johnson, and John and Yvette Verger; her  four grandchil-
dren, Aaron Johnson, Logan Reeder, James Verger and Nissa Thompson; and her  great-grandchild,
Alexandra Verger; now, therefore,
Enrolled Senate Concurrent Resolution 209 (SCR 209-INTRO) Page 1
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of  the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly, celebrate the life and accom-
plishments of Joanne Russell  Verger, and we recognize and honor her contributions to the people
of this state; and be it further
Resolved, That we share a deep sense of loss with the entire Capitol community  in the absence
of this remarkable public servant; and be it further
Resolved, That a copy of this resolution shall be  presented to the family of Joanne Russell
Verger as an expression of our sympathy and condolences.
Adopted by Senate March 5, 2024
Obadiah Rutledge, Secretary of Senate
Rob Wagner, President of Senate
Adopted by House March 7, 2024
Dan Rayfield, Speaker of House
Enrolled Senate Concurrent Resolution 209 (SCR 209-INTRO) Page 2
