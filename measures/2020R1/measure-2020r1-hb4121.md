## Full text

80th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2020 Regular Session
House Bill 4121
Sponsored by Representatives BONHAM, WILLIAMS (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Provides that Northern Oregon  Regional Correctional Facility may not contract with United
States Immigration and Customs Enforcement if Legislative Assembly appropriates, for  distribution
to facility, specified amount of moneys.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to correctional facility contracts.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
If the Legislative Assembly for  any biennium appropriates to the Depart-
ment of Corrections, for distribution to the Northern Oregon Regional Correctional  Facility,
at least $1,640,000, the  Northern Oregon Regional Correctional Facility may not renew or
renegotiate a contract, or negotiate a new contract,  with United States Immigration and
Customs Enforcement during that biennium.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 147
