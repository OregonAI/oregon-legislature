**Relating to:** Relating to land use; and declaring an emergency.

Digest: This Act amends temp UGB program and lets LCDC award grants. (Flesch Readability Score: 80.3). 
 Expands eligibility for cities and Metro to amend their urban growth boundaries under a temporary program. 
 <b>Provides an alternative pathway within the temporary program by which the City of Woodburn may amend its urban growth boundary.</b> 
 Authorizes the Land Conservation and Development Commission to issue grants to implement the commission's duties. 
 Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
