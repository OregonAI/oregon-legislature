## Summary

**Catchline:** Permits a person who will be 17 years of age on the date of a primary election and 18 years of age on the date of the general election to vote at the primary election for candidates of a major political party with which the person is affiliated.

**Relating to:** Relating to voting at primary elections.

Digest: The Act allows certain persons who are 17 years old on the date of a primary to vote in the primary. (Flesch Readability Score: 68.6). 
 Permits a person who will be 17 years of age on the date of a primary election and 18 years of age on the date of the general election to vote at the primary election for candidates of a major political party with which the person is affiliated. 
 Appropriates moneys from the General Fund to the Secretary of State for the purpose of printing and counting additional ballots.
