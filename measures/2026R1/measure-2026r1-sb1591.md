83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2026 Regular Session
Senate Bill 1591
Sponsored by Senator BROADMAN, Representative BREESE-IVERSON, Senator  MCLANE; Representatives
KROPF, LEVY B, SMITH G (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act tells OBDD to study the creation of a new inland port. (Flesch  Readability
Score: 89.5).
Requires the Oregon Business Development Department  to study the legislative establishment
of an inland port for the purposes of coordinating infrastructure development, improving trade  mo-
bility and promoting economic development.
Directs the department to submit findings to the interim committees  of the Legislative Assembly
related to economic development no later than September 15, 2027.
Sunsets on January 2, 2028.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to an inland port; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The  Oregon Business Development Department shall conduct a study
regarding the legislative  establishment of an inland port in one or more of the counties of
Deschutes, Jefferson and Crook, to be known as the  Port of Central Oregon, the purposes
of which are to coordinate infrastructure development, improve trade  mobility and promote
economic development.
(2) The study must include recommendations regarding:
(a) A governing body for the port;
(b) Geographic boundaries of the port;
(c) Establishment of a foreign trade zone within the port boundaries;
(d) Budgeting and financing, including bonding authority and taxation authority;
(e) Additional powers, not available to ports generally under ORS chapter 777, that  are
necessary or convenient for the Port of Central Oregon to effectuate its purposes;
(f) Methods to ensure efficient  use of resources and infrastructure, including through the
application of industrial symbiosis principles;
(g) Methods to promote economic development and multimodal freight systems;
(h) Coordination with federally recognized Indian tribes in this state; and
(i) Potential ownership and operational structures relating to:
(A) The emergency response and training facility in  Deschutes County known as CORE3;
(B) The Bend Municipal Airport;
(C) The area known as Juniper Ridge near Bend, Oregon; and
(D) Any other existing or future locations or facilities  within port boundaries that may
be used to further the purposes of the port.
(3) The department shall submit a report in the manner provided  by ORS 192.245, and

SECTION 2.
Section 1 of this 2026 Act is repealed on January 2, 2028.
SECTION 3. This 2026 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on  which the 2026
regular session of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
