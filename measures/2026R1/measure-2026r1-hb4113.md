## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2026 Regular Session
House Bill 4113
Sponsored by Representative  HELM; Representatives BREESE-IVERSON, LEVY E, Senator ANDERSON (Preses-
sion filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: This Act makes DLCD study housing credits. (Flesch Readability Score: 66.7).
Requires the  Department of Land Conservation and Development to study housing development
opportunities conditioned upon land conservation.  Directs the department to submit findings to the
interim committees of the Legislative  Assembly related to land use no later than September 15, 2027.
Sunsets on January 2, 2028.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to housing development opportunities conditioned upon land conservation.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The Department of Land  Conservation and Development shall study housing
development opportunities conditioned upon land conservation. The department shall submit
a report in the manner provided by ORS 192.245, and  may include recommendations for leg-
islation, to the interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to land use no later
than September 15, 2027.
SECTION 2.
Section 1 of this 2026 Act is repealed on January 2, 2028.

New sections are in  boldfaced type.
