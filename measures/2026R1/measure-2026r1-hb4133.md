## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2026 Regular Session
House Bill 4133
Sponsored by Representative ELMER (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Tells state  agencies to help the state to meet federal laws that fund education. (Flesch
Readability  Score: 65.7).
Requires participation by the state in  a federal program that allows tax credits for contributions
by individuals to scholarship granting organizations.
Provides directives to  the Governor and state agencies to ensure compliance with the federal
Workforce Pell Grant program.
Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to alignment with federal education law; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Department of Education, on behalf  of the State of Oregon, shall
elect to participate in the federal program that allows tax credits for contributions by indi-
viduals to scholarship granting organizations, as provided by section 70411 of Public Law
119-21.
(2) The department shall:
(a) No  later than January 1 of each year, produce a list of scholarship granting organ-
izations in this  state that meet the requirements described in section 70411(c)(5) of Public
Law 119-21, and:
(A) Submit the list to the Secretary of the Treasury of the United States; and
(B) Publish the list on the department’s website.
(b) Certify that the  department has the authority to submit the list on behalf of the State
of Oregon, as provided by this section.
(c) Accept applications from scholarship granting organizations throughout the year  for
inclusion on the list produced pursuant to paragraph (a) of this subsection.
SECTION 2.
(1) As used in this  section, “eligible workforce training programs” means
programs that meet applicable requirements in section 83002 of Public Law 119-21.
(2) The Governor, in consultation with the State Workforce  and Talent Development
Board, shall approve all workforce training programs that meet the requirements of an  eli-
gible workforce training program.
(3) The State Workforce and Talent Development Board shall:
(a) Coordinate approval of eligible workforce training programs with other state and
federal workforce or training programs.
(b) Align and leverage available state and federal funds to support the  implementation
and expansion of eligible workforce training programs.
SECTION 3.
This 2026 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
