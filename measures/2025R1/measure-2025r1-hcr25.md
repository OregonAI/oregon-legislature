## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Concurrent Resolution 25
Sponsored by Representative RUIZ, Senator MANNING JR
Whereas Carol Rulla began attending Kelly Creek Neighborhood Association  meetings in 2005,
and shortly after, her passion for solving problems and  commitment to supporting those who were
unaccustomed to speaking up for themselves saw her elected president of the association; and
Whereas Carol Rulla led the Kelly Creek Neighborhood Association for three  years before she
began serving as its land use director, a role she remains passionate about to this day; and
Whereas in 2006, Carol Rulla was elected vice president of the Coalition of Gresham  Neighbor-
hood Associations, and a year later, she was voted president of the coalition; and
Whereas Carol Rulla has spent two decades uplifting, informing and advocating  for the neigh-
borhoods of Gresham and helping to provide agency  to all who live in the communities she serves;
and
Whereas Carol Rulla’s  drive to help her neighbors across Gresham gain knowledge and under-
stand how to advocate effectively has  helped strengthen the Coalition of Gresham Neighborhood
Associations into a cohesive community of neighborhoods and a respected,  trusted voice at Gresham
City Hall and within the community; and
Whereas whether she is wading into the  murky world of land use rules and city code, creating
concept plans for Gresham’s undeveloped parks or improving the  city’s sustainability through her
involvement with the Gresham Repair Cafe, Carol Rulla’s tireless  devotion to her community has
significantly improved the quality of life for all those who call Gresham home; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members  of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly, recognize and honor Carol Rulla
for her remarkable contributions to Gresham and to the people of this state.
Enrolled House Concurrent Resolution 25 (HCR 25-A) Page 1
Adopted by House April 28, 2025
Timothy G. Sekerak, Chief Clerk of House
Julie Fahey, Speaker of House
Adopted by Senate May 22, 2025
Rob Wagner, President of Senate
Enrolled House Concurrent Resolution 25 (HCR 25-A) Page 2
