## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Senate Bill 508
Sponsored by Senator SMITH DB (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: Tells an insurer that it may not  use a device or technology to track where a person is
when the person is driving. (Flesch Readability Score: 65.7).
Prohibits insurers from using any device or  technology that records, discloses or allows access
to information that tracks or reveals the geographic location  of an insured during any time in which
the insured is  occupying or operating a motor vehicle that the motor vehicle liability insurance
policy covers. Specifies exceptions.
Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to prohibiting geographic tracking in connection with motor vehicle  liability insurance
policies; creating new provisions; amending ORS 742.449; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
 ORS 742.449 is amended to read:
742.449. (1) An  insurer [ issuing] that issues  motor vehicle liability insurance policies in this
state may not :
(a) Assign an insured or applicant for insurance  to a higher risk category than the [ person]
insured or applicant  would otherwise be assigned  to solely because the [ person] insured or ap-
plicant has:
[(1)] (A)  Let a prior motor vehicle liability policy lapse, unless the  [ person] insured or appli-
cant was in violation of ORS 806.010 at any time after the prior policy lapsed; or
[(2)] (B)  Had driving privileges suspended [ pursuant to ] under ORS 809.280 (6) or  (8) if the sus-
pension is based on a nondriving offense.
(b) Use or require the use of any device  or technology that records, discloses or allows
access to information that tracks or  reveals the geographic location of an insured during any
time in which the insured is occupying or operating a motor vehicle that the motor vehicle
liability insurance policy covers.
(2) Notwithstanding the prohibition in subsection (1)(b) of this  section, an insurer may
obtain and use information that  reveals the geographic location of an insured solely for the
purpose of providing emergency services or roadside assistance to an insured if  under the
motor vehicle liability insurance policy the insurer provides or agrees with  another person
to provide such services or assistance and the insured affirmatively consents  to the use of
the information for the purpose of receiving emergency services or roadside assistance.
SECTION 2. The amendments to ORS 742.449 by section 1 of this 2025 Act apply to motor
vehicle liability insurance policies that an insurer  issues or renews on or after the effective
date of this 2025 Act.
