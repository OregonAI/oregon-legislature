## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Concurrent Resolution 14
Sponsored by Representative FAHEY (Presession filed.)
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That the organizational session of the  Senate and the House of Representatives of the Eighty-
third Legislative Assembly is adjourned. The regular  session of the Eighty-third Legislative Assem-
bly shall convene on January 21, 2025.
Adopted by House January 13, 2025
Timothy G. Sekerak, Chief Clerk of House
Julie Fahey, Speaker of House
Adopted by Senate January 13, 2025
Rob Wagner, President of Senate
Enrolled House Concurrent Resolution 14 (HCR 14-INTRO) Page 1
