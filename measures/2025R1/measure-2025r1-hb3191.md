83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 3191
Sponsored by Representatives NOSSE, ANDERSEN; Representatives GRAYBER, LEVY E,  MARSH, NATHANSON,
SMITH G, Senators ANDERSON, FREDERICK, MANNING JR, MEEK,  PATTERSON, REYNOLDS (Presession
filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act would appropriate funds to support the arts and culture in Oregon. (Flesch
Readability Score: 63.4).
Appropriates moneys to support arts and cultural organizations in Oregon.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to support of cultural organizations; creating new provisions;  and amending section 6,
chapter 786, Oregon Laws 2013.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There is appropriated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services,
for the biennium beginning July 1, 2025, out of the General Fund, the amount of  $8,875,000
for distribution in accordance with section 2 of this 2025 Act.
SECTION 2. The Oregon Department of  Administrative Services shall distribute the
amount appropriated under section 1 of this 2025 Act to the following organizations:

Museum at Warm Springs ........ $ 1,500,000
Oregon Shakespeare Festival .... $ 375,000
PHAME ..................................... $ 600,000

New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 954
World Forestry Center ............. $ 1,000,000
SECTION 3. There is appropriated to the Oregon Business  Development Department, for
the biennium beginning July 1, 2025, out of the General Fund, the amount  of $400,000 for
deposit in the Cultural Resources Economic Fund established under section 6,  chapter 786,
Oregon Laws 2013, to be used for any purpose for which moneys in the fund may be used.
SECTION 4.  Section 6, chapter 786, Oregon Laws 2013, as amended by  section 27, chapter 812,
Oregon Laws 2015, section 23, chapter 748, Oregon  Laws 2017, section 36, chapter 671, Oregon Laws
2019, section 45, chapter 682, Oregon Laws 2021, and section 41, chapter 599, Oregon Laws  2023, is
amended to read:
Sec. 6.  (1) The Cultural Resources Economic Fund  is established in the State Treasury, separate
and distinct from the General Fund. Interest earned on the moneys in  the Cultural Resources Eco-
nomic Fund shall be credited to the fund.
(2) The Cultural Resources Economic Fund consists of :
(a) Moneys deposited in the fund under :
(A) Section 5, chapter 786, Oregon Laws 2013[ ,];
(B) Section 26, chapter 812, Oregon Laws 2015[ ,];
(C) Section 22, chapter 748, Oregon Laws 2017[ ,];
(D) Section 44, chapter 682, Oregon Laws 2021[ , and];
(E) Section 38 , chapter 599, Oregon Laws 2023  [ of this 2023 Act, and may include ]; and
(F) Section 3 of this 2025 Act; and
(b) Fees, revenues or other income deposited in the fund by the Legislative Assembly.
[(2)] (3) Moneys in the fund are continuously  appropriated to the Oregon Business Development
Department for disbursement for the purposes set forth in section 5,  chapter 786, Oregon Laws 2013,
section 26, chapter 812, Oregon Laws 2015, section 22, chapter 748, Oregon Laws 2017, section  44,
chapter 682, Oregon Laws 2021,  and section 38 , chapter 599, Oregon Laws 2023  [ of this 2023
Act].
SECTION 5.
There is appropriated to the Oregon Business  Development Department, for
the biennium beginning July 1, 2025, out of  the General Fund, the amount of $50,000 for the
purpose of carrying out the provisions of section 6 of this 2025 Act.
SECTION 6.  (1) The Oregon Business Development Department shall conduct a compet-
itive grant program to award the moneys appropriated under  section 5 of this 2025 Act to a
nonprofit organization whose mission is to create a thriving environment  for arts, culture,
history, heritage and humanities throughout Oregon.
(2)(a) The nonprofit organization  awarded the grant shall use the moneys to fund its
provision of technical assistance to the arts and cultural organizations of its selection.
(b) The technical assistance provided under this subsection shall consist of assistance
with:
(A) Receiving and expending public moneys; and
(B) Preparing applications for public moneys.
