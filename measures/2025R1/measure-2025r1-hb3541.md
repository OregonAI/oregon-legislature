## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 3541
Sponsored by Representative NELSON; Representative GAMBA
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act makes drive-through restaurants have  a garbage can outside for customers to
use from a car. (Flesch Readability Score: 65.1).
Requires drive-through restaurants to provide a garbage can within  15 feet of a service window
for customers to dispose of garbage from a motor vehicle after purchasing food or  drink from the
drive-through restaurant.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to drive-through restaurants.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used in this section, “drive-through  restaurant” means a restaurant,
as defined in ORS 624.010, with a service window that allows customers to order  and receive
food or drink from a motor vehicle for consumption on or off the premises.
(2) A drive-through restaurant shall provide a garbage can within 15  feet of a service
window for customers to  dispose of garbage from a motor vehicle after purchasing food or
drink from the drive-through restaurant.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 4212
