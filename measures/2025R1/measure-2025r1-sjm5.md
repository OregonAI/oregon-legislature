## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Senate Joint Memorial 5
Sponsored by Senator SMITH DB (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The measure concerns timber. The  measure asks the President and Congress to take
certain actions. The  measure explains that revenues from timber have declined. (Flesch Readability
Score: 61.2).
Urges the President  and Congress to pursue Oregon and California Railroad grant land man-
agement reforms.
JOINT MEMORIAL
To the President of the United States and the Senate and the House of Representatives of the United
States of America, in Congress assembled:
We, your memorialists,  the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon, in legisla-
tive session assembled, respectfully represent as follows:
Whereas the revested Oregon and California Railroad grant  lands, commonly known as O&C
lands, which are managed predominantly by the Bureau of Land Management, were once privately
owned but were returned to federal ownership in 1916 and now constitute  more than 2.4 million
acres of legally unique federal lands that are intended to be used for  sustained yield timber pro-
duction, to produce revenues that are shared  with counties and to create and sustain jobs in rural
Oregon communities; and
Whereas O&C lands once provided revenues vital to the economic survival of many  county
governments in Oregon; and
Whereas the O&C Act of 1937, which governs the bureau’s management  of O&C lands, states
that all the timberlands shall be managed for  sustained yield production and also mandates a mini-
mum harvest each year of 500 million board feet; and
Whereas timber harvests have been reduced  by 90 percent over recent decades, which has re-
sulted in a drastic decline in revenues from those lands and left many of the 18 Oregon  counties in
which O&C lands are located without adequate revenues to provide essential  public services and
employment opportunities; and
Whereas because of the loss of federal timber sales revenue, the counties have suffered a  peril-
ous loss of  jobs and infrastructure due to sawmill and peeling mill closures and the disappearance
of other forest-based industries and have been forced to lay off law enforcement  officers and public
health providers and mental health providers, close county  correctional facilities and dramatically
reduce all other county services; and
Whereas the bureau  has signed a Record of Decision finalizing the new Resource Management
Plans for Western Oregon that further limit  sustainable, active management of O&C lands and limit
the ability of counties to provide public services required under the O&C Act of 1937; and
Whereas the Secure Rural Schools and Community Self-Determination Act  of 2000 expired Sep-

tember 30, 2015,  leaving counties to rely on shared timber receipts from O&C lands—at an 86 per-
cent reduction from the historical payment average; and
Whereas the  transfer of services from financially distressed counties to the State of Oregon will
adversely affect public education, human services and other state-supported services; and
Whereas a much higher level of timber harvest than is planned by the bureau’s  new resource
management plans can be achieved while still providing for the  recovery of endangered species, in-
creasing carbon storage and providing clean water, fish habitat  and a wide range of recreational
opportunities; and
Whereas members of the Oregon  Congressional Delegation have proposed plans that would re-
sult in stable county revenues in an effort to avoid  the financial crises currently confronting many
Oregon counties and assist in the revival of the ailing economies in the 18  O&C counties; now,
therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the  members of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly, urge the President and the
Congress of the  United States of America to reform federal management of revested Oregon and
California Railroad grant lands to provide certainty and predictability to Oregon counties  in which
O&C lands are located; and be it further
Resolved, That a copy of this  memorial with original signatures shall be sent to the President
of the United States and to each member of the Oregon Congressional Delegation.
