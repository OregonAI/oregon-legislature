## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 3413
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON RULES (at the request of Representative Ben Bowman)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: This Act requires the State  Department of Fish and Wildlife to study and make a report
on natural resources. (Flesch Readability Score: 62.8).
Requires the State  Department of Fish and Wildlife to study natural resources. Directs the de-
partment to submit findings to the interim  committees of the Legislative Assembly related to natural
resources no later than September 15, 2026.
Sunsets on January 2, 2027.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to natural resources.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The State Department of Fish and Wildlife  shall study natural resources in
this state. The department shall submit a report in the manner provided by ORS 192.245,  and
may include recommendations for legislation,  to the interim committees of the Legislative
Assembly related to natural resources no later than September 15, 2026.
SECTION 2. Section 1 of this 2025 Act is repealed on January 2, 2027.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 3289
