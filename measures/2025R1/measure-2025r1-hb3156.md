## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Bill 3156
Sponsored by Representative CATE; Representatives CHAICHI,  GAMBA, OSBORNE, RESCHKE,
Senators PHAM K, SOLLMAN (Presession filed.)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to utilities.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2025 Act is added to and made a part of ORS chapter 757.
SECTION 2. The Public Utility Commission shall require each public utility to include at
the bottom of every utility bill the following information:
(1) The phone numbers for the commission’s consumer services.
(2) The website address, in shortened form, for the commission’s online consumer com-
plaint form.
