83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Bill 2077
Introduced and printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the request of House In-
terim Committee on Revenue for Representative Nancy Nathanson)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to property tax exemption for nonprofit corporation low income  housing; amending section
6, chapter 660, Oregon Laws 1985; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.  Section 6, chapter  660, Oregon Laws 1985, as amended by section 1, chapter 108,
Oregon Laws 1993,  section 1, chapter 215, Oregon Laws 2003, and section 1, chapter 191, Oregon
Laws 2011, is amended to read:

before July 1, [ 2027] 2033.
