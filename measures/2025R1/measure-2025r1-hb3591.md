## Summary

The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject

measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability

Digest: This Act tells some insurers, OEBB and PEBB to pay all children’s  care providers the
same and to cover this care in  the same way for in-network and out-of-network care. (Flesch Read-
ability Score: 60.7).
Requires a group or individual health benefit plan, policy  or contract, including plans offered
by the Public Employees’  Benefit Board or the Oregon Educators Benefit Board, to reimburse out-
of-network  providers  the same as in-network providers for pediatric care and prohibits additional
out-of-pocket costs for pediatric care.

SECTION 1.
Sections 2 and 3 of this 2025 Act are added to and made  a part of the In-
surance Code.
SECTION 2. (1) As used in this section:
(a) “Health benefit plan” has the meaning given that term in ORS 743B.005.
(b) “In-network” has the meaning given that term in ORS 743B.280.
(c) “Out-of-network” has the meaning given that term in ORS 743B.280.
(d) “Pediatric care”  means health care that treats the physical, behavioral and develop-
mental issues that affect children from birth to 18 years of age.
(2) An  insurer offering a policy or certificate of group health insurance or an individual
health benefit plan, policy or contract that reimburses out-of-network  providers for pediatric
care may not reimburse the cost of covered pediatric care services provided  by an out-of-
network provider in an amount that is less than the reimbursement paid to an in-network
provider for the same service, treatment or procedure.
(3) Coverage under this section may  not be subject to terms and conditions, including
provisions imposing out-of-pocket costs such as copayments,  deductibles and coinsurance,
other than the terms and conditions that apply to the reimbursement of  the cost of pediatric
care services provided by in-network providers.
(4) This section is exempt from ORS 743A.001.
SECTION 3.
(1) As used in this section:
(a) “Health benefit plan” has the meaning given that term in ORS 743B.005.
(b) “Out-of-network” has the meaning given that term in ORS 743B.280.
(c) “Pediatric care” means  health care that treats the physical, behavioral and develop-
mental issues that affect children from birth to 18 years of age.
