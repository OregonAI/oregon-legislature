83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Bill 790
Printed pursuant to Senate Interim  Rule 213.28 by order of the President of the Senate in conform-
ance with presession filing rules, indicating neither advocacy nor  opposition on the part of the
President (at the request of Governor Tina Kotek for Oregon Advocacy Commissions Office)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to the Oregon Advocacy Commissions Office; amending ORS 185.010.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
 ORS 185.010 is amended to read:
185.010. (1)  The Oregon Advocacy Commissions Office shall be staffed by an [ administrator]
executive director, who is responsible for the performance of  the duties, functions and powers of
the office.
(2) A board consisting of the chairpersons of the commissions served by the office and  the
Governor shall establish the qualifications  for and appoint the [ Administrator of the Oregon Advo-
cacy Commissions Office ] executive director .
(3) The [ Administrator of the  Oregon Advocacy Commissions Office ] executive director  shall
receive a salary as prescribed by law, or as prescribed by the board described in subsection (2) of
this section if a salary is not prescribed by law.
(4) The [ Administrator of the Oregon Advocacy  Commissions Office ] executive director is in the
unclassified  service.
(5) The [ Administrator of the  Oregon Advocacy Commissions Office ] executive director  shall
provide each commission served by the office with the administrative support needed by the  com-
mission to carry out the statutory duties of the commission. Subject  to any applicable provisions
of the State Personnel Relations Law, the [ administrator] executive director shall employ all per-
sons necessary for the operation of the office, prescribe the duties  of those employees and establish
the compensation payable to those employees.
