**Relating to:** Relating to activities regulated by the State Marine Board; and prescribing an effective date.

<b>Digest: The Act redefines "charter guide" and defines "crew member." The Act says folks must register with SMB before acting as a crew member.  Modifies the definition of "charter guide" for purposes of outfitter and guide laws. 
 <b>Requires a person to register with the State Marine Board before acting as a crew member. Defines "crew member" for the purposes of outfitter and guide laws.</b> 
 Increases the registration fee for all outfitters and guides. Becomes operative November 1, 2025. 
 Takes effect on the 91st day following adjournment sine die.

[(1) “Charter guide” means an individual who: ]

(2) “Crew member” means an employee who:

while aboard a boat;

who is physically present and in direct communication with the crew member; and
(c) Is registered with the State Marine Board as a  crew member under section 5 of this
2025 Act.
[(2)] (3) “Employee” means an individual  who, in exchange for compensation or monetary gain,
provides outfitting and guiding services under  the direction, supervision and control of an outfitter
and guide or a charter guide.

ing, jeep touring,  backpacking, alpine mountain climbing, camping, trips utilizing pack animals, dog
sled trips, whitewater float trips, rafting  trips, drift boat trips, kayak trips, inflatable canoe trips
and canoe trips, the duration of which may be for a few hours or for several days or weeks.
[(4)(a)] (5)(a)  “Outfitter and guide” means any person:
(A) Who, for compensation  or monetary gain, provides, offers to provide or advertises the pro-
vision of:
(i) Outfitting and guiding services in this state; or
