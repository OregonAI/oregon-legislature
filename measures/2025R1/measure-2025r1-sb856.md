## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Bill 856
Printed pursuant to Senate Interim  Rule 213.28 by order of the President of the Senate in conform-
ance with presession filing rules, indicating neither advocacy nor  opposition on the part of the
President (at the request of Governor Tina Kotek for Oregon Racing Commission)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to prohibited activities related to racing; amending ORS 462.273.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
 ORS 462.273 is amended to read:
462.273. (1) No member, employee or appointee of the Oregon Racing  Commission, or the im-
mediate family member of such a person, may:
[(1)] (a)  Own or have any other financial interest in any animal  participating in any race meet
in Oregon.
[(2)] (b) Own or have any other financial  interest in any race meet, public training track or race
course in Oregon.
[(3)] (c)  Bet  or wager, in any manner, on any race meet held under the license and supervision
of the commission if such individual acts in an official  capacity in connection with that race meet ,
except as provided in subsection (2) of this section .
(2) Subsection (1)(c) of this section does not prohibit an  officer or employee of the com-
mission from placing a bet or wager in the course of carrying out official auditing duties.
