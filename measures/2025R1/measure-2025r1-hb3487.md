## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Bill 3487
Sponsored by Representatives MCINTIRE, RESCHKE; Representative LEVY B
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to funding grasshopper suppression efforts; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Counties that receive  moneys appropriated under section 408, chapter 114,
Oregon Laws 2024, may use up to  eight percent of the moneys to pay expenses incurred in
administering grasshopper suppression efforts.
SECTION 2. This 2025 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist, and  this 2025 Act takes effect
on its passage.
