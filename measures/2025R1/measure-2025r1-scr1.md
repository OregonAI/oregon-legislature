## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Concurrent Resolution 1
Sponsored by Senator WAGNER; Senator MANNING JR (Presession filed.)
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That the 2025 regular  session of the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Eighty-third
Legislative Assembly is adjourned sine die.
Adopted by Senate June 27, 2025
Obadiah Rutledge, Secretary of Senate
Rob Wagner, President of Senate
Adopted by House June 27, 2025
Julie Fahey, Speaker of House
