## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Concurrent Resolution 19
Sponsored by Senator SMITH DB; Representative BOICE
Whereas Leonard Warren Krug was born in 1957 in Ashland, Oregon; and
Whereas Leonard Krug graduated from Ashland High School  in 1976, and he attended Southern
Oregon State College for two years; and
Whereas Leonard  Krug began working with Pacific Power in a temporary summer position and
he remained with the company until his retirement, steadily rising through the  ranks along the way;
and
Whereas Leonard Krug was a proud member of  Local 659 of the International Brotherhood of
Electrical Workers; and
Whereas Leonard Krug had a legendary sense of humor, and he always had  a joke or two up
his sleeve to lighten any occasion; and
Whereas Leonard Krug’s passion was hunting and fishing, and he  worked tirelessly as a volun-
teer to share his passion with others; and
Whereas Leonard Krug was a board member of Oregon  South Coast Fishermen and was a
founding member and president of Oregon Anglers Alliance; and
Whereas Leonard Krug believed strongly in the need to protect fishing opportunities for  future
generations,  and  he was instrumental in the fight to keep Oregon’s fisheries and hatcheries open;
and
Whereas Leonard Krug brought the  recreational and commercial fishing communities together
with legislators, local and state officials, environmentalists and tribal  agencies to maintain and en-
hance sustainable fishing resources for all Oregonians; and
Whereas Leonard Krug was a humble man who was always willing to roll up his sleeves and
do the hard work himself, behind the scenes, day after day; and
Whereas Leonard Krug devoted  his life to his family, to his community and to preserving
nature’s bounty for all Oregonians; and
Whereas Leonard Krug passed away on October 6, 2024, doing  what he most enjoyed: fishing
and spending time with loved ones; and
Whereas Leonard Krug was survived by his beloved wife, Anna; his children, Chris,  Andrew,
Christa and Jeff; and seven grandchildren; and
Whereas Leonard Krug was a powerful force for positivity in the lives of  others, and he was
always available to listen and to provide advice, sympathy or his own experiences; and
Whereas Leonard Krug will be forever remembered  as a legendary angler and a tireless volun-
teer who touched countless lives and made the State of Oregon a better place; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of the Eighty-third Legislative Assembly,  honor and celebrate the life of
Leonard Warren Krug,  and we express our gratitude for his service to the people of the State of
Oregon; and be it further
Resolved, That a copy of this resolution shall be presented to the  family of Leonard Krug as
an expression of our sympathy and condolences.
Enrolled Senate Concurrent Resolution 19 (SCR 19-INTRO) Page 1
Adopted by Senate March 20, 2025
Obadiah Rutledge, Secretary of Senate
Rob Wagner, President of Senate
Adopted by House May 19, 2025
Julie Fahey, Speaker of House
Enrolled Senate Concurrent Resolution 19 (SCR 19-INTRO) Page 2
