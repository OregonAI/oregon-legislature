## Summary

**Catchline:** Amends portions of an Act that restricts management services organizations from owning a majority interest in professional medical entities and that prohibits noncompetition agreements, nondisparagement agreements and nondisclosure agreements between medical licensees and other persons.

**Relating to:** Relating to health care; and declaring an emergency.

Digest: Makes changes to an Act that keeps people from running both a company that does not do medical work and another entity that does medical work. Goes into effect right away. (Flesch Readability Score: 62.8). 
 Amends portions of an Act that restricts management services organizations from owning a majority interest in professional medical entities and that prohibits noncompetition agreements, nondisparagement agreements and nondisclosure agreements between medical licensees and other persons. Makes a prohibition on the use of noncompetition agreements retroactive. 
 Declares an emergency, effective on passage.
