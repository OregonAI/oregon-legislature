## Full text

83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Bill 803
Printed pursuant to Senate Interim  Rule 213.28 by order of the President of the Senate in conform-
ance with presession filing rules, indicating neither advocacy nor  opposition on the part of the
President (at the request of Governor Tina Kotek for Oregon Military Department)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to the state-sponsored life insurance program for Oregon National Guard members.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used in this section, “state-sponsored life insurance program” means
the group life insurance program allowed under 37 U.S.C. 707 and exclusively offered to all
members of the Oregon National Guard through the Oregon National Guard Association.
(2) The Adjutant General shall, at the discretion of the Adjutant General:
(a) Allow efforts to make the state-sponsored life insurance  program available to all
members of the Oregon National Guard;
(b) Provide opportunities for members of the Oregon National  Guard to purchase pro-
ducts from the state-sponsored life insurance program; and
(c) Allow representatives  from the state-sponsored life insurance program to provide in-
formation through briefings on the state-sponsored life insurance  program to members of
the Oregon National Guard during active and inactive duty training periods.
