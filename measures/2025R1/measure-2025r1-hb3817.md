83rd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2025 Regular Session
House Bill 3817
Sponsored by Representative SKARLATOS, Senator SMITH DB; Representatives  CHOTZEN, LEWIS, LIVELY,
Senators STARR, THATCHER
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced. The statement includes a measure digest written in compliance with applicable readability
standards.
Digest: The Act tells OHA and DVA to set up a process  to let a person with a certain disorder
use ibogaine to help treat the disorder. (Flesch Readability Score: 63.3).
Directs the Oregon Health Authority in collaboration with the Department of Veterans’ Affairs
to establish a process through which a certain  individual may consume ibogaine for a specified
purpose. Defines “ibogaine.” Requires the authority and the department to submit a report  to the
interim committees of the Legislative  Assembly related to health care and veterans not later than
September 15, 2029. Exempts ibogaine, when obtained and consumed through the established process,
from the definition of “controlled substance.”
Sunsets on January 2, 2030.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to ibogaine; creating new provisions; and amending ORS 475.005.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) As used in this section, “ibogaine” means a naturally occurring indole
alkaloid from the root bark of the Tabernanthe iboga shrub.
(2) The Oregon Health Authority shall,  in collaboration with the Department of Veterans’
Affairs, establish a process through which an individual  who has post-traumatic stress dis-
order, major depressive disorder, an anxiety disorder or substance use disorder may consume
ibogaine for the purpose of treating  the disorder. The process established under this sub-
section must require that:
(a) Prior to consuming ibogaine, the individual is screened for preexisting cardiac-related
conditions that may place the  individual at a heightened risk of fatal cardiac arrhythmia
from the consumption of ibogaine;
(b) The consumption of ibogaine occur in a controlled setting:
(A) Where a health  care provider experienced in managing cardiac complications is
available on site; and
(B) That is  created in consideration of the needs of an individual experiencing the effects
of ibogaine consumption; and
(c) The results of ibogaine consumption are reported  to the authority and the depart-
ment.
(3) In establishing the process described in subsection (2) of this section, the  authority
and the department shall review peer-reviewed studies related to the consumption of ibogaine
and consult with individuals who have personal and professional knowledge of the  impacts
of ibogaine consumption on the disorders described in subsection (2) of this section.
(4) The authority and  the department may adopt rules as necessary to carry out this
section.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.

ORS 192.245, and may include recommendations  for legislation, to the interim committees

2029.
(6) Nothing in this section may be construed to:
(a) Require a person to violate federal law; or
(b) Exempt a person from federal law or obstruct the enforcement of federal law.
