## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Bill 5531
Introduced and printed pursuant to House  Concurrent Resolution 23 (2023) (at the request of Oregon
Department of Administrative Services)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to the financial administration of the Psychiatric Security Review  Board; and declaring an
emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. There is  appropriated to the Psychiatric Security Review Board, for the
biennium beginning July 1, 2023, out of the General Fund, the amount of $4,536,912.
SECTION 2. This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency  is declared to exist, and this 2023 Act takes effect
July 1, 2023.
Passed by Senate June 16, 2023
