## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 3130
Sponsored by Representatives OWENS, HELM; Senator FINDLEY
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys to Water Resources Department to fund staff position, or contract for
services, related to voluntary agreements among ground water users from same reservoir.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to voluntary agreements among ground water users; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Water Resources Department, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, out of  the
General Fund, the amount of  $ , which may be expended for funding a staff position
at the department, or contracting with a qualified entity, to:
(1) Advise  local communities concerning voluntary agreements among ground water us-
ers from the same ground water reservoir, as described in ORS 537.745;
(2) Assist the communities with development of the voluntary agreements; and
(3) Assess the opportunities and limitations of the voluntary agreements.
SECTION 2.
This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2023 Act takes effect
on its passage.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 2179
