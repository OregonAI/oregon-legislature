## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2422
Sponsored by Representative HUDSON (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Legislative Administrator to pay costs of reasonable  accommodation of member of Leg-
islative Assembly who is afforded rights and protections as person with  disabilities under specified
federal and state law. Directs Legislative Administration Committee to adopt interactive process  to
determine reasonable accommodations entitled to payment.
Appropriates moneys to Legislative Administration Committee to fund payments.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to removing financial barriers inhibiting persons with disabilities  from serving in the Leg-
islative Assembly; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The Legislative Administrator  shall pay costs of a member of the Legis-
lative Assembly who is afforded rights and protections as  a person with disabilities under
Title II of the Americans With Disabilities  Act (42 U.S.C. 12131 to 12165) and ORS chapter
659A that are incurred implementing reasonable accommodations to  enable the member to
fully perform their duties as a member of the Legislative Assembly,  including but not limited
to:
(a) Costs  incurred for accessible transportation to and from the State Capitol, district
meetings or other legislative activities;
(b) Costs  incurred for staff necessary to ensure that the member can participate in all
legislative functions and activities and perform all  of the duties imposed on a member of the
Legislative Assembly;
(c) Costs  incurred in acquiring assistive technology needed to allow a member to fully
participate in all legislative activities and fully perform all legislative duties; and
(d) Costs incurred to access or modify temporary housing used by a member during  a
legislative session.
(2) The Legislative Administration Committee shall adopt processes to implement the
provisions of this section, including an interactive process to determine the reasonable  ac-
commodations that are entitled to payment under this section.
SECTION 2. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the Legislative Administration Committee, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023,
out of  the General Fund, the amount of $_______, which may be expended for the purpose of
making payments described in section 1 of this 2023 Act.
SECTION 3.
This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2023 Act takes effect
on July 1, 2023.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
