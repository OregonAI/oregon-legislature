## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 3098
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON AGRICULTURE, LAND USE, NATURAL  RESOURCES, AND WATER (at the re-
quest of Representative Ken Helm)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Directs State Department of Agriculture,  in consultation with Pesticide Analytical and Response
Center, to establish farmworker  outreach program. Appropriates moneys to department to implement
program.
Takes effect on 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to a farmworker pesticide outreach program; and prescribing an effective date.
Whereas the Pesticide Analytical and Response Center  (PARC) was created by executive order
in 1978 and was reauthorized by statute under the State Department of Agriculture in 1991; and
Whereas the eight member-agencies of PARC are the Oregon  Health Authority, the State De-
partment of Fish and Wildlife, the Department of Environmental Quality, the State Forestry  De-
partment, the Occupational Safety and Health Division of  the Department of Consumer and Business
Services, the Department of the  State Fire Marshal, the Poison Control and Drug Information Pro-
gram of the Oregon Health and Science University and the State Department of Agriculture; and
Whereas PARC serves as a single point of contact for pesticide concerns and incidents; and
Whereas calls to PARC are accepted 24 hours a day  via the statewide 2-1-1 system and callers
receive a response from PARC within one business day; and
Whereas Oregon state agencies lack programming for  direct and sustained engagement with
farmworkers regarding pesticide use and incidents; now, therefore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2023 Act is added to and made a part of ORS chapter 634.
SECTION 2. (1) The State Department of  Agriculture, in consultation with the Pesticide
Analytical and Response  Center, shall establish a farmworker outreach program. The pur-
poses of the program are to:
(a) Conduct outreach and distribute to farmworkers educational materials related  to safe
pesticide use, health and environmental incidents  involving pesticides and procedures for
reporting pesticide incidents; and
(b) Coordinate actions among the public  bodies represented by the public officials listed
in ORS 634.550 to improve  the effectiveness of farmworker pesticide incident reporting,
tracking and responses.
(2) Outreach under the program shall be conducted:
(a) At locations that are at or near  the places where farmworkers work or ordinarily
gather; and
(b) In a culturally sensitive manner.
(3) The department and center shall collaborate with Oregon State University to develop
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 2364
culturally sensitive education  and training for pesticide applicators who are nonnative Eng-
lish speakers or members of a minority group. Materials  developed under this section shall
include clear instructions for pesticide incident reporting.
SECTION 3.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the State Department of Agriculture, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, out
of the  General Fund, the amount of $1,000,000, which may be expended for the purpose of
carrying out the provisions of section 2 of this 2023 Act.
SECTION 4.
This 2023 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on which the 2023
regular session of the Eighty-second Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
