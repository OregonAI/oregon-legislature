## Summary

**Catchline:** Increases fees related to driver licenses, driver permits, identification cards and motor vehicles.

**Relating to:** Relating to transportation funding.

<i>Directs Department of Transportation to study issues related to transportation funding and to report to appropriate committee or interim committee of Legislative Assembly on or before September 15, 2024.</i>] 
  <i>Sunsets January 2, 2025.</i>] 
 <b>Increases fees related to driver licenses, driver permits, identification cards and motor vehicles. 
 Provides that registration period for new mopeds and motorcycles is two years instead of four years.</b>
