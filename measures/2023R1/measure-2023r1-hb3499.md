## Summary

**Catchline:** Prohibits public universities from charging tuition or mandatory enrollment fees to resident undergraduate or graduate students.

**Relating to:** Relating to higher education; declaring an emergency.

Prohibits public universities from charging tuition or mandatory enrollment fees to resident undergraduate or graduate students. 
 Prohibits community colleges from charging tuition to students who are residents of community college district. 
 Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Higher Education Coordinating Commission for purpose of eliminating tuition and mandatory enrollment fees. 
 Requires Higher Education Coordinating Commission to conduct study to determine best methods for eliminating governing board at each public university, eliminating bonding authorities of public universities and making commission responsible for direct oversight and management of each public university. 
 Declares legislative finding that it is not in public interest of state for public universities to have independent governing boards and declares study is first step towards making commission responsible for direct oversight and management of each public university no later than 2027-2028 academic year. 
 Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2023.

## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 3499
Sponsored by Representative CHAICHI
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Prohibits public universities from charging tuition or mandatory enrollment fees to resident
undergraduate or graduate students.
Prohibits community colleges from charging tuition to students who  are residents of community
college  district.
Appropriates moneys from  General Fund to Higher Education Coordinating Commission for
purpose of eliminating tuition and mandatory enrollment fees.
Requires Higher  Education Coordinating Commission to conduct study to determine best meth-
ods for eliminating governing board at each public university, eliminating bonding  authorities of
public universities and making commission responsible  for direct oversight and management of each
public university.
Declares  legislative  finding that it is not in public interest of state for public universities to
have independent governing boards and declares study is first step towards making  commission re-
sponsible for  direct oversight and management of each public university no later than 2027-2028
academic year.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to higher education; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) Notwithstanding ORS 352.102 or any other provision of law, a public uni-
versity listed in ORS 352.002 may not charge tuition or mandatory enrollment fees.
(2) This section applies to tuition and mandatory enrollment fees for:
(a) Resident undergraduate students who are earning  their first undergraduate degree;
and
(b) Resident graduate students who are earning their first graduate degree.
SECTION 2.
(1) Notwithstanding ORS 341.290 or any other provision of law, a community
college operated under ORS chapter 341 may not charge tuition.
(2) This section applies to tuition for students who are  residents of the community col-
lege district in which the community college is located.
SECTION 3. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the  Higher Education Coordinating Commission, for the biennium beginning July 1,
2023, out of the General Fund, the amount of $____, to be allocated to  public universities
listed in ORS 352.002  and community colleges operated under ORS chapter 341 for the pur-
pose of carrying out the provisions of sections 1 and 2 of this 2023 Act.
SECTION 4. (1) The Higher Education Coordinating Commission shall  conduct a study to
determine the best methods for:
(a) Eliminating the governing board at each public university listed in ORS 352.002;
(b) Eliminating the authorities of public  universities with respect to state bonds that are
set forth in ORS 352.388 to 352.415; and
(c) Making the commission responsible for the direct oversight and management of  each
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 4273
public university listed in ORS 352.002.
(2) The  commission shall submit a report in the manner provided by ORS 192.245, and
must include recommendations for legislation, to the interim  committees of the Legislative
Assembly related to higher education no later than September 15, 2024.
(3) Each  public university shall provide the commission with any assistance required by
the commission that the commission determines is necessary for the  commission to com-
plete the study and report in the manner described in this section.
(4) As used in this section, “governing board” and “state bonds”  have the meanings given
those terms in ORS 352.029.
SECTION 5.
(1) The Legislative Assembly finds that having  an independent governing
board for each public university listed in ORS 352.002 is not in the  best interests of this state
and declares that section 4 of this 2023 Act is  the first step towards making the Higher Ed-
ucation Coordinating Commission responsible for direct oversight and management of each
public university no later than by the 2027-2028 academic year.
(2) As used in this section, “governing board” has the  meaning given that term in ORS
352.029.
SECTION 6.
This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2023 Act takes effect
July 1, 2023.
