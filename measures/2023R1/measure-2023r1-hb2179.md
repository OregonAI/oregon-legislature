## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2179
Sponsored by Representatives SMITH DB, WRIGHT (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires State Department of  Fish and Wildlife to establish and operate fish incubation nursery
program for rearing of salmonids on suitable waterways in  coastal areas of southern Oregon. Re-
quires department to report to interim committee of Legislative Assembly no later than  September
15, 2025, regarding establishment and operation of program.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to department for program purposes.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to incubation nursery program for salmonids; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
The State Department of  Fish and Wildlife shall establish and operate a fish
incubation nursery program for the rearing of salmonids  on salmon-bearing or potentially
salmon-bearing waterways located in the Counties  of Coos, Curry or Douglas and within 100
miles of the Pacific Ocean.
SECTION 2. The State Department of Fish and Wildlife shall report on  the establishment
and operation of the fish incubation nursery program required under section 1 of this 2023
Act, including but not limited  to any findings or recommendations of the department re-
garding the  program, to an interim committee of the Legislative Assembly relating to na-
tural resources in the manner provided by ORS 192.245 no later than September 15, 2025.
SECTION 3. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the State Department of Fish and Wildlife, for the biennium  beginning July 1, 2023,
out of the General Fund, the amount of $470,000,  which the department shall expend for es-
tablishing and operating the fish incubation nursery  program required under section 1 of this
2023 Act.
SECTION 4. This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the  public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is  declared to exist, and this 2023 Act takes effect
on its passage.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 1983
