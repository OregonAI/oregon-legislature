## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
Senate Bill 932
Sponsored by Senator MEEK
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Authorizes governing body of local government to  adopt ordinance establishing that owner for-
feits intangible property worth $10 or less if local government holds property for more than two
years and owner does  not claim property. Provides that local government may assume ownership
of forfeited intangible property and place monetary value of property in  general fund to be used for
general operating expenses.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to unclaimed property.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2023 Act is added to and made a part of ORS 98.302 to 98.436.
SECTION 2. (1) As used in this section, “local government” has the meaning given that
term in ORS 174.116.
(2) The governing body of a local government  may adopt an ordinance establishing that
an owner of intangible property of a value equal to or less than $10 forfeits any rights  to and
interest in the intangible property if the local government has  held the intangible property
for more than two years and the owner has not  claimed the intangible property during that
period.
(3) Intangible property subject to forfeiture under an ordinance  adopted under subsection
(2) of this section is not presumed abandoned.
(4) A local government that holds intangible property forfeited  under an ordinance
adopted under subsection (2) of this section may assume ownership of the intangible property
and place the monetary  value of the intangible property in the general fund of the local
government to be used for the payment of the  general operating expense of the local gov-
ernment.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 4150
