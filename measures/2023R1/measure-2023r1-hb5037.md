82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Bill 5037
Introduced and printed pursuant to House  Concurrent Resolution 23 (2023) (at the request of Oregon
Department of Administrative Services)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to the financial administration of the Department  of State Lands; and declaring an emer-
gency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any other law limiting  expenditures, the following amounts
are established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, as the maximum limits for  payment
of expenses  from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts, but ex-
cluding lottery funds,  federal funds and funds described in sections 2 to 5 of this 2023 Act,
collected or received by the Department of State Lands, for the following purposes:
(1) Common School Fund
programs................................... $ 40,626,491
(2) South Slough National
Estuarine Research
Reserve operations.................... $ 3,076,736
(3) Capital improvements ............... $ 572,116
SECTION 2.
Notwithstanding any  other law limiting expenditures, the amount of
$2,121,125 is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, as the maximum limit  for
payment of expenses by the Department of State Lands from the Portland  Harbor Cleanup
Fund established by ORS 274.064.
SECTION 3. Notwithstanding any other law limiting expenditures, the amount of  $200,000
is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, as the maximum limit for payment
of expenses by the Department of State Lands from the Submerged Lands  Enhancement
Fund established by ORS 274.388.
SECTION 4.
Notwithstanding any other law limiting expenditures,  the amount of
$2,181,141 is  established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, as the maximum limit for
payment of expenses by the Department of  State Lands from the Oregon Removal-Fill Miti-
gation Fund established by ORS 196.640.
SECTION 5. Notwithstanding any other law limiting expenditures, the amount of  $600,000
is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, as the maximum limit for payment
of expenses by the Department of State Lands from the Oregon Ocean Science Fund estab-
lished by ORS 196.567.
SECTION 6.
Notwithstanding any other law limiting  expenditures, the following amounts
are established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, as the maximum limits for payment

SECTION 7.
