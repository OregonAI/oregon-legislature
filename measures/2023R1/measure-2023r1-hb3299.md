## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 3299
Sponsored by Representative DEXTER
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Requires administrator of Oregon Prescription Drug Program to  make bulk purchases of opioid
reversal medications to distribute statewide to entities that serve vulnerable populations.
Establishes Opioid Reversal Medication  Bulk Purchasing Fund as repository of moneys from
specified sources to be used to make bulk purchases of opioid reversal medications.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to opioid reversal medications; creating new provisions; amending ORS 414.320.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The administrator of the Oregon  Prescription Drug Program shall, using
powers and duties prescribed in ORS 414.312, undertake bulk purchases of opioid  reversal
medications for  the purpose of expanding access to opioid reversal medications throughout
this state by entities that serve vulnerable populations, including but not limited to:
(a) Hospitals and emergency departments;
(b) First responders;
(c) Law enforcement agencies;
(d) Courts and other departments within the criminal justice system;
(e) Organizations that provide services to homeless individuals;
(f) Veterans’ organizations;
(g) Religious organizations;
(h) Schools and universities;
(i) Substance abuse treatment and recovery facilities, including  inpatient, outpatient,
residential facilities and sobering centers;
(j) Public libraries; and
(k) County public health or behavioral health agencies.
(2) To make the bulk purchases of opioid reversal  medications under this section, the
administrator may use funds from the Opioid  Reversal Medication Bulk Purchasing Fund
established in section 2  of this 2023 Act or from gifts, grants, bequests, endowments or do-
nations made for the purchase of opioid reversal medications from the Prescription Drug
Purchasing Fund established in ORS 414.318.
SECTION 2.
(1) The Opioid Reversal Medication Bulk  Purchasing Fund is established in
the State Treasury, separate and distinct from the General Fund. Interest earned  by the
Opioid Reversal Medication Bulk Purchasing Fund shall be credited to the fund.
(2) The  Opioid Reversal Medication Bulk Purchasing Fund consists of moneys received
by the Oregon Health Authority from opioid litigation settlements, grants from the Sub-
stance Abuse and Mental Health Services  Administration within the United States Depart-

(3) The moneys in the Opioid  Reversal Medication Bulk Purchasing Fund are contin-
