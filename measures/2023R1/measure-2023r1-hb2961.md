## Summary

**Catchline:** Establishes legislative findings relating to expanding need-based financial aid opportunities, ensuring that historically underrepresented students have increased access to wraparound services and support programs at post-secondary institutions of education and increasing levels of targeted state investment that result in increased student service levels.

**Relating to:** Relating to funding post-secondary education; and declaring an emergency.

<b>Establishes legislative findings relating to expanding need-based financial aid opportunities, ensuring that historically underrepresented students have increased access to wraparound services and support programs at post-secondary institutions of education and increasing levels of targeted state investment that result in increased student service levels. 
 <b>Requires Higher Education Coordinating Commission to regularly evaluate administrative rules relating to distribution of grants and financial aid and, to degree permitted by law, amend rules to ensure enrollment needs of adult Oregonians who are currently in professional workforce are met in equitable manner. 
 <b>Requires commission to establish program to enable post-secondary institutions of education to provide current high school students with services and outreach regarding transitioning from high school to post-secondary institutions of education. 
 <b>Requires commission to establish block grant program that allows any public institution of higher education to apply for block grant for any program desired by institution. 
 <b>Requires Department of Early Learning and Care to conduct comprehensive review of Oregon Student Child Care Grant Program and child care centers located at post-secondary institutions of education to determine combination of direct student support and state grants that best meets needs of greatest number of student parents enrolled at post-secondary institutions of education. Requires department to submit report to interim committees of Legislative Assembly related to higher education detailing findings of review. 
 <b>Requires each public institution of higher education to internally develop integrated network that includes all student support services and provide each enrolled student with liaison to help student navigate and utilize integrated network. 
 <b>Authorizes commission to award Oregon Opportunity Grant awards in excess of maximum statutory amount to low-income students. Sunsets authority on June 30, 2025. 
 <b>Appropriates moneys to commission.</b> 
  <i>Appropriates $5,000,000 to Higher Education Coordinating Commission for allocation to expand scope and community reach of college access and success programs at public universities and community colleges.</i>] 
 Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2023.

## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2961
Sponsored by  Representative RUIZ; Representative LIVELY, Senators DEMBROW, GORSEK, SOLLMAN (Preses-
sion filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates $5,000,000 to Higher Education  Coordinating Commission for allocation to expand
scope and community reach of college access and success  programs at public universities and com-
munity colleges.
Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2023.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to funding post-secondary education; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there  is appropri-
ated to the  Higher Education Coordinating Commission, for the biennium beginning July 1,
2023, out of the General Fund, the amount of  $5,000,000, to be allocated to expand the scope
and community reach of college access and success programs at public  universities listed in
ORS 352.002 and community colleges operated under ORS chapter 341.
SECTION 2. This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2023 Act takes effect
on July 1, 2023.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 3386
