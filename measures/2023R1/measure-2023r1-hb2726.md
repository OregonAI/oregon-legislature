## Full text

82nd OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2023 Regular Session
House Bill 2726
Sponsored by Representatives MARSH, REYNOLDS, Senator CAMPOS; Senator PATTERSON (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Early  Learning Division of Department of Education
to distribute to specified Early Learning Hub to administer pilot program  to coordinate services and
other resources  provided to low-income pregnant individuals and families with children under five
years of age by local and state agencies, human services providers, early learning services providers,
health care providers and other community organizations in Jackson and Josephine Counties.
Requires Early Learning Division to report to Legislative Assembly,  by September 15, 2024, on
outcomes of pilot program and recommendations for expanding program statewide.
Sunsets January 2, 2029.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to coordination of resources for low-income individuals; and declaring an emergency.
Whereas in many areas of this state the  majority of families with children under the age of five
have household incomes of 200 percent or less of the federal poverty guidelines; and
Whereas for these families, issues such as support during pregnancy,  housing costs, access to
quality child care and other basic needs can be  overwhelming and, where services and supports are
available, the services and supports are often challenging to access and are not well  coordinated;
and
Whereas, given that the issues facing low-income families  with small children cross all sectors,
including education, health  care, mental health care, housing, human services, public safety, the
community justice systems and workforce development, a well coordinated system of support for
pregnant individuals and  families with children under five years old must be developed; now, there-
fore,
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) Southern Oregon  Early Learning Services, the Early Learning Hub for
Jackson and Josephine  Counties, shall administer a pilot program, the Southern Oregon
Early Childhood Support Network, to coordinate resources and supports offered to  low-
income pregnant individuals and families with children under five years of age by:
(a) Coordinated care organizations serving Jackson and Josephine Counties;
(b) The systems of care, as defined in ORS 418.976, for Jackson and Josephine Counties;
(c) Public health home visiting programs;
(d) The Child Care Resource Network  within the Southern Oregon Education Service
District;
(e) Child care centers;
(f) Preschools;
(g) The Family Connection;
(h) Local school districts;
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 1268
(i) Rogue Community College;
(j) Southern Oregon University;
(k) The Asante and Providence hospitals and health systems;
(L) Local federally qualified health centers;
(m) Southern Oregon Pediatrics;
(n) Local recovery centers and organizations;
(o) Community-based organizations;
(p) Jackson County Community Justice;
(q) Josephine County Community Justice;
(r) The Rogue Workforce Partnership;
(s) Connect Oregon; and
(t) Other community partners.
(2) Using moneys appropriated for the pilot program by  section 2 of this 2023 Act,
Southern Oregon Early Learning Services shall create the following two funds:
(a) The Early Childhood Support Fund, containing $10,000,000, which will  provide moneys
to community action agencies  in Jackson and Josephine Counties to provide financial support
to low-income pregnant individuals and families with children up to six years  of age for rent
and other  housing assistance, basic necessities such as food, clothing and diapers, educa-
tional opportunities for parents and other needs.
(b) The Child Care Workforce Fund, containing $15,000,000, from which Southern  Oregon
Early Learning Services will provide grants to providers of early learning services  to assist
with the development  and renovation of child care facilities and other capacity needs and to
pay living wage salaries and provide health care coverage to child care providers.
(3) No later than September 15, 2024, the Early  Learning Division of the Department of
Education shall submit a report to the interim committees of the Legislative  Assembly re-
lated to human services, in the manner provided in ORS 192.245, on the outcomes from the
pilot program and recommendations for expanding the pilot program to  other areas of this
state.
SECTION 2.
There is appropriated to the Early  Learning Division of the Department of
Education, for the biennium beginning July 1, 2023, out of the  General Fund, the amount of
$25,000,000 to be distributed to Southern Oregon Early Learning Services to carry out  the
pilot program described in section 1 of this 2023 Act.
SECTION 3. Section 1 of this 2023 Act is repealed on January 2, 2029.
SECTION 4. This 2023 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2023 Act takes effect
on its passage.
