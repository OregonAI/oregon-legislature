## Summary

**Catchline:** Prescribes timelines and redacting requirements for records related to provision of special education.

**Relating to:** Relating to sharing of information related to special education; and prescribing an effective date.

Prescribes timelines and redacting requirements for records related to provision of special education. 
  <i>Provides that school district bears burden of proof related to special education and determinations and appropriateness regarding identification, evaluation, individualized education program or educational placement and provision of free appropriate public education.</i>] 
 Prohibits school district from preventing or discouraging school employee or volunteer from taking certain actions related to special education and violation of related laws. Prohibits certain nondisclosure agreements between school district and student, parent, school employee or school volunteer related to provision of special education. 
 Takes effect on 91st day following adjournment sine die.
