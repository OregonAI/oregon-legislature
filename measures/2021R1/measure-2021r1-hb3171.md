Includes insurance in definition of real estate, goods and services that are subject to penalties for unlawful trade practices. 
 Permits person to obtain, and court to award, appropriate equitable relief in addition to monetary damages under Unlawful Trade Practices Act.

The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject

 ORS 646.608 is amended to read:
646.608. (1) A person engages in an unlawful practice if in the course of the  person’s business,
