## Full text

81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Joint Memorial 3
Sponsored by Representative SMITH DB; Representatives EVANS, LEVY, MOORE-GREEN,
MORGAN, Senator KENNEMER (Presession filed.)
To the President of the  United States and the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
United States of America, in Congress assembled:
We, your memorialists, the Eighty-first Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon, in  legisla-
tive session assembled, respectfully represent as follows:
Whereas 2020 was a  year of unprecedented wildfires throughout the western United States, in-
cluding in Oregon; and
Whereas the 2020 Oregon wildfire season was the most destructive season on record; and
Whereas Oregon wildfires killed  at least 11 people, burned more than 1 million acres and de-
stroyed thousands of homes; and
Whereas the pattern and magnitude  of wildfire-induced destruction in Oregon was repeated in
California and throughout the western United States, where a total of more  than 6 million acres
have burned; and
Whereas backlogs in adequate forest management coupled with drier, hotter  conditions, have
resulted in unhealthy, overly dense forests; and
Whereas when fires inevitably occur, current forest conditions result  in larger, more cat-
astrophic  fires  that are difficult to control, destructive to both urban and rural communities and
pose great threats to both private property and human life; and
Whereas the bipartisan Emergency Wildlife and Public Safety Act of 2020 (S.4431) was intro-
duced in the United States Senate  to begin to address the catastrophic wildfire conditions that
persist in Oregon and other western states; and
Whereas the Emergency Wildfire and Public Safety Act of 2020 was the  result of over one year
of extensive deliberation, stakeholder engagement  and outreach across party lines to ensure a bi-
partisan, pragmatic approach to reduce the risk of wildfire; and
Whereas enactment of federal legislation based on the Emergency Wildfire and Public  Safety
Act of 2020  will provide federal agencies with new tools to reduce hazardous fuels, protect commu-
nities, support forestry jobs, biomass development and smarter energy practices; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of the  Eighty-first Legislative Assembly, respectfully urge the Congress
of the United States to pass, and the President to sign, legislation promoting  cooperative fuel load
measurement and management on federal  and state lands, and appropriate legislation that is based
on the Emergency Wildfire and Public Safety Act of 2020, excluding any  provision that exempts
forest management activities from environmental or administrative review, that could  foreseeably
compromise the ecological health  of Oregon forests or that facilitates the exportation outside of the
United States of unprocessed timber harvested from public lands; and be it further
Resolved, That a copy of this memorial  shall be sent to the President of the United States, to
the Senate Majority Leader, to the Speaker of the House of Representatives and to  each member
of the Oregon Congressional Delegation.
Enrolled House Joint Memorial 3 (HJM 3-B) Page 1
Adopted by House April 19, 2021
Readopted by House June 26, 2021
Timothy G. Sekerak, Chief Clerk of House
Tina Kotek, Speaker of House
Adopted by Senate June 26, 2021
Peter Courtney, President of Senate
Enrolled House Joint Memorial 3 (HJM 3-B) Page 2
