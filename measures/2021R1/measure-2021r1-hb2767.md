## Full text

81st OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2021 Regular Session
House Bill 2767
Sponsored by Representative SMITH DB (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs State Department of Agriculture  to establish grant program for purpose of facilitating
eradication of gorse for wildfire mitigation in southwest Oregon.
Establishes Southwest Oregon Gorse Wildfire Risk Reduction Fund to support grant program.
Appropriates moneys to department for deposit into fund.
Sunsets grant program and fund on December 31, 2025.
Takes effect on 91st day following adjournment sine die.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to  a grant program that facilitates the eradication of gorse for wildfire mitigation in
southwest Oregon; and prescribing an effective date.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) The State Department  of Agriculture shall establish by rule a grant
program for the purpose of facilitating the  eradication of gorse for wildfire mitigation in
southwest Oregon.
(2) In administering the program, the department:
(a) Shall develop criteria for receiving a grant and a process for applying for  a grant; and
(b) May require a recipient  of a grant to report to the department on the use of the grant
funds.
(3) The department shall review the grant program on a biennial basis to assess to  what
extent the  purpose described in subsection (1) of this section is being achieved. The depart-
ment shall modify the process for awarding grants as necessary to better achieve  the pur-
pose described in subsection (1) of this section.
SECTION 2.
(1) There is established in the State Treasury, separate and distinct from the
General Fund, the Southwest Oregon Gorse Wildfire Risk Reduction Fund.  Interest earned
by the Southwest Oregon Gorse Wildfire Risk Reduction  Fund shall be credited to the fund.
All moneys in  the Southwest Oregon Gorse Wildfire Risk Reduction Fund are continuously
appropriated to the State Department of Agriculture to carry  out the purposes described in
section 1 of this 2021 Act.
(2) The Southwest Oregon Gorse Wildfire Risk Reduction Fund shall consist of all moneys
credited to the fund, including  moneys appropriated or transferred to the fund by the Leg-
islative Assembly.
SECTION 3. There is appropriated to the State Department of Agriculture,  for the
biennium beginning of July 1, 2021, out of the  General Fund, the amount of $1,000,000 for
deposit into the Southwest  Oregon Gorse Wildfire Risk Reduction Fund established in section
2 of this 2021 Act.
SECTION 4. (1) Sections 1 and 2 of this 2021 Act are repealed on December 31, 2025.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 2441
(2) Any moneys remaining in the Southwest Oregon Gorse Wildfire  Risk Reduction Fund
that are unexpended and unobligated on December 31, 2025, shall revert  to the General Fund.
SECTION 5.
This 2021 Act takes effect on the 91st day after the date on  which the 2021
regular session of the Eighty-first Legislative Assembly adjourns sine die.
