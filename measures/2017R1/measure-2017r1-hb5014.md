## Full text

79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Bill 5014
Introduced and printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession  filed (at the request of Oregon
Department of Administrative Services)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to the  financial administration of the Commission on Judicial Fitness and Disability; and
declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. There are appropriated to the Commission on Judicial Fitness and Disability,
for the biennium beginning July 1, 2017, out of the General  Fund, the following amounts, for
the following purposes:
(1) Administration.......................... $ 227,713
(2) Extraordinary expenses ............ $ 18,226
SECTION 2. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist, and  this 2017 Act takes effect
July 1, 2017.
