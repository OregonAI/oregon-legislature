## Full text

79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Resolution 1
Sponsored by Representative WILSON; Representative ESQUIVEL (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Recognizes State of Oregon’s dependence on federal funds to provide  needed services and oper-
ations and  further recognizes risks of reductions in federal funds due to changes in federal budget
priorities and federal tax revenues.
Commits House  of Representatives to assessing risks of reductions in federal funds and to cre-
ating contingency plans and budget  reserves to support funding for mission-critical services and
operations. States expectation  of House of Representatives that Governor and state agencies simi-
larly develop contingency plans and incorporate contingencies in future budget recommendations.
HOUSE RESOLUTION
Whereas the State of Oregon, like many other states, accepts  billions of dollars in tax revenues
from the federal government in exchange for implementing and administering programs  that comply
with standards imposed by the federal government; and
Whereas for the 2015-2017 biennium, the Legislative Fiscal  Office has reported legislatively ap-
proved expenditures of federal funds that total $22.917 billion; and
Whereas the legislatively approved federal funds expenditures for  the 2015-2017 biennium rep-
resent an 18.1 percent increase from the 2013-2015 biennium legislatively approved budget for federal
funds; and
Whereas it has become a common practice in the State of Oregon to rely on federal funds to
artificially bolster the general revenues of this state to support programs; and
Whereas there is a significant risk that federal funding that is assumed in the  state budget
process will decrease or be eliminated due to changes in federal budget priorities and federal  tax
revenues; and
Whereas the State of Oregon lacks contingency plans to continue  operations in the event of
foreseeable reductions in federal funds to be distributed to Oregon; and
Whereas in the absence of careful  contingency planning and allocation of state funding re-
sources, the State of Oregon may become unable to maintain necessary levels of service and opera-
tion; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the House of Representatives of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of  the House of Representatives of the Seventy-ninth Legislative Assem-
bly, recognize the reliance the State of Oregon now places on federal funds; and be it further
Resolved, That we are committed to assessing the risks of a  reduction in federal funds received
in the state budget being developed this session and in future budgets; and be it further
Resolved, That we are committed to creating  or adjusting state budget resources to hold in re-
serve for use in funding mission-critical services and operations  when federal funds are reduced; and
be it further
Resolved, That we are committed to ensuring that the budget processes and documents  used by
