79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 2664
Sponsored by  Representatives NOSSE, LIVELY, Senator MONNES ANDERSON, Representative BUEHLER; Rep-

SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Specifies criteria for licensing of extended  stay centers and requires Oregon Health Authority
to adopt rules. Requires authority and Department of  Consumer and Business Services to convene
extended stay center advisory group.
Adjusts inappropriate series references.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to health care facilities; creating new provisions; and amending ORS  192.660, 441.020,
441.025, 441.030, 441.065, 441.077, 442.015, 442.700 and 677.515.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. Section 2 of this  2017 Act is added to and made a part of ORS 441.015 to
441.087.
SECTION 2. (1) The Oregon Health  Authority shall adopt rules for the licensing of ex-
tended stay centers. The rules must ensure that each licensed extended stay center:
(a) Is sponsored by a medical facility that  has demonstrated safe operating procedures
in an outpatient surgery setting and  has been in compliance with rules applicable to the fa-
cility for no less than 24 consecutive months;
(b) Has an emergency transfer agreement with  a local hospital or has emergency proce-
dures in place for transferring patients in need of hospitalization to a local hospital;
(c) Is located in close proximity to the facility where the extended stay patients are  re-
ceiving surgical services;
(d) Conforms to  all requirements applicable to hospitals in this state with regard to op-
erations, environmental sanitation, physical plant maintenance, safety, food service and  pa-
tient care;
(e) Orally and in writing, clearly notifies patients with Medicare coverage that  services
provided in an extended stay center are not covered by Medicare;
(f) Requires certification by a  patient’s physician prior to the patient’s admission to the
extended stay  center that the patient requires services provided in the extended care center;
and
(g) Admits patients for no more than 48 hours from  the time of admission to the ex-
tended stay center except as medically necessary.
(2) The authority shall license no more than 15 extended stay centers.
SECTION 3.
(1) The Oregon Health Authority shall submit a  request to the Centers for
Medicare and Medicaid Services  to waive provisions of the Medicaid Act (Title XIX of the
Social Security Act) that prevent  federal financial participation in the costs of extended stay

SECTION 4.

(a) One member  of the Senate appointed by and serving at the pleasure of the President

of the Speaker of the House of Representatives; and
(c) The following members appointed by the authority in collaboration with the  depart-
ment:
(A) One member representing a hospital or health system;
(B) One member representing the insurance industry;
(C) One member from a statewide organization that represents physicians in this state;
(D) One member  of an organization that advocates on behalf of health care consumers
in this state;
(E) One member representing long term care facilities in this state;
(F) One member from an organization that represents ambulatory surgery centers in this
state; and
(G) One member from  a statewide organization representing orthopedic physicians in this
state.
(2) The advisory group shall meet at least two times  each calendar year for the purpose
of advising the authority  and the department regarding the implementation of section 2 of
this 2017 Act and other matters related to the regulation of extended stay centers.
(3) If there is a vacancy for any cause, the appointing authority shall make  an appoint-
ment to become immediately effective.
(4) Members of the  advisory group who are not members of the Legislative Assembly are
not entitled to compensation, but may be reimbursed for  actual and necessary travel and
other expenses incurred by them in the performance of their official  duties in the manner
and amounts  provided for in ORS 292.495. Claims for expenses incurred in performing func-
tions of the advisory group shall be paid out  of funds appropriated to the authority for pur-
poses of the advisory group.
