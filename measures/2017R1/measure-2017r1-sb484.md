## Full text

79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 484
Sponsored by Senator PROZANSKI (at the request of Partnership for Safety and Justice) (Presession filed.)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Authorizes Department of Corrections to allocate moneys for  purpose of funding facilitated di-
alogue program services necessary to comply with victims’ rights.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to victims’ right to facilitated dialogue.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
For the biennium beginning July 1, 2017, and  each biennium thereafter, the
Department of Corrections may allocate $  from the  Department of Corrections
Account for the purpose  of funding facilitated dialogue program services necessary to comply
with victims’ rights.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
