Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Office of Governor for biennial expenses. 
 Limits biennial expenditures from lottery moneys for Regional Solutions Program. 
 Limits biennial expenditures from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts, but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received by Office of Governor. 
 <b>Appropriates moneys from General Fund to Office of Governor for Oregon Volunteers Commission for Voluntary Action and Service program. 
 Limits biennial expenditures from federal funds for program.</b> 
 Declares emergency, effective July 1, 2017.

## Full text

79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Bill 5521
Printed pursuant to Senate Interim  Rule 213.28 by order of the President of the Senate in conform-
ance with presession filing rules, indicating neither advocacy nor  opposition on the part of the
President (at the request of Oregon Department of Administrative Services)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to the financial administration of the Office of the Governor; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
There is appropriated to the Office of the Governor,  for the biennium be-
ginning July 1, 2017, out of the General Fund, the amount of $13,223,027.
SECTION 2. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Office of the Governor, for the biennium beginning  July 1, 2017, out of the Gen-
eral Fund, the amount of $50,000 for the necessary expenses of  the Governor in connection
with the duties imposed by law and for such other expenses as may be incumbent  upon or
requested by the Governor.
SECTION 3. Notwithstanding any other law limiting expenditures,  the amount of
$3,827,547 is  established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2017, as the maximum limit for
payment of expenses from lottery moneys allocated from the  Administrative Services Eco-
nomic Development Fund to the Office of the Governor for the Regional Solutions Program.
SECTION 4. Notwithstanding any  other law limiting expenditures, the amount of
$3,432,918 is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2017, as the maximum limit  for
payment of  expenses from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts,
but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received by the  Office of the
Governor.
SECTION 5. In addition to and not in lieu of any other appropriation, there is appropri-
ated to the Office of the Governor, for the biennium beginning  July 1, 2017, out of the Gen-
eral Fund, the  amount of $200,000 for the Oregon Volunteers Commission for Voluntary
Action and Service program.
SECTION 6. Notwithstanding any  other law limiting expenditures, the amount of
$6,907,780 is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2017, as the maximum limit  for
payment of expenses from federal funds collected or received by the Office of  the Governor
for the Oregon Volunteers Commission for Voluntary Action and Service program.
SECTION 7. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency  is declared to exist, and this 2017 Act takes effect
July 1, 2017.
