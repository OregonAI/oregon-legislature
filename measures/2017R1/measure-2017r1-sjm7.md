## Full text

79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Enrolled
Senate Joint Memorial 7
Sponsored by Senator ROBLAN, Representative GOMBERG; Representative BOONE
To the President of the United States,  the Senate and the House of Representatives of the United
States of America, in  Congress assembled, and the Commandant of the United States Coast
Guard:
We, your memorialists, the Seventy-ninth Legislative  Assembly of the State of Oregon, in legis-
lative session assembled, respectfully represent as follows:
Whereas the men and women of  the United States Coast Guard are held in the highest esteem
and regard by Oregonians for their courage, dedication and intrepidity in the cause of saving  lives;
and
Whereas in recognition of the needs  of the commercial, charter and recreational users of the
ocean and bays, residents and tourists  alike, who frequent our beautiful but often dangerous Pacific
coast,  the  United States Coast Guard has maintained, since 1987, a search and rescue helicopter
presence at its air facility in Newport, Oregon, based at the Newport Municipal Airport; and
Whereas Congress,  through enactment of the Coast Guard Authorization Act of 2015, both
funded continuation of the air facility in Newport and required that it be kept open; and
Whereas by continuing its funding  of the air facility’s services in the face of proposed budget
cuts, Congress has shown its recognition of these essential services along the central Oregon  coast
as a vital bulwark against grave threats to the safety of Oregon’s mariners and all others  who work
and play on the ocean and its shoreline; and
Whereas the Legislative Assembly,  the Newport Fishermen’s Wives, other state and local
stakeholders and all Oregonians are grateful for the continued  support provided by Congress and
the President of the United States to protect and save lives along the coastal borders of our country;
now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of  the Seventy-ninth Legislative Assembly, respectfully request that the
Congress of the United  States of America authorize and appropriate adequate funding to the United
States Coast Guard to maintain the United States Coast Guard’s  air facility in Newport, Oregon, in
perpetuity; and be it further
Resolved, That a copy of this  memorial be sent to the President of the United States, to the
Senate Majority Leader, to the  Speaker of the House of Representatives, to each member of the
Oregon Congressional Delegation and to the Commandant of the United States Coast Guard.
Enrolled Senate Joint Memorial 7 (SJM 7-INTRO) Page 1
Adopted by Senate April 20, 2017
Lori L. Brocker, Secretary of Senate
Peter Courtney, President of Senate
Adopted by House May 23, 2017
Tina Kotek, Speaker of House
Enrolled Senate Joint Memorial 7 (SJM 7-INTRO) Page 2
