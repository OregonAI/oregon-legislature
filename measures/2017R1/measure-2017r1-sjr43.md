## Full text

79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Joint Resolution 43
Sponsored by Senator THATCHER
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Urges Governor and executive branch state agencies,  when directed by Legislative Assembly to
make unspecified reductions in state agency budgets or when  responding to allotment reductions
directed by Governor, to make reductions to programs and services in  manner that causes least
harm and inconvenience to persons agencies serve.
JOINT RESOLUTION
Whereas the Legislative Assembly has declared by  law that the legislatively adopted or ap-
proved budget for a  state agency constitutes a determination by the Legislative Assembly of the
amount needed by the agency for the biennium to meet the responsibilities imposed on  the agency
through the budget and through statutes governing the agency; and
Whereas the Legislative  Assembly by law has also provided incentives for state agencies to
spend only the moneys that the agencies need  to meet the responsibilities imposed on the agencies
through the budget and through statutes governing the agencies; and
Whereas the Legislative Assembly has directed the Governor, when reducing  allotments to state
agencies, to follow legislative funding priorities as expressed in statutes and the  legislatively
adopted or approved budget; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
That we, the members of the Seventy-ninth Legislative Assembly, urge the Governor and exec-
utive branch state agencies, when directed by the  Legislative Assembly to make unspecified re-
ductions in state agency budgets or when responding  to allotment reductions directed by the
Governor because there is a  projected deficit in the biennium, to make reductions to programs and
services  in  a manner that causes the least harm and inconvenience to the persons the agencies
serve.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 2547
