## Full text

79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 696
Sponsored by Senator KNOPP
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Prohibits State Fish and Wildlife Commission from using license, tag and  permit revenue to fund
activities related to wolf management.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to limiting moneys used by the State Fish and Wildlife Commission  for the purpose of wolf
management funding.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. Section 2 of this 2017 Act is added to and made a part of the wildlife laws.
SECTION 2. The State Fish and Wildlife Commission may not use moneys received from
the sale of licenses, tags and permits under the wildlife laws for the purpose of funding ac-
tivities related to wolf management.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 2181
