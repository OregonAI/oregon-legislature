**Relating to:** Relating to state financial administration; and declaring an emergency.

Requires Legislative Counsel and Legislative Fiscal Officer to review state law governing investment by State Treasurer of moneys of state and of local and tribal governments. 
 <b>Permits Oregon Liquor Control Commission to make expenditures related to marijuana regulation from Oregon Liquor Control Commission Account. Requires commission to repay amounts expended plus amount for interest. 
 Modifies operative date of law related to funding of science, technology, engineering and mathematics education. 
 Modifies repeal of law related to appearance of Department of Human Services at certain court proceedings. 
 Modifies operative and repeal dates of laws relating to certain state aid programs. 
 Transfers housing of Oregon Volunteers Commission for Voluntary Action and Service to Office of Governor from Housing and Community Services Department. 
 Requires Department of Transportation to transfer certain moneys to Oregon Business Development Department for grant to advance manufacturing technologies. 
 Modifies applicability date of law relating to legislator salaries. 
 Permits county to use certain moneys to provide conciliation and mediation services in circuit courts. 
 Provides that certain expenditures from Water Supply Development Account are not subject to application process or public benefit scoring. 
 Transfers moneys from specified funds and accounts to General Fund for general governmental purposes. 
 Directs distribution of revenue from tax on marijuana items.</b> 
 Declares emergency, effective on passage.

SECTION 1. Notwithstanding ORS 173.240:
(1) The Legislative Counsel and  the Legislative Fiscal Officer shall conduct a review of
state law governing the investment by the State Treasurer of  the funds of this state and of

(a) Identify deficiencies in current law; and
(b) Make recommendations to clarify, simplify, consolidate and update  the existing stat-

may consult with the Department of Justice and the State Treasurer  and any other officer
or employee of this state or of any local or tribal government.

manner provided in ORS 192.245, and  may include recommendations for legislation, to the
Joint Committee on Ways and Means, or the Joint Interim  Committee on Ways and Means,
