## Full text

79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Joint Memorial 5
Sponsored by Representative  HOLVEY, Senator BEYER; Representative BARNHART, Senator
JOHNSON
To the President of the  United States and the Senate and the House of Representatives of the
United States of America, in Congress assembled:
We, your memorialists, the Seventy-ninth Legislative  Assembly of the State of Oregon, in legis-
lative session assembled, respectfully represent as follows:
Whereas the United States Department of Energy and its  predecessor agencies’ activities
producing plutonium at the Hanford Nuclear Site at Richland, Washington, created  large amounts
of radioactive and chemical waste; and
Whereas the methods used for  handling radioactive and chemical waste from the Hanford Nu-
clear Site resulted in extensive ground water contamination; and
Whereas some of the contaminated ground water from the Hanford Nuclear Site  enters the
Columbia River, causing localized contamination where plumes enter the river; and
Whereas cleanup of the Hanford Nuclear Site has been underway  since 1989, and will likely
continue for another 40 years or more; and
Whereas although significant progress has been made in reducing the  flow of contaminants in
ground water to the Columbia  River, expansion of treatment systems is still needed, especially on
Hanford’s Central Plateau, to ensure sufficient long-term protection of the river; and
Whereas federal funding for the Hanford Nuclear Site cleanup is not keeping pace  with actions
needed to continue widespread progress with  the overall Hanford Nuclear Site cleanup, including
expansion of the ground water treatment systems; now, therefore,
Be It Resolved by the Legislative Assembly of the State of Oregon:
(1) The Congress of the United States is respectfully urged  to continue to appropriate and ex-
pend funds as necessary  to support the continued cleanup of radioactive and chemical contaminants
at the Hanford Nuclear Site,  with a specific focus on expansion of the existing ground water treat-
ment systems.
(2) A copy of this memorial shall be  sent to the President of the United States, to the Senate
Majority Leader, to the Speaker of the House of Representatives and to each member of the  Oregon
Congressional Delegation.
Enrolled House Joint Memorial 5 (HJM 5-A) Page 1
Adopted by House April 6, 2017
Timothy G. Sekerak, Chief Clerk of House
Tina Kotek, Speaker of House
Adopted by Senate May 16, 2017
Peter Courtney, President of Senate
Enrolled House Joint Memorial 5 (HJM 5-A) Page 2
