## Full text

79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Enrolled
House Bill 5038
Introduced and printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession  filed (at the request of Oregon
Department of Administrative Services)
CHAPTER .................................................
AN ACT
Relating to  the financial administration of the Teacher Standards and Practices Commission; and
declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any  other law limiting expenditures, the amount of
$7,476,138 is established for the biennium beginning July 1, 2017, as the maximum limit  for
payment of  expenses from fees, moneys or other revenues, including Miscellaneous Receipts,
but excluding lottery funds and federal funds, collected or received  by the Teacher Standards
and Practices Commission.
SECTION 2. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist, and  this 2017 Act takes effect
July 1, 2017.
