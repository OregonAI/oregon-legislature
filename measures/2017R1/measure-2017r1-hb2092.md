## Full text

79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
House Bill 2092
Introduced and  printed pursuant to House Rule 12.00. Presession filed (at the request of House Interim Committee
on Rural Communities, Land Use and Water)
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features  of the
measure as introduced.
Specifies that, in service area for which telecommunications carriers  are not receiving universal
service fund moneys, telecommunications carriers are not required to perform certain obligations.
Becomes operative on July 1, 2017.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to a telecommunications carrier’s obligations with respect to retail  telecommunications
services; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
Section 2 of this 2017 Act is added to and made a  part of ORS 759.400 to
759.455.
SECTION 2. In any service area for which a telecommunications carrier is not receiving
moneys allocated by the Public Utility Commission  from the universal service fund estab-
lished under ORS 759.425, the telecommunications carrier is not required to:
(1) Provide a retail telecommunications service on demand;
(2) Extend a retail  telecommunications service to persons or areas that do not have ac-
cess to that retail telecommunications service; or
(3) Rebuild retail telecommunications service infrastructure or modify the provision of
a retail telecommunications service for the purpose of improving retail  telecommunications
service quality.
SECTION 3.
Section 2 of this 2017 Act becomes operative on July 1, 2017.
SECTION 4. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2017 Act takes effect
on its passage.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 1005
