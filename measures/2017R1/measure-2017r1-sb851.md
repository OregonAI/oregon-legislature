## Full text

79th OREGON LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY--2017 Regular Session
Senate Bill 851
Sponsored by COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS
SUMMARY
The following summary  is not prepared by the sponsors of the measure and is not a part of the body thereof subject
to consideration by  the Legislative Assembly. It is an editor’s brief statement of the essential features of the
measure as introduced.
Directs Oregon Military Department to submit report to interim legislative committees, no later
than July 1, 2018, regarding implementation of Oregon Code of Military Justice.
Declares emergency, effective on passage.
A BILL FOR AN ACT
Relating to the Oregon Military Department; and declaring an emergency.
Be It Enacted by the People of the State of Oregon:
SECTION 1.
(1) No later than July 1, 2018, the Oregon Military Department  shall submit
a report to the interim legislative committees on veterans’ affairs and military affairs  re-
garding implementation of the Oregon Code of Military Justice in this state.
(2) The report shall include a summary of implementation barriers and may  include rec-
ommendations for legislative changes.
SECTION 2. This 2017 Act being necessary for the immediate preservation of the public
peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist,  and this 2017 Act takes effect
on its passage.
NOTE: Matter in  boldfaced type in an amended section is new;  matter [ italic and bracketed] is existing law to be omitted.
New sections are in  boldfaced type.
LC 3901
